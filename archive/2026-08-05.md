1. [Latent Reward Registers for Diffusion Preference Alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03929v1)
   - 分类：Diffusion Alignment / RLHF
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接解决扩散模型偏好对齐中的稀疏奖励与信用分配难题，是RLHF与生成式AI的高价值交叉。
   - 痛点：扩散模型在与人偏好对齐时普遍采用稀疏的终端奖励，只有完成整个去噪生成后才能获得反馈，中间每一步的噪声状态得不到有效评估，导致时间信用分配非常困难；现有奖励模型要么依赖完整生成样本，要么直接使用策略梯度优化，需要大量采样rollout，GPU开销巨大且训练不稳定。此外，缺乏能够从中间噪声潜变量推断最终偏好的可学习机制，也就无法在不改变生成器的情况下为采样过程提供引导。这一问题在长序列和高分辨率生成中尤为突出，制约了对齐效率与生成质量。
   - 方法：论文提出Latent Reward Registers机制，在冻结的Diffusion Transformer输入序列中前置插入多个位置无关、可学习的寄存器token，通过后续层的注意力聚合，使这些寄存器能够从任意中间噪声潜变量直接读出终端偏好的预测，形成密集可微分的奖励信号，且不干扰生成器原有状态。训练阶段采用Reward-Gradient On-Policy Distillation（RG-OPD），沿当前策略的真实去噪轨迹计算奖励梯度并蒸馏到生成器，避免传统策略梯度所需的大量在线rollout；推理阶段则采用Reward-Guided Sampling（RGS），用幅度匹配的奖励梯度以无...
   - 结果：实验表明，在高噪声水平（t=0.8）下，寄存器奖励模型的配对准确率显著优于现有潜变量奖励方法；RG-OPD不仅性能超过在线强化学习基线，还将GPU训练时间最高减少33倍，解决了对齐优化的算力瓶颈。推理时RGS在多个基准上超过已有训练自由方法，取得新的SOTA，并在提升与人类偏好一致性的同时改善了FID等感知质量指标，证明了从中间状态进行奖励引导的有效性与实用性。代码和权重已公开，便于复现。

2. [JoyAI-Video-Edit: Real-Time Open-Ended Video Editing with Autoregressive Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03974v1)
   - 分类：Video Generation / Autoregressive Diffusion
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：面向实时视频编辑的扩散模型架构改进，对生成式AI实时应用有重要参考价值。
   - 痛点：实时视频编辑需要在未知时长、无法访问未来帧的流式场景中工作，但现有扩散模型往往以离线批处理方式生成，延迟高且受限于预定义长度；同时，在保持源视频保真度和长时时间一致性方面，自回归生成容易累积漂移。现有流式编辑系统在计算预算有限时难以兼顾质量与速度，亟需能够平衡因果生成、源锚定和长期一致性的架构。
   - 方法：提出16B参数的自回归扩散框架，采用分块自回归适应使模型在因果限制下持续生成；通过Source-Anchored Distribution Matching Distillation (SA-DMD)确保两步生成时源锚定内容的分布匹配，从而保持源保真；Long-Horizon Autoregressive Distillation通过长程蒸馏减少训练与推理不一致以及累积时间漂移，最终实现端到端的实时编辑。该方法支持开放式的编辑时长，无需预设视频长度或未来帧。
   - 结果：在自动和人工评测中，JoyAI-Video-Edit在短/长视频上大幅超越现有流式编辑器，并与强离线系统性能持平；在单个NVIDIA B200 GPU上实现720p约30FPS的实时编辑速度，证明了蒸馏和自回归架构组合在实际应用中的可用性。代码已开源。

3. [UniWorld-Design: From Pixel Generation to Layer-Native Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03971v1)
   - 分类：Image Generation / Layered Design
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：重新定义图像生成为分层视觉组合，与结构化解耦和可编辑生成相关。
   - 痛点：传统图像生成模型以像素为输出单元，生成的图像是平坦的，无法像设计师那样分离前景、背景、物体等语义层，导致后续编辑修改非常困难，不具备agent可控性。针对分层图像的生成与理解，现有方法多基于可见像素分割，移动或删除层时会破坏语义完整性。需要一种以层为原子单位的生成框架，使生成、理解和编辑三者统一。
   - 方法：提出UniWorld-Design框架，包含Text-to-RGBA (T2RGBA)和Image-to-Layer (I2L)两个模型。T2RGBA直接从文本生成带alpha通道的RGBA资源；I2L以最终图像、全局指令和每层提示为输入，联合生成有序、完整的语义RGBA层。指令接口支持顶层分解、递归分解和目标提取，使分层成为指令可寻址的操作，从而实现智能体式编辑。
   - 结果：在Crello基准上，I2L将逐层RGB L1误差降低37%，Alpha Soft IoU相对Qwen-Image-Layered提升34%；T2RGBA在CLIP Score上超过LayerDiffuse和OmniAlpha，展示了生成可编辑语义层的有效性。该工作为生成式AI向设计工具演进提供了新路径。

**主要痛点**
- 扩散模型偏好对齐中的稀疏奖励与信用分配难题
- 实时视频编辑在低延迟下保持时间一致性和源保真性的挑战
- 图像生成缺乏结构化的语义层表示，后续编辑困难
- 合成数据评估指标与下游任务性能脱节，需领域定制
- 边缘设备部署生成式策略受算力限制，量化与蒸馏是方向

**方法趋势**
- 在扩散Transformer中插入寄存器token用于密集奖励预测
- 自回归扩散与分布匹配蒸馏用于实时视频编辑
- 以RGBA层为原子单位的图像生成与理解框架
- 分层扩散生成将物理约束嵌入生成过程
- 流匹配用于动作生成与机器人强化学习

**结果信号**
- RG-OPD减少33倍GPU时间并获得SOTA对齐质量
- 视频编辑实现720p实时30FPS，同步保持源保真
- 分层生成在Crello上RGB误差降低37%，Alpha Soft IoU提升34%
- 病理专用评估指标与下游任务相关性从0.07提升至0.61
- 扩散策略在边缘设备上可通过量化获得9倍加速但需注意收敛性

1. [Test-Time Scaling in Reasoning LLMs: Inference Regimes, Evaluation, and Reproducibility](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04001v1)
   - 分类：Test-Time Scaling / Reasoning LLMs
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：为测试时扩展提供统一框架和评估规范，直接关系高效推理的训练与评估。
   - 痛点：测试时扩展目前涵盖多种异构推理算法（单轨迹扩展、重复采样投票、搜索等），它们在统计结构、计算核算和失败模式上差异显著，但现有研究常将其视为可互换过程或只报告准确率而不说明推理协议，导致结果难以比较。缺乏形式化框架来区分不同机制、规定评估原则和可复现性要求，严重阻碍领域科学化进展。
   - 方法：将测试时扩展形式化为自回归模型隐式前缀树上的预算推断，区分单轨迹顺序缩放、叶级缩放和前缀级缩放三种结构机制。评估上，将对象视为整个推理系统，分离端到端系统性能与候选库诊断，提出评估档案以统一或界定常见指标；同时规定协议匹配的计算和不确定性报告，区分精确重放与分布可复现性，并整理开放权重模型生态。
   - 结果：在通识知识、符号推理和竞赛数学基准上应用了该框架，并发布超过20亿条完整推理轨迹及递增的验证器/token级信号。该工作为测试时扩展提供了统一的理论语言、评估协议和大规模数据资源，使得不同算法可以在同一坐标系下比较和复现。

2. [Cross-Model KV Cache Transfer in LLM Families: A Closed-Form Linear Mapping for Prefill Reuse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03893v1)
   - 分类：Efficient Inference / KV Cache
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接解决生产环境模型切换时的重复预填充开销，是高效推理的实用创新。
   - 痛点：生产环境常在同一模型族不同尺寸模型间切换（如14B→32B），每次切换都迫使接收模型从头预填充整个上下文，在长对话和多轮场景下产生显著延迟和计算浪费。现有方法需要微调或重训练，无法零成本复用已有KV缓存，缺少可避免重复预填充的轻量方案。
   - 方法：发现跨模型KV在匹配的头结构上存在显著线性关系（单源层可解释目标键56%方差）。基于此提出闭式岭回归映射器：先从源层中选出top-k个最预测性层并拼接KV，再剥离键上的RoPE使映射与位置无关，最后在小校准集（500×1024 token）上拟合逐层映射。也评估了MLP非线性变体以修复线性失效的情况。
   - 结果：在六个模型对上，线性映射器在四个对上保留了73-98%的独立预填充准确率，两个对严重退化；MLP可恢复失败对最多+37个百分点。映射器运行速度比重新预填充快2.7-25倍，并在多轮切换下保持稳定，使跨模型KV缓存迁移变得实际可用。

3. [Interpretable Adaptive Sampling for LLM Test-Time Scaling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03961v1)
   - 分类：Test-Time Scaling / Efficient Inference
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：实现可解释的自适应推理计算分配，直接改善测试时扩展的效率和透明度。
   - 痛点：现有LLM测试时扩展普遍使用固定每查询采样预算，对简单和困难问题等量分配计算，浪费资源且不透明。用户无法理解为何某个提示获得特定样本数，固定预算也限制了系统调优。需要可解释的动态预算分配机制来提升效率并支持审计。
   - 方法：提出轻量模糊控制器，将提示复杂度估计和模型置信度等可解释信号映射为每查询采样预算：简单/高置信度分配较少样本，困难/低置信度分配更多。采用公平对齐协议（匹配解码设置+受控答案选择），与best-of-N、计算感知扩展、自置信度基线在QA和数学任务上对比，并报告样本数减少程度。
   - 结果：自适应模糊控制在多个数据集上优于标准基线，同时保持接近选择器匹配的全预算控制准确性，并明显降低平均采样数。实验证明可解释的自适应采样是高效且实用的测试时推理方向，兼顾性能、计算效率与可理解性。

**主要痛点**
- 测试时扩展算法多样，缺乏统一框架和评估协议。
- 模型切换导致重复预填充，推理开销大。
- 固定采样预算不透明且浪费计算。
- 低比特量化在紧凑网络上退化，全参数耦合训练忽视子空间异质性。
- 多模态模型扩展中视觉-语言计算分配僵化，内存延迟开销高。

**方法趋势**
- 提出预算推断形式化、评估档案和可复现性规范。
- 跨模型KV缓存线性映射，闭式岭回归+RoPE剥离。
- 模糊控制器实现可解释自适应采样。
- 识别归一化仿射参数子空间，进行定向优化。
- 并行分支共享骨干实现可扩展计算分配。

**结果信号**
- 统一框架和大型数据集发布。
- KV映射保留大部分准确率，速度提升2.7-25倍。
- 自适应采样在接近全预算准确性下减少样本数。
- NAP恢复严重量化退化，超越饱和QAT。
- ParVL整体性能优于单分支基线。

1. [EvoHIL: Self-Evolving Reward and Flow-Matched Policy Optimization for Robust Human-in-the-Loop Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03872v1)
   - 分类：Human-in-the-Loop RL / Manipulation
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接解决具身智能中接触丰富操作的奖励自适应、动作一致性和视觉域适应问题。
   - 痛点：人类在环强化学习（HIL-RL）部署接触丰富操作时面临三个耦合限制：静态视觉奖励模型在场景变化（如光照）下失效，导致奖励信号不可靠；独立采样动作产生时间不一致的运动，降低任务成功率；视觉策略对外观变化敏感，泛化差。现有方法仅独立缓解单一缺陷，缺乏统一框架，且真实交互样本稀缺，难以在有限交互中同时适应奖励、动作和视觉域。
   - 方法：提出EvoHIL统一框架，分阶段自适应三个组件：自进化奖励（SER）利用人类确认的正例和临时弱负例在线调整成功分类器，适应场景变化；动作流稳定（AFS）通过流匹配生成时间一致的动作块，并将策略更新锚定在执行的动作前缀上，保证运动平滑；保留感知的离线微调重放重光照交互数据，并锚定AFS演员-评论家到先前行为，在不额外交互下适应视觉域变化。整个流程在分阶段人机协作循环中交错进行。
   - 结果：在Franka FR3和SO-101双臂的六个接触丰富操作任务上，在受控光照变化下，相比HIL基线和模仿基线，任务成功率提升，与人类确认标签的一致性提高，运动平滑度改善，完成时间缩短。消融验证了各组件贡献，整体框架在真实机器人上展现了鲁棒性和样本效率，证明其有效解耦了奖励、动作和视觉域的适应问题。

2. [Bimanual Manipulation Within an 8 GB Budget: Zero-Copy Sensing and Quantized ACT on an Entry-Level Jetson](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03938v1)
   - 分类：Embodied Manipulation / Edge Deployment
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：将双臂操作策略部署到8GB边缘设备，提供量化与零拷贝感知的实证方案。
   - 痛点：双臂操作策略通常在数据中心级GPU上训练和评估，边缘嵌入式设备（如Jetson）上的部署成本未被充分研究。实时感知流水线存在主机-设备间冗余拷贝，导致CPU峰值占用高（98%）和延迟波动（117ms）；同时，行为克隆策略如ACT在边缘上是否需要量化、TensorRT INT8量化对ACT内部结构（如Transformer层）的影响未被记录，制约了低成本实时操控。
   - 方法：在Jetson Orin Nano Super（8GB）上构建完整SO-101双臂系统，使用NVMM缓冲的GStreamer流水线实现三相机零拷贝感知，降低CPU占用和延迟；分别以参考预算训练ACT和Diffusion Policy，并将ACT转换为TensorRT FP16/INT8，逐层分析量化影响。通过调整动作分块配置，测试全精度与量化在不同重预测策略下的可行边界，并测量推理延迟与任务成功率。
   - 结果：ACT在20次试验中成功19次，而Diffusion Policy即使加倍步数也未收敛；TensorRT FP16/INT8将平均推理延迟从114.02ms降至17.93ms/12.65ms，成功率保持19/20、18/20、19/20。发现INT8校准量化ResNet18但拒绝所有145个Transformer层，解释INT8体积仅下降0.9%却带来28%延迟增益；且当动作重新预测需要时间集成时，全精度不可行，量化成为必要条件。

3. [Stochastic Multiple Shooting Trajectory Optimization via Sequential Local Policy Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03978v1)
   - 分类：Trajectory Optimization / MBRL
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：改进强化学习中的轨迹优化样本效率，适用于黑箱动力学和实时规划。
   - 痛点：随机单射轨迹优化（如MPPI）在长动作序列上满足终端约束时样本效率低，需要大量迭代收敛，限制实时应用。传统方法通常需要解析梯度或模型信息，对黑箱动力学（如神经网络近似）不友好。此外，短控制序列之间的连接缺乏反馈机制，导致轨迹不连续，难以在复杂多阶段任务中稳定到达终端集合。
   - 方法：提出随机多射轨迹优化方法，将长动作序列分解为短控制序列，每个片段通过局部线性反馈策略连接，提升优化路径连续性。仅从rollout数据合成近似系统Jacobian，避免显式模型梯度，适用于黑箱动力学。在每次迭代中顺序评估局部策略，相当于在隐式前缀树上的多射打靶，在保持随机适应性的同时增强终端集收敛性。
   - 结果：在三个非线性欠驱动任务上验证：经典cartpole swingup（解析动力学）、带学习神经网络动力学的cartpole swingup，以及VTOL四平面高攻角精确失速着陆。相比MPPI等单射方法，达到终端集的样本效率显著提升，收敛精度提高，且不依赖真值梯度，展示了对噪声和模型误差的鲁棒性。

**主要痛点**
- 接触丰富的机器人操作在真实部署中面临奖励模型对场景变化不鲁棒、动作时间不一致和视觉域漂移等耦合问题。
- 轨迹优化方法在长视野和黑箱动力学下样本效率低，终端约束难以满足。
- 边缘硬件上部署具身策略受限于感知延迟、内存带宽和量化精度损失。
- LLM推理强化学习中的信用分配稀疏，失败轨迹未被有效利用。
- 扩散模型对齐面临稀疏奖励的时间信用分配问题。

**方法趋势**
- 自进化奖励与流匹配动作生成统一框架，结合保留感知的离线微调适应视觉域。

- 关键词：agent, GAME theory, foundation models, OWN decision-making, theory for the social, essential for ensuring safety
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03958v1>

**原始摘要**

As autonomous agents powered by foundation models are increasingly integrated into social and economic systems, understanding the principles governing their collective behavior is essential for ensuring safety and cooperation. Classical game theory, the dominant framework for modeling rational interaction, is built upon the assumption of `decoupled agency,' where agents treat their own decision-making as independent of the environment and other actors. Modern AI agents, however, jointly predict their own future actions alongside external observations. Here, we report a striking finding: when interacting in stylized social dilemmas, foundation model agents engaging in optimal planning consistently converge to stable cooperation, directly contradicting classical game-theoretic predictions of mutual defection. To understand this phenomenon, we introduce the `embedded Bayesian agent,' a theoretical model for foundation model agents. By shifting from decoupled to embedded agency, these agents model themselves as part of the universe they inhabit, maintaining epistemic uncertainty about their own decision-making algorithms. We show that by inferring whether others are behaviorally similar, an embedded agent treats its own deliberation during planning as evidence: a decision to cooperate predicts a similar decision by a similar partner. We formalize this mechanism of similarity inference through the `embedded equilibrium,' a novel solution concept replacing the Nash equilibrium to provide a foundational game theory for the social behavior of modern AI agents.

### 4. [ETA: A New Agentic Paradigm for Embodied Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03924v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：agent, open-source implementation. ETA centers, ETA centers the robot, REAL robots. for codex, embodied tasks. WHEN WILL, robots. for codex openeta
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03924v1>

**原始摘要**

When will robots have their ChatGPT moment? Such a breakthrough requires a general-purpose robot that can handle unfamiliar tasks in unfamiliar environments, remain controllable over long interactions, and learn from experience. Today's embodied systems largely follow an end-to-end observation-to-action path. Despite rapid progress, they remain far from this goal: their generalization depends heavily on the coverage of robot training data, while long task execution remains difficult to control and inspect. To realize this goal, we introduce the Embodied Task Agent (ETA), a new paradigm for extending digital agents into the physical world, and release OpenETA as its open-source implementation. ETA centers the robot around a Planner that chooses one Tool call at a time, an Interface that controls execution, and a World that returns the result and a fresh observation. This loop allows the agent to verify outcomes, adapt its plan, and turn successful and failed interactions into reusable experience. OpenETA provides replaceable Planners, composable Tools and Skills, auditable memory, replayable trajectories, and common interfaces for simulation and real robots. For Codex, OpenETA can operate as a lightweight plugin that exposes only observe, mark_point, and move_to.

### 5. [Socially Grounded Agentic AI: Coordinating Plural Perspectives through Social Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03910v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：socially grounded, alignment, among perspectives and context-sensitive, context-sensitive evaluation. for agentic, perspectives and context-sensitive evaluation., HOW values are socially
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03910v1>

**原始摘要**

As AI systems are deployed across increasingly diverse social contexts, alignment can no longer be framed as the optimization of a single, unified set of values. Instead, systems must be able to recognize, represent, and respond to multiple legitimate perspectives. This has led to growing interest in pluralistic alignment, which seeks to move beyond one-size-fits-all models of appropriate behaviour. However, current approaches often lack a clear account of how values are socially organized, contested, and coordinated in practice. In this paper, we argue that social theory provides essential conceptual and design resources for addressing these challenges. Drawing on established traditions in sociology, we show how perspectives can be understood as structured by roles, shaped through interaction, and distributed across fields of power and expertise. We translate these insights into concrete implications for AI system design, including role-based representations, structured coordination among perspectives, and context-sensitive evaluation. For agentic systems, this requires aligning not only final outputs, but also the role activations, deliberative traces, aggregation rules, and feedback loops through which those outputs are produced. Our contribution is to reposition pluralistic alignment as a problem of socially grounded coordination rather than output diversification. We outline a design space for systems that engage multiple perspectives in structured and accountable ways, and we identify directions for future work to implement and empirically evaluate these approaches in real-world settings.

### 6. [BanglaWild: An In-the-Wild Bengali Scene Text Recognition Benchmark for OCR and Vision-Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03884v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：bengali scene TEXT, in-the-wild bengali scene, scene TEXT recognition, conventional OCR, benchmark, vision-language
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03884v1>

**原始摘要**

In-the-wild Bengali scene text recognition is largely unmeasured: existing resources target handwritten documents or constrained sign-board parsing, report only aggregate edit-distance metrics, and evaluate either conventional OCR or VLMs, never both on the same in-the-wild data. To address this gap, we introduce BANGLAWILD, a benchmark of 2,535 Bengali scene text images, each paired with a verbatim gold transcription, two categorical axes, four diagnostic attributes, and an orthographically standard form where the in-image text deviates from canonical spelling. We evaluate fifteen VLMs and three conventional OCR systems under three prompting strategies, fine-tune 6 open-source models with LoRA, and complement edit-distance metrics with an LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation. Our results reveal a persistent gap in which larger models within the same family do not outperform smaller ones. Our fifteen-class error taxonomy shows that visual mis-recognition accounts for ~60% of errors in the strongest systems, while conjunct-related errors contribute under 2%, challenging a long-standing assumption in Bengali OCR research; the same visual dominant profile also holds across architectures, including the one conventional baseline that reads Bengali reliably. Prompt language mainly affects cross-script drift and LoRA reduces catastrophic failures in weak models without lifting the ceiling on already competent ones. Code and data will be publicly released.

### 7. [MultiGlobeQA: A Multilingual and Globally Diverse Benchmark for Geospatial Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03882v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：benchmark, reasoning, large language models, english and additional high-, additional high- and low-resource, high- and low-resource languages.
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03882v1>

**原始摘要**

Geospatial reasoning, i.e., computing distances, containment, and other spatial relations over real-world entities, is central to navigation and logistics, yet large language models (LLMs) struggle with the required geometric and topological computation despite storing considerable geographic knowledge. Existing benchmarks localize these failures only partially: they are synthetic or smallscale, largely monolingual, and offer limited control over geographic coverage. We introduce MultiGlobeQA, a multilingual benchmark of 46,060 question-answer pairs spanning 14 spatial-function families and 15 answer formats, with execution-based ground truth over three knowledge graphs. It covers 201 countries and territories via income- and density-stratified sampling, with parallel questions in English and 16 additional high- and low-resource languages. Across parametric, reasoning, and agentic settings, LLMs collapse on tasks requiring grid indexing and shape computation, while topological relations and directions fare best. Retrieval and tool use yield considerable gains, yet performance plateaus below two thirds even when gold facts are supplied, indicating that computation, not access to knowledge, is the bottleneck. Models also underperform on low-income regions, a gap that gold facts widen rather than close.

### 8. [ContinualSkillBench: Can LLM Agents Truly Evolve Their Capabilities?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03874v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：LLM agents, in-context learning, large language models, systems can effectively evolve, effectively evolve their skills, truly evolve their capabilities
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03874v1>

**原始摘要**

Modern agent frameworks equip large language models with external skill libraries to solve complex tasks. However, it remains unclear whether these systems can effectively evolve their skills and whether the resulting skills improve task-solving capabilities. To bridge this gap, we introduce ContinualSkillBench, a dynamic evaluation framework for in-context continual skill learning. It covers five representative domains, each containing 100 interconnected subtasks ordered by increasing difficulty and opportunities for cross-task skill reuse. Our experiments show that sequential execution generally improves performance, but the gains vary substantially across models and domains. Moreover, in-context learning performs comparably to explicit skill maintenance on average, suggesting that much of the improvement arises from adaptation to prior context and feedback rather than reusable skill abstraction alone. Explicit skills nevertheless provide selective benefits for tasks requiring reusable procedures or precise outputs. We further find that less capable models tend to accumulate larger, more fragmented collections of task-specific skills. These findings show that current in-context skill evolution mechanisms can support continual adaptation, but still struggle to consistently consolidate experience into robust and transferable skills.
