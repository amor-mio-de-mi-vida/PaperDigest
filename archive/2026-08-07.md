1. [GeniWorld: A Generalizable Interactive World Model for Robotic Manipulation via Visual Actions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06332v1)
   - 分类：Robotic World Model / Visual Action
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：通过将数值动作转换为视觉动作表示，该工作把预训练视频生成模型改造为可控的交互式世界模型，显著提升了对未见环境的泛化能力。
   - 痛点：通用机器人策略在复杂未见环境中的鲁棒性有限，而大规模真实世界机器人数据和评估成本高昂；现有动作条件世界模型往往受限于动作可控性差、对分布外（OOD）场景泛化能力弱，难以作为可扩展的策略评估器和数据生成器。因此，如何用世界模型在有限的固定场景数据上训练出能零样本泛化到随机新场景的交互模型，是机器人学习中的关键瓶颈。
   - 方法：GeniWorld建立在预训练视频生成模型之上，使用URDF渲染将数值动作转换为视觉动作表示，使动作在空间上有明确接地；通过显式解耦本体运动学与环境动力学，缓解场景过拟合并促进机器人-环境交互建模。为了实现闭环控制，构建了自回归视频预测模型与高频机器人运动学控制相集成的框架，既能与机器人策略交互，也能供人类遥操作。训练时仅需有限的固定场景数据，推理时可生成多样操作轨迹。
   - 结果：即使仅在有限固定场景数据上训练，GeniWorld在域内任务上取得优越性能，并能对高度随机化、未见环境进行稳健的零样本泛化；作为策略评估器，在环境扰动下依然可靠。更进一步的实验表明，用有限的真实世界演示生成多样化操作轨迹，能够提升下游策略在复杂环境中的性能和鲁棒性。该结果展示了基于视觉动作的世界模型作为低成本策略评估器与数据增强器的巨大潜力。

2. [PRISM: Distribution-Gated Flow Matching for Controllable Unpaired Image Translation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06240v1)
   - 分类：Unpaired Image Translation / Flow Matching
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：该工作将流匹配与逐特征门控结合，实现了无配对图像翻译中内容保持与外观改变的精细控制，对生成式医疗图像分析有直接价值。
   - 痛点：无配对图像翻译需要在没有配对监督的情况下逐图像决定保留什么内容、改变什么外观；现有很多扩散式翻译器使用单一的全局噪声或引导值控制全图，无法在空间上区分要保留的内容与要改变的外观，导致结构失真或内容泄漏。尤其对医学图像等要求严格结构保留的任务，现有方法难以在目标真实感与源结构保持之间取得平衡。
   - 方法：提出PRISM，一个无GAN的流匹配框架，用学习到的逐特征门控替代全局控制：门控的空间先验来自每个源特征到目标特征分布的标准化距离，远离目标分布的特征被释放，与目标一致的特征被保留。该门控同时控制初始化（将真实源潜在与任务匹配的破坏混合）和ODE集成过程中的传输时序；破坏方式与任务匹配，对保留结构的翻译使用内容锚定AdaIN，对改变结构的翻译使用部分锚定。门控可在推理时通过文本或检测器局部覆盖，无需重新训练。
   - 结果：在五个自然图像和生物医学基准（AFHQ猫->狗、CelebA-HQ外观翻译、昼夜重打光、虚拟染色、乳腺冷冻切片->永久切片）上，在统一同分割协议下，PRISM在四个基准上取得最佳Inception FID和KID，第五个上也表现有竞争力；在组织病理学上，细胞核计数比最接近理想值，表明其在目标真实感与结构保持之间取得了有利平衡。

1. [On-Policy Self-Distillation without Any Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06296v1)
   - 分类：Self-Distillation / LLM Reasoning
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：与OPSD、DASH等同属基于自蒸馏的高效训练方向，本文率先实现无监督自蒸馏。
   - 痛点：现有基于策略的自蒸馏（OPSD）方法虽然能提供token级稠密监督，但仍依赖外部监督信号，包括真值答案、环境奖励或更强模型产生的指导，这限制了自蒸馏在无标注或弱标注场景的适用性，也让“自我”蒸馏名不副实。真正无监督的自蒸馏如何仅利用模型自身生成与内部一致性信号实现有效推理能力提升，是当前重要的研究空白。
   - 方法：U-OPSD首先采样多条rollout，在自一致性阈值下通过多数投票构建伪解，取最短伪解作为条件来构建教师分布；然后选择模型自身最长错误补全的前缀作为学生分布，将该教师分布蒸馏到这些前缀上。这样模型只在“自信但错误”的位置获得修正信号，不需要任何外部标签或奖励。整个过程仅依赖模型自身生成与投票一致性，实现了真正的无监督自蒸馏。
   - 结果：在AIME24、AIME25、HMMT25、MATH500、AMC23等多个数学推理基准上，U-OPSD在Qwen3非思考模式下4B/8B尺度相对基座平均提升8.5%和10.7%，并平均超过监督版OPSD 3.2%和2.3%；在思考模式下，4B超越OPSD 0.9%，8B持平，并分别超过GRPO 0.7%和1.1%。这些结果表明无监督自蒸馏可以匹配甚至超越有监督基线。

2. [DASH: Divergence-Adaptive Supervision Horizons for On-Policy Self-Distillation of Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06243v1)
   - 分类：Reasoning Distillation
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：在OPSD基础上改进，利用rollout中时序差异自适应调整token监督权重，与OPSD、U-OPSD直接相关。
   - 痛点：标准OPSD虽提供稠密token级监督，却对所有位置的局部散度一视同仁，未利用生成过程中散度序列的时间结构；相同大小的散度可能对应完全不同的师生差异演化历史，而局部标量无法区分这些上下文，导致监督权重不能自适应于实际差异轨迹，限制了自蒸馏对推理过程的学习效率。因此需要一种根据散度动态调整监督权重的机制。
   - 方法：DASH将每个局部蒸馏信号与序列级均值的差值映射为自适应传播门，再用这些门控制反向多步聚合，从而根据局部散度在生成过程中的动态演化调整token级监督权重。当局部散度高于或低于均值时，门控分别放大或抑制对应位置的梯度传播。该设计完全复用OPSD已有的教师和学生分布，不增加额外前向或后向计算。
   - 结果：在三个数学推理基准、三种模型规模上，DASH在所有基准和尺度上均胜过匹配的vanilla OPSD重跑结果。由于DASH复用OPSD的分布计算，增益不增加额外teacher或student前向开销，说明时序自适应的自蒸馏是高效且有效的能力提升手段，验证了OPSD中时间结构的重要价值。

3. [BaKron: Efficient Quantization with Kronecker-Factored Hessians](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06291v1)
   - 分类：Quantization / Efficient Inference
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：面向模型量化加速，与GPTQ等低比特推理方法相关；算法复杂度降低，适合大规模模型推理。
   - 痛点：GPTQ等自适应舍入仅利用输入激活的一侧信息，而两侧Kronecker因式Hessian近似虽可捕获输出坐标间的相关性，但直接应用GPTQ于向量化权重域计算复杂度极高（O(m^2n^2)），限制了其在现代大模型量化中的实用性。如何在保留丰富曲率信息的同时将复杂度降至与GPTQ相当，是低比特推理的重要挑战。
   - 方法：BaKron基于BoA/YAQA的两侧自适应舍入公式，结合反对角并行和递归分治构造，将权重矩阵量化求解的串行步骤降到O(m+n)，总工作量从O(m^2n^2)降至O(mn(m+n))，从而在保留丰富曲率信息的同时达到与GPTQ相当的立方复杂度。该求解器对基础量化器和Hessian估计器模块化可替换，并支持多种Hessian估计器。
   - 结果：BaKron在理论上将两侧自适应舍入的复杂度降到与GPTQ同阶，并利用更丰富的曲率信息改善量化质量；在维度从d=10到d=40的实验中，其估计器接近oracle误差，而经典条件MLE产生不可逆拟合。实践基准显示其在多种Hessian下有效，为大规模模型量化提供了高效新工具。

**主要痛点**
- 现有自蒸馏/自监督方法仍依赖外部监督信号，限制了无标注场景下的推理能力提升。
- 自蒸馏方法忽略生成过程中的时间结构，token级监督权重无法自适应。
- 模型量化算法复杂度高，未能充分利用双侧Hessian信息，影响大模型推理效率。
- 智能体与推理系统评估缺乏统一的、可验证的优化协议与诊断工具。
- 多模态与视觉工具使用存在因果幻觉，模型看似获得增益实则证据未被有效利用。

**方法趋势**
- 无监督自蒸馏：通过自一致性投票构造伪解并对自信错误前缀进行蒸馏。
- 时序自适应门控：根据局部散度演化动态调整token级监督权重。
- Kronecker因式Hessian量化：利用反对角并行与分治降低求解复杂度。
- 因果审计与可控基准：通过干预检验视觉证据的因果贡献，构建带可执行轨迹的评估集。
- 生成-排序统一架构与蒸馏：用单一模型替代级联推荐/检索系统。

**结果信号**
- U-OPSD在数学推理基准上稳定超过有监督OPSD，证明无监督自蒸馏可行。
- DASH在全部测试的基准与规模上优于vanilla OPSD，不增加额外计算。
- BaKron将双Hessian量化复杂度降至与GPTQ同阶，并提升量化质量。
- HarnessOpt-Bench显示模型在harness优化上可测量且尚未被充分利用。
- 视觉工具审计发现多数rollout中返回的观察并无因果效果，需重新设计策略。

1. [$ω$-0: A Latent Predictive World Action Model for Concurrent Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06375v1)
   - 分类：Humanoid Loco-Manipulation / World Action Model
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：面向人形机器人移动-操作并发任务，提出潜空间预测世界行动模型，直接输出控制器兼容的全身动作，是具身智能与强化学习的重要探索。
   - 痛点：现有的人形机器人策略通常将移动与操作解耦，导致在真实家庭环境中难以执行边走边拿、调整姿态并操作物体等并发任务。近期世界-行动模型或偏手臂中心、或依赖视频重建，未能直接输出控制器兼容的全身动作潜变量，且缺少同时包含多视角观测、全身运动、机器人状态和动作潜变量的真实世界数据集，限制了并发移动-操作学习。此外，动作空间异构和人类先验到机器人可执行动作的迁移也是一大挑战。
   - 方法：提出ω-0，潜预测全身世界-行动模型。不重建未来视频，而是学习紧凑未来观测嵌入作为轻量预测目标，将潜视觉预见与扩散全身动作生成耦合。支持自我中心RGB、外在RGB和深度输入，通过基于控制器的仿真回放，将人类/公开视觉-运动先验转化为机器人可执行的动作潜变量。构建40+小时真实家庭数据集ω-HOME，含同步多视角、SMPL全身运动、机器人状态和动作潜变量。
   - 结果：真实实验覆盖11种家务任务，任务涉及移动中操作、姿态调整与平衡维持。单个ω-0模型即可持续产生平滑并发行为，在成功率与行为协调性上稳定优于代表性模仿学习、VLA、人形机器人专用模型和此前最优世界行动模型。模型无需为每个任务单独训练，一个统一模型即可支撑多技能，并在多视角输入下保持鲁棒，证明潜预测目标与全身扩散生成耦合的有效性。

2. [DyPES-VLA: Learning Shared Dynamics Priors and Embodiment-Specific Control for Cross-Embodiment Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06374v1)
   - 分类：Cross-Embodiment VLA
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：解决跨机器人平台的操作策略泛化问题，是具身智能中多形态机器人学习的关键方向。
   - 痛点：现有跨具身VLA策略训练时，未能有效利用来自不同机器人视觉与交互数据中共享的动力学先验；同时通常需要繁琐的人工预处理将异构动作空间转换成统一格式，限制了策略的泛化与规模化。此外，现有模型对跨机器人共享的时间动作结构建模不足，导致迁移效率低，难以让一个通用策略直接适应不同机械结构并在原生动作空间中输出控制。
   - 方法：提出DyPES-VLA，通过未来预测目标在跨具身数据上训练VLM，学习共享动力学先验，驱动共享查询表征捕捉物体运动、接触和交互引起的场景变化。设计具身特定MoE动作头，共享注意力层提取共同时序结构，具身特定前馈专家处理各自运动学约束与控制语义，直接输出各具身原生动作空间的控制量，无需预先对齐动作格式。
   - 结果：在LIBERO、RoboCasa-GR1和RoboTwin 2.0仿真与真实评测中均达最先进性能，成功率分别为98.0%、59.25%和89.02%，超过此前跨具身与单具身VLA方法，验证了共享动力学先验与具身特定控制解耦的有效性。

3. [VIDP: Variable Impedance Diffusion Policy for Compliant Robot Manipulation from Diverse Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06210v1)
   - 分类：Variable Impedance / Diffusion Policy
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：关注接触丰富的机器人操作中柔顺性与可变阻抗学习，直接提升具身操作的安全性与成功率。
   - 痛点：接触丰富的操作任务需要既能精确跟踪又能保持柔顺的变阻抗控制，但静态刚度无法适应变化接触约束。从演示中学习阻抗技能时，力/阻抗是隐藏变量，而轨迹变化可能源于几何适应而非主动柔顺，现有方法难以从无力传感器的运动数据中提取物理一致的阻抗意图。
   - 方法：提出变阻抗扩散策略（VIDP），使用任务参数化的方向感知混合模型（TP-DAMM）从多样化演示中提取物理上一致的轨迹分布，将分布映射为刚度曲线，并利用扩散策略联合预测位姿动作与任务柔顺性，无需力传感器。
   - 结果：在真实机器人实验上，VIDP在任务成功率上显著优于固定阻抗基线，相比高刚度控制器降低接触力，相比低刚度基线降低跟踪误差，验证了可变阻抗对接触操作的有效性。

**主要痛点**
- 人形机器人并发移动-操作难以协调，现有方法解耦移动与操作，缺乏真实场景全身动作数据。
- 跨具身VLA需要共享动力学先验和原生动作空间输出，手工对齐动作格式严重阻碍规模化。
- 接触丰富的操作中，静态阻抗无法适应变化接触条件，从演示中难以提取隐含的柔顺意图。
- LLM强化学习中奖励稀疏，生成式奖励模型与标量奖励不匹配，on-policy自蒸馏未能充分利用时间结构。

**方法趋势**
- 潜空间世界-行动模型，以未来观测嵌入为预测目标，耦合扩散动作生成。
- 未来预测学习共享动力学先验，MoE动作头解耦共享与具身特定控制。
- 任务参数化混合模型映射轨迹分布到刚度，扩散策略联合预测动作与阻抗。
- 排名奖励构建、无监督自蒸馏、散度自适应监督视界等提升RL信号。

**结果信号**
- ω-0在11项家庭任务上产生平滑移动-操作行为，优于VLA和WAM基线。
- DyPES-VLA在LIBERO、RoboCasa和RoboTwin上取得最先进成功率。
- VIDP在真实机器人上提升成功率并降低交互力。
- DASH/U-OPSD/RRC在数学推理和对话基准上带来稳定提升。

   - 关联：直接解决LLM agent长轨迹错误定位与调试的痛点，提供可操作的诊断反馈，对agent工程实践有很高参考价值。
   - 痛点：LLM智能体在长时程任务中经常出现级联错误，一个早期的小错误可能被后续步骤放大并最终导致任务彻底失败。现有失败分析工具很难在几百步长的轨迹中定位最早的关键错误步骤，因为判断某一步是否出错所需证据往往分散在相隔很远的指令、观测和先前上下文中，需要跨上下文关联；同时，失败的轨迹通常包含多个局部错误，但其中只有一部分会沿传播链对最终结果产生因果影响，现有方法无法区分这些错误的真实责任。这种对错误“生命周期”缺乏系统建模的现状，使得关键错误识别和可操作的调试反馈都非常困难。
   - 方法：TrajDebug提出错误生命周期追踪框架。首先，通过多粒度历史压缩在长上下文中保留并索引关键信息，使后续证据定位不受长度限制；然后，基于压缩后的证据对每个步骤进行错误识别，判断该步骤是否出错；最后，通过追踪每个错误的解决状态（是否被后续修复）和终端影响（是否最终导致失败），实现关键错误归因——即找出对最终失败负有因果责任的“种子”错误。整个流程支持模块化诊断输出，可以直接生成可反馈给下游agent的具体修改建议。
   - 结果：作者构建了TrajErrBench基准，包含486条手动标注的失败轨迹，覆盖Tau2Bench和SWE-Bench Pro中的真实工具使用与编码场景。在多个agent基准上，TrajDebug在关键错误检测和定位任务上取得了超过现有基线的最佳整体性能，能够准确区分因果关键错误与无关局部错误。应用研究进一步表明，使用TrajDebug生成诊断反馈对下游agent进行改进后，其后续任务成功率有可测量的提升；代码与数据将开源，便于社区复现和扩展。

2. [The Illusion of Visual Tool-Use: A Causal Audit of Thinking with Images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06270v1)
   - 分类：Multimodal Agents / Causal Analysis
   - 研究价值：值得精读

### 1. [Learning When to Trust via Selective Context Preference Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06377v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：preference optimization, selective trust, benchmark, reasoning, WHEN the added context, accuracy WHEN the added
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06377v1>

**原始摘要**

Language models increasingly condition their answers on external signals, and a single misleading one can turn a correct answer wrong. The obvious remedy, training models to resist such signals, hides a failure mode: a model that ignores all context looks robust yet is useless when the context is worth trusting. We recast the problem as selective trust and introduce MIST, a human-annotated benchmark that renders each reasoning item under four matched conditions (clean, misleading, correct-context, and irrelevant-context), together with SC2W, a paired metric counting how often a misleading signal flips a clean-correct answer to wrong. Across a comprehensive benchmark study, we observe that such a susceptibility is universal. We then propose SCOPE, which mines clean-correct/misleading-wrong failures and optimizes a standard Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) objective over matched preference pairs balanced equally across all four conditions, rather than over misleading items alone. Our approach substantially reduces SC2W on popular open-sourced models while preserving accuracy when the added context is clean, correct, or irrelevant. With this work, we argue that models should be judged on selective trust, not on resistance alone.

### 2. [DyPES-VLA: Learning Shared Dynamics Priors and Embodiment-Specific Control for Cross-Embodiment Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06374v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：dynamics priors, shared dynamics, priors and embodiment-specific, vision-language, MoE, attention
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06374v1>

**原始摘要**

Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models have become a powerful paradigm for robot manipulation, but training a single generalist policy for heterogeneous robot embodiments remains an open problem. Existing methods have two main limitations. First, they underuse dynamics priors shared across diverse visual and interaction data, limiting cross-embodiment transfer. Second, they require extensive manual preprocessing to convert embodiment-specific actions into a common format. To overcome these limitations, we propose DyPES-VLA, a cross-embodiment VLA that learns shared Dynamics Priors and Embodiment-Specific control. First, we learn shared dynamics priors by training the vision-language model (VLM) with a future-prediction objective on cross-embodiment data, driving the shared query representation to capture object motion, contact, and interaction-induced scene changes. Second, an embodiment-specific Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) action head translates these shared dynamics priors into executable controls directly in each embodiment's native action space, without manually pre-aligning heterogeneous actions into a common format. This head shares attention layers to capture common temporal action structures, while its embodiment-specific feed-forward experts resolve the unique kinematic constraints and control semantics of distinct embodiments. As a generalist policy, our \ourmethod achieves state-of-the-art performance across simulation and real-world evaluations, reaching 98.0% success on LIBERO, 59.25% on RoboCasa-GR1, and 89.02% on RoboTwin~2.0.

### 3. [The Bitter Lesson of Tool Calling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06370v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：TOOL calling, programmatic TOOL, JSON TOOL, agent, benchmark, TOOL use transforms LLMS
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06370v1>

**原始摘要**

Tool use transforms LLMs into agents that act beyond their training data, and for code-capable models, programmatic tool calling extends this further by replacing rigid JSON calls with scripts that chain and parallelize naturally. However, a systematic evaluation of tools as code on an established benchmark across current and prior model generations under real-world task conditions has not been conducted. In this work, we empirically compare programmatic tool calling (PTC) to native JSON tool calling across 14 language models on BFCL v4. In the programmatic tool calling paradigm, tools are exposed as typed Python stubs that the model invokes through code, with execution and results handled in a single agent turn. Programmatic tool calling matches or exceeds native JSON tool calling in 11 of 14 models on BFCL v4, with the GPT-5.6 family achieving a 10.6% improvement over the JSON tool calling baseline. Further, it matches or outperforms baseline in 13 of 14 models under parallel fan-out, and holds stable under context rot conditions where baseline degrades 2.3% on average. Our results demonstrate that programmatic tool calling is a viable and robust alternative to JSON tool calling, with performance tracking model capability across release generations.

### 4. [AV-AIVAT: 74x Cheaper Agent Evaluation with Certified Anytime-Valid Stopping in Imperfect-Information Games](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06362v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：agent, aivat av-aivat whose online, av-aivat whose online value, CSS into anytime-valid aivat, ratio. av-aivat turns variance, sequences CSS into anytime-valid
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06362v1>

**原始摘要**

Deciding which of two agents is stronger means playing games until skill outweighs luck, and every game costs money, model inference, or expert time. Since the number of games needed is unknown, fixed-budget evaluations either keep paying after the result is settled or stop before the agents can be told apart, while naive optional stopping with an ordinary confidence interval invalidates the stated level. We make such an evaluation stop as soon as its evidence suffices, with the guarantee intact. The Action-Informed Value Assessment Tool (AIVAT) reduces variance in imperfect-information games through conditional mean-zero corrections, by a median $54\times$ across 15 LLM agent configurations spanning 71,439 paired Heads-Up No-Limit Hold'em (HUNL) hands, but does not say when to stop. We combine AIVAT with continuously monitored Confidence Sequences (CSs) into anytime-valid AIVAT (AV-AIVAT), whose online value model learns only from past games so that no game scores its own correction. At the nominal 95\% level and a target precision of $\pm1$ Big Blind, raw outcomes need a median $74\times$ as many hands as AIVAT-corrected outcomes to stop under the Asymptotic CS (AsympCS). Exact finite-sample certification uses the Empirical-Bernstein CS (EB-CS), which needs an independently justified bound on corrected payoffs. We establish such a bound structurally for Leduc hold'em and characterize a width floor set by the CS's bet cap and that bound, which governs how much of a variance gain becomes earlier stopping; the descriptive HUNL EB-CS runs show a median $1.37\times$ stopping-time ratio. AV-AIVAT turns variance reduction into efficient, auditable early stopping while separating asymptotic screening from exact certification, so an evaluation can stop the moment its evidence suffices and hand a third party everything needed to recheck the verdict at that very stopping time.

### 5. [The Low Frequency Trap: Video Language Models Fail at Simple Event Bookkeeping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06361v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：real-world video, event count, reasoning, video tasks bouncing-ball WALL, event trace for capability-surface, controlled video tasks bouncing-ball
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06361v1>

**原始摘要**

Real-world video benchmarks provide broad coverage, but their fixed clips entangle event count, rate, duration, and visual complexity, making failure modes hard to isolate. While existing programmatic benchmarks offer better control, they score only the final answer rather than auditing reported events against executable ground truth. To bridge this gap, we introduce trace-grounded parametric profiling for event counting in three controlled video tasks: bouncing-ball wall contacts, visual blinks, and categorical state transitions. Across 2,190 videos, we vary event count N and frequency F while holding rendering fixed. Each video includes an executable event trace for capability-surface estimation and timestamp-level evaluation. Our results reveal a staged temporal failure. At an 80% reliability threshold, Gemini 3.6 Flash reliably counts persistent state transitions up to 12 events at 0.5 and 1.0 Hz, yet demonstrates no reliable positive-count region for transient blinking events. Thus, event representation dictates whether a model initially accesses evidence -- a limitation that compounds as count and frequency increase. In the high-count, high-frequency regime, only 0.2% of final counts are correct and the model recovers just 18.1% of true events. To test if visual access is the primary bottleneck, we increase sampling rate. Although this boosts Bounce Ball accuracy from 19.6% to 29.3%, the reported sequence agrees with ground truth only 3.7% of the time. Extra frames can therefore inflate final scores without producing faithful event recovery. Different prompting strategies yield similarly limited gains, and real-world video evaluations show the same concentration of success at low event counts. Ultimately, trace-grounded profiling shifts video evaluation from aggregate accuracy metrics to a detailed diagnostic of where temporal reasoning fails.

**原始摘要**

Training terminal agents requires executable and verifiable tasks that are not merely solvable, but appropriately challenging for learning. ### 7. [RP-OPSD: Reasoning-Pivot-Guided On-Policy Self-Distillation for Multilingual Reasoning Transfer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06347v1)

**原始摘要**

Multilingual reasoning transfer is crucial for extending reasoning capabilities of large language models (LLMs) beyond high-resource languages. On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) and its variants have emerged as a promising paradigm, providing dense token-level supervision on student-generated rollouts, yet their objectives do not explicitly prioritize reasoning signals most critical to cross-lingual transfer. We characterize that target-language reasoning comprises the generation of both surface text and reasoning pivots, which are decisions that advance or redirect the reasoning process and shape subsequent inference. ### 9. [Scalable estimation of VARMA models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06340v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：autoregressive, brings likelihood-based varma estimation, varma models have LONG, estimation framework that removes, moving-average varma, varma models. vector
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06340v1>

**原始摘要**

Vector autoregressive moving-average (VARMA) models have long been considered impractical beyond moderate dimensions: the likelihood is non-convex, the parametrization is identified only up to equivalence, and every evaluation costs a pass over the entire series. Yet their moving-average term captures with a few parameters what a pure autoregression matches only with many lags. We introduce an estimation framework that removes this computational barrier: each optimization iteration is independent of the series length $T$. The framework combines a partial-autocorrelation reparametrization that guarantees stationarity and invertibility by construction, Gaussian priors on the reparametrized coefficients with separate scales for diagonal and off-diagonal entries, and losses that depend on the data only through fixed-size sufficient statistics, evaluated by a Parseval (Fourier) identity at near-linear cost in the truncation length. This yields two point estimators: a regularized least-squares fit and a covariance-marginalized maximum-a-posteriori estimator. We prove that both recover the infinite-autoregressive representation of the true process at a near-parametric rate in fixed dimension, so the truncation introduces no asymptotic bias. The same machinery extends, at the same leading cost, to seasonal dynamics, exogenous regressors (VARMAX), and rolling-window refits. Empirically, the estimators stay close to the oracle forecast error from $d=10$ to $d=40$ (where classical conditional MLE returns non-invertible fits whose forecasts diverge) and match or beat VAR, Bayesian-VAR, component-wise ARMA, and sparse-VARMA baselines on retail-demand, meteorological, and air-quality data. This brings likelihood-based VARMA estimation, at a per-iteration cost independent of the series length, to the problem sizes where practitioners have so far relied on VAR models.

### 18. [Bias Analysis of L2 Speaking Assessment Systems Using Concept Activation Vectors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06300v1)

- 关键词：speaking assessment, assessment systems, concept activation, concept recoverability, multimodal, transformer
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06300v1>

**原始摘要**
