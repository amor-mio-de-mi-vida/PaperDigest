1. [XYZFlow:Scaling Multi dimensional Shortcut Flows for Efficient Generative Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12276v1)
   - 分类：Efficient Flow Matching / Generative Modeling
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：与扩散/流模型的少步采样和高效生成直接相关
   - 痛点：高保真图像生成长期面临速度与质量的根本性权衡：扩散模型虽能生成强视觉质量，但需要数十至上百次迭代采样，计算开销巨大。现有高效方案主要依赖将预训练教师模型蒸馏为少步采样器，但蒸馏过程复杂、成本高，且生成质量紧密绑定教师模型的上限；同时，单纯学习单步映射的表达能力有限，概率路径在可辨识性与可学习性上存在严重问题，导致少步采样时轨迹出现歧义与伪影。这些问题限制了少步生成方法的适用性和可扩展性。
   - 方法：提出XYZFlow，通过多维缩放shortcut flows来重新思考高效生成。具体包含两个正交维度：时间维度采用非马尔可夫条件，使去噪网络利用完整的去噪历史而非仅当前时间步，从而增强概率路径的可辨识性；空间维度提出Next Shortcut Prediction，将图像按序列生成，每个补丁以前序补丁的去噪轨迹作为先验，等效于将自回归建模融入flow straightening，降低轨迹歧义。该框架从训练数据中直接学习多尺度条件，无需依赖教师模型蒸馏，并可通过顺序生成与历史条件实现更好的质量-延迟权衡。
   - 结果：实验表明XYZFlow在图像生成基准上取得SOTA性能，实现7.2-8.5倍教师模型加速的同时保持有竞争力的FID值。多维度消融显示，Next Shortcut Prediction在质量-延迟权衡上显著优于单纯增加模型规模或减少采样步数，并且该框架具有良好可扩展性，能够适应不同模型尺度和生成分辨率。进一步分析表明，多维条件通过降低轨迹重叠来提升采样稳定性，验证了多维缩放范式的有效性。

2. [StateFlow: Building, Evolving, and Accessing 3D World States for Previsualization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12314v1)
   - 分类：3D World Generation / Previsualization
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：与可编辑生成、3D场景表示和视频生成相关
   - 痛点：电影、游戏等预可视化流程需要先于生产对场景、动作、相机和时空动态进行迭代设计，但现有生成方法通常依赖简单文本或图像提示，通过一次性图像/视频合成联合控制所有因素，缺乏对场景元素的精确操控与局部修改能力，也无法在多次编辑中保持世界一致性。根本缺陷在于缺少显式、持久的3D世界工作状态，导致用户只能在像素级输出上反复试错，无法享受结构化状态带来的复用与重组优势。
   - 方法：StateFlow采用状态中心（state-centric）框架，将可编辑3D世界作为预可视化的核心工作表示。该方法包含三阶段：状态构建（State Construction）通过先验引导、冲突感知的双视图初始化，将从2D生成模型得到的内容提升为具有几何与外观一致性的3D场景；状态演化（State Evolution）将用户意图解析为结构化状态转换，在保留世界记忆的前提下局部更新物体属性和空间关系，避免全场景重生成；状态访问（State Access）利用渲染反馈反思机制，将粗糙的相机计划细化为视觉上可行的轨迹，弥补纯VLM语义推理对几何可行性的忽视。
   - 结果：在视频创作和游戏原型等任务上的实验显示，StateFlow生成的3D世界具有高质量几何结构、外观一致性和可编辑性。相比一次性生成基线，StateFlow支持对场景元素的迭代修改与状态复用，显著提升预可视化流程的灵活性和可控性；在状态构建、演化和访问各级模块的消融实验也验证了各阶段设计的必要性，最终输出可直接用于驱动视频生成或交互原型。

3. [Generation as Auxiliary Supervision: Enhancing Visual Understanding at Zero Inference Overhead via Decoupled Embedding Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12209v1)
   - 分类：Multimodal Representation Learning / MLLM
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：与多模态大模型训练、生成-理解协同相关
   - 痛点：多模态大语言模型（MLLM）中，视觉理解与视觉生成往往被视为相互冲突或割裂的目标。现有统一模型要么采用离散视觉token，要么使用扩散目标，而这些生成表示与理解模型所依赖的连续视觉表示不一致，导致生成损失难以直接用于增强已有预训练MLLM；并且通用图像合成任务与理解任务的相关性弱，强行联合训练反而可能干扰高层语义推理。因此如何在不损害理解性能的前提下从生成中获益仍然缺乏实用方案。
   - 方法：本文提出GAS（Generation as Auxiliary Supervision）框架，将视觉生成重新解释为表示学习的辅助监督信号。在解耦的Mixture-of-Transformers（MoT）架构中，共享低层trunk并由高层的并行分支分别处理理解与生成任务；通过Next Embedding Prediction（NEP）作为跨模态生成范式，让生成损失以较高梯度权重作用于共享视觉通路，从而强化空间精度与视觉保留，同时屏蔽对理解分支的干扰。此外构造需要深层认知推理的高度关联生成任务，促进模型理解和推理能力。
   - 结果：在多个模型规模和训练阶段下，GAS一致地提升多模态理解聚合指标，尤其在感知与空间理解上增益稳定且显著。受控对比实验与表示层分析表明，生成辅助训练的收益源于共享视觉通路的信息密化，而非简单数据增强；由于推理时完全丢弃辅助生成分支，因此获得这些改进的推理开销为零。该方法为生成-理解协同提供了一条实用且可扩展的路线。

**主要痛点**

- 少步采样依赖蒸馏且概率路径可辨识性不足，效率与质量难平衡
- 多模态理解与生成目标割裂，视频黑盒随机性强需人工试错

**方法趋势**

   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：面向边缘部署的低内存TTA，直接支撑高效推理场景。

   - 方法：CAZO提出曲率感知零阶优化，通过滑动窗口估计损失函数对角Hessian，构造各向异性高斯协方差作为扰动采样分布，从而让零阶梯度估计贴合损失曲率、降低方差。训练时冻结预训练权重，只优化极少量adapter参数，全程只用前向传播；Hessian估计同样基于前向差分，不引入额外反向传播。
   - 结果：在多个标准TTA基准（如ImageNet-C等）上，CAZO显著超越现有ZO-TTA方法并达到SOTA，同时保持与一阶TTA相当的内存节省。相比一阶BP方法，CAZO大幅减少显存占用；相比普通ZO，精度提升显著。代码已开源，为设备端低内存TTA提供了实用方案。

2. [One Frozen Simulator Is Not Enough: Simulator Collapse in Multi-Agent RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12253v1)
   - 分类：Multi-Agent RL
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：与团队在多智能体RL和LLM模拟器方面的研究直接相关，揭示了单一LLM模拟器的模式坍塌问题。
   - 痛点：多人智能体强化学习在与人类交互时，通常使用单个大型语言模型（LLM）作为用户仿真器来生成训练数据。然而，LLM 在采样时存在模式坍塌（mode collapse），导致仿真器只生成少数几种典型行为，而真实用户的行为分布要宽得多。策略模型在这种窄分布上训练会过拟合仿真器的主导模式，一旦在真实用户或其他仿真器上评估，成功率大幅下降。现有研究往往忽略仿真器本身的质量和多样性，而是将注意力集中在策略优化上；这使得‘仿真器坍塌’问题成为多轮 RL 从训练到部署的关键瓶颈，但缺乏系统性认识和有效对策。
   - 方法：提出并形式化‘仿真器坍塌’问题，并给出两种互补解决路径。推理时方案 Verbalized Sampling：在仿真器输出时，先让 LLM 以口语化方式给出多个可能的响应候选（不实际生成最终文本），再从中采样，从而扩大响应分布、降低模式坍塌；该方法不改变仿真器参数。训练时方案 Co-Training：维护一个由多个可训练仿真器组成的种群，并使策略同时与所有仿真器交互优化，通过 population-based training 鼓励策略不被任何单一模式捕获，从而提升泛化性。同时开源 SCOPE 框架，支持种群协同训练多智能体强化学习。
   - 结果：在三个多轮对话基准（Persuasion for Good、τ²-bench、CooperBench）上验证了有效性：相比单仿真器 RL，Verbalized Sampling 在保留集上成功率最高提升 9%，Co-Training 进一步将增益提升到 14%。在真人评估中，两种方案也带来相似幅度的提升。此外，训练出的策略多样性明显高于单仿真器基线，证明策略没有过拟合到单一模式。理论分析解释了坍塌的来源和解耦方案的必要性。研究结果表明，训练环境的多样性与策略本身同等重要。

   - 关联：与团队在具身智能和视觉导航上的研究高度相关，提供了空中VLN的扩散规划新范式。
   - 痛点：空中视觉语言导航要求智能体在部分可观测环境中整合时序视觉证据、规划未来动作序列，并判断何时到达目标。现有视觉-语言-动作模型直接端到端预测，但面临三个关键缺陷：一是历史上下文窗口有限，无法建模长距离因果依赖；二是动作规划采用单步或短视策略，难以考虑未来多步约束；三是终止判断隐式地耦合在动作生成中，导致过早或过晚停止。这些问题在空中场景（视野变化大、目标远距离）中尤为突出，使得传统 VLA 方法导航误差高，难以实际部署。
   - 方法：提出 DreamFly，基于 Dream-VLA 扩散模型的框架。包含三个创新设计：(1) 因果对齐历史记忆——仅使用当前决策步骤之前的观测来增强当前视觉表示，通过因果掩码避免未来信息泄漏，从而在扩散推理中保留时序一致性；(2) 再ceding-horizon扩散规划——策略一次预测 K 步动作块，但只实际执行第一步，然后重新规划；这样用未来动作作为辅助监督目标，同时保持闭环视觉反馈；(3) LiteStop 终止预测器——在扩散初始掩码状态下，直接从动作 logits 计算出停止概率，将显式终止从动作生成中解耦出来。三者联合训练。
   - 结果：在 OpenFly 空中导航基准上，DreamFly 在 test-seen 和 test-unseen 分割上分别达到成功率和路径加权成功率：seen 32.04%/28.22%，unseen 29.46%/23.54%，显著优于所有基线（包括 VLA 和传统 VLN 方法），并取得最低导航误差。消融实验证实三个组件各有贡献，其中再ceding-horizon规划对长距离导航提升最明显，LiteStop 有效降低了过早停止率。结果表明，通过历史、未来和终止的联合建模，扩散规划能够显著提升空中 VLN 的可靠性和泛化能力。

### 1. [StateFlow: Building, Evolving, and Accessing 3D World States for Previsualization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12314v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：video creation and game-like, world memory avoiding full-scene, creation and game-like prototyping., actions cameras and spatial-temporal, cameras and spatial-temporal dynamics., state-centric framework for generative
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12314v1>

**原始摘要**

Previsualization is an intermediate layer between ideas and production in film, games, architecture, and urban design. It lets creators iteratively refine scenes, actions, cameras, and spatial-temporal dynamics. Yet existing generative methods rely on simple prompts to jointly control all of these factors through one-shot image or video synthesis, offering weak controllability and limited support for iterative editing. Fundamentally, a world comprises multiple elements with geometry, appearance, and other attributes, together with cameras. Different frames are produced through local modifications or recombinations of this shared state, which is otherwise largely reused. Therefore, we argue that the missing component is an explicit and persistent working state. To address this, we present StateFlow, a state-centric framework for generative previsualization. Rather than generating videos in one shot, StateFlow uses an editable 3D world to organize scene structure, evolution, and cameras, while off-the-shelf video models enhance visual quality when higher fidelity is desired. This world is maintained as a persistent structured 3D state of scene elements and camera configurations, serving as the core working representation for previsualization. Built on this insight, StateFlow has three stages to construct, evolve, and access the world state. State construction lifts generated 2D content into a coherent 3D world through prior-guided, conflict-aware dual-view initialization, while State evolution translates user intent into structured state transitions while preserving world memory, avoiding full-scene regeneration for each edit. State access uses render-feedback reflection to refine camera plans into visually feasible trajectories, avoiding reliance on VLM semantics alone. Experiments show that StateFlow produces high-quality 3D worlds for video creation and game-like prototyping.

### 2. [DreamFly: Causal Memory and Receding-Horizon Diffusion Planning for Aerial Vision-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12308v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：receding-horizon diffusion planning, aerial vision-language, agent, benchmark, reasoning, causal memory and receding-horizon
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12308v1>

**原始摘要**

Aerial vision-language navigation (VLN) requires an embodied agent to integrate visual evidence over time, plan future actions, and determine when it has reached a navigation goal under partial observability. Although recent VLA models offer a promising perception-to-action paradigm, adapting them to aerial navigation remains challenging due to limited historical context, short planning horizons, and unreliable implicit termination. To address these challenges, we propose DreamFly, a diffusion-based aerial VLN framework built on Dream-VLA. DreamFly introduces a causally aligned historical memory that augments the current visual representation using only observations preceding the current decision step, enabling temporal reasoning without future information leakage. We further formulate navigation as receding-horizon diffusion planning, where the policy predicts a $K$-step action chunk but executes only the first action before replanning. This plan-$K$, execute-one strategy uses future actions as auxiliary planning targets while preserving closed-loop visual feedback. Experiments on the OpenFly benchmark demonstrate consistent improvements in seen and unseen environments. DreamFly achieves 32.04%/29.46% SR and 28.22%/23.54% SPL on the test-seen/test-unseen splits, respectively, outperforming all compared methods on both metrics while attaining the lowest navigation error. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of jointly modeling historical context, future action structure, and explicit termination for aerial VLN.

### 3. [Beyond Trial-and-Error: Agentic Optimization for Image-to-Video Adherence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12290v1)

- 来源：arXiv

This work provides a practical and extensible methodology for enhancing the predictability and control of state-of-the-art video generation models, moving the field beyond speculative curiosities toward reliable, production-ready tools.

### 6. [One Frozen Simulator Is Not Enough: Simulator Collapse in Multi-Agent RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12253v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：multi-agent rl., reinforcement learning, simulator collapse, rl. multi-agent reinforcement, population co-training multi-agent, NOT ONLY the policy
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12253v1>

**原始摘要**

Multi-agent reinforcement learning for human-AI interaction typically relies on a single large language model to simulate user behavior. We show that this approach systematically fails to generalize, and trace the failure to simulator collapse: because the simulator LLM is mode-collapsed, an LLM policy trained against it overfits to narrow strategies that exploit the simulator's dominant mode, and such a policy transfers poorly to unseen simulators and real users. We formalize this collapse theoretically and propose two complementary solutions, one at inference time and one at training time. The inference-time solution, Verbalized Sampling, broadens the simulator's behavior by sampling from a verbalized response distribution, reducing mode collapse. The training-time solution, Co-Training, jointly optimizes the policy against a population of trainable simulators, preventing it from overfitting to any single simulator's mode. Verbalized Sampling improves held-out success by up to 9% over single-simulator RL, and Co-Training pushes gains further to 14%; the human study shows similar gain on real users. Both solutions preserve the policy diversity that collapses under single-simulator RL. To support further work in this direction, we release SCOPE, an open-source framework for Population Co-Training multi-agent RL. More broadly, our results suggest that the diversity of the training environment, not only the policy, is critical to the generalization of multi-turn RL to real-world deployment.
