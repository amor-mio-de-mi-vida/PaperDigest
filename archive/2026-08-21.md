### Diffusion / 生成式AI

1. [4DAnyone: Create Anyone in 4D from a Casual Monocular Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20335v1)
   - 分类：4D Human Reconstruction / Video Diffusion
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：与视频扩散和多视角3D重建紧密相关，是本次Top Pick 1。
   - 痛点：从单目视频重建4D人体常依赖视频扩散模型生成多视角一致视频，但现有相机控制扩散模型在目标视图数量增至十数个时，因单次DiT前向容量限制被迫分组建模，产生两个耦合瓶颈：参考上下文随生成视图数量线性增长（O(N)），削弱交叉视图外观引导；目标上下文组间无法直接通信，导致全局结构漂移。这使模型无法生成重建级多视角视频，成为高保真4DGS重建的关键障碍，现有方法在此规模下明显失真。
   - 方法：提出4DAnyone框架，采用双互补设计解决上述瓶颈。Reference Context Packing (RCP) 将不断累积的参考视图压缩为固定长度的混合分辨率上下文，使参考复杂度由O(N)降为O(1)，既保留关键外观信息又避免冗余；Target Context Routing (TCR) 在去噪过程中周期性地轮转目标视图分组，使高噪声步中不同组通过共享上下文保持全局一致，低噪声步隔离细节以稳定外观。为强化训练，构建游戏引擎MVGameHuman数据集，并与光舞台和野外视频数据联合训练，实现鲁棒泛化。
   - 结果：在DNA-Rendering和DyMVHumans两个基准上，4DAnyone生成的新视角视频的PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS等质量指标以及下游4DGS重建精度均超过此前最先进方法，在遮挡、姿态复杂场景下亦保持一致性；在野外视频上展示出可用泛化能力。消融实验验证了RCP与TCR各自对参考上下文压缩和目标跨组信息流通的关键贡献，项目已开源并提供视频结果。

2. [WithEveryone: Unified Planning and Identity Grounding for Group Image Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20336v1)
   - 分类：Group Image Generation / Identity Grounding
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：同属扩散生成领域，聚焦身份保持群组生成，是本次Top Pick 2。
   - 痛点：身份保持图像生成在场景包含多个指定人物时性能急剧下降：模型不仅要逐一保留参考身份，还必须将每个参考绑定到场景中的特定人物和位置，而训练时身份损失需要在多个噪声预测人脸之间建立对应关系，该过程缺乏稳定监督，容易导致身份混淆、漏生成或直接将参考人脸复制粘贴到图像中，难以扩展到十人规模的群组生成。
   - 方法：提出WithEveryone统一框架，将每个选定身份注入为可寻址token，并预测结构化身份-布局计划，再渲染为视觉条件引导生成。核心创新是Layout-Grounded ID Loss：利用标注人脸区域直接监督期望身份，避免脆弱嵌入匹配；ID Representation Forcing在图像合成前先行训练每个身份的预测，强化身份绑定。该机制显式建立身份与空间位置对应，无需直接复制参考人脸即可容纳至多十人。
   - 结果：在身份不相交的群组基准上，WithEveryone实现了最高的目标上下文身份相似度，将人脸相似度从GPT-Image-2的0.462提升到0.499，同时将复制粘贴伪影从0.169降至0.055；请求身份覆盖率达97.3%，重复率仅2.8%。结果表明显式身份-布局锚定能有效扩展到更大群组，且不依赖参考人脸直接复制，具有强泛化性。

3. [Swift-Image: Exploring the Performance Frontier of Compact Unified Image Generation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20334v1)
   - 分类：Unified Image Generation / DiT / Training Engineering
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：统一生成模型训练工程，为小型扩散/DiT模型提供系统性方法论，是本次Top Pick 3。
   - 痛点：在固定算力预算下构建统一的图像生成模型需同时支持文本生成、单图编辑和多图编辑，但不同任务的目标存在显著干扰：高分辨率视觉质量、生成与编辑统一监督、推理效率等互相制约。现有模型为达成多能力依赖更大参数量或大量算力，而小模型虽部署友好却难以达到聚合性能，缺乏系统的训练工程方法论。
   - 方法：提出Swift-Image，采用6B单流DiT架构，设计渐进式训练管线：从广泛语义覆盖预训练逐步推进到高分辨率优化、统一生成-编辑联合监督。后训练阶段采用并行专家RL和多教师on-policy蒸馏，以缓解异构目标间的干扰；额外引入Prompt Enhancer将用户请求翻译为生成器对齐的视觉规约，解耦高层推理与像素渲染。部署侧结合结构化剪枝与少步蒸馏，产出3B和加速变体。
   - 结果：在多项文生图、单图编辑和多图编辑基准的聚合评估中，Swift-Image以仅6B参数量和243K GPU训练小时取得开源模型领先成绩；压缩的3B版本几乎无损，少步蒸馏甚至进一步提升编辑聚合性能并大幅减少采样步。论文还系统总结架构、数据课程、后训练等七方面实践经验，可直接复用于下一代模型训练。

**主要痛点**
- 扩散模型多视角一致性受DiT上下文容量限制，难以支撑重建级输出。
- 多人身份保持生成中身份-位置绑定与多脸对应易导致复制伪影。
- 统一图像生成在算力受限下多任务干扰，缺乏系统训练工程方法。
- 医学报告解读需同时满足事实性与患者沟通，多目标RL难优化。
- 视频扩散模型直接用于几何回归存在计算重、遮挡离屏难处理问题。

**方法趋势**
- 参考上下文打包与目标上下文路由（RCP/TCR）。
- 身份-布局锚定与布局接地损失（Layout-Grounded ID Loss）。
- 渐进式训练、专家RL、多教师蒸馏、提示增强器（Swift-Image）。
- 清单对齐RL与多源检索原子验证（G-CARL）。
- 确定性Clean-Latent编码器+双向时空解码器（DreamHand）。

**结果信号**
- 4DAnyone在新视角质量与4DGS重建上超越SOTA。
- WithEveryone提高身份相似度并大幅减少复制伪影。
- Swift-Image以6B/243K小时取得开源领先聚合性能。
- G-CARL在医学报告解读上优于现有RL基线。
- DreamHand在遮挡/离屏手部恢复上最多降低60%误差。

   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接评估训练算法设计能力，是高效训练与推理的终极目标之一。

   - 方法：构建10个冻结研究仓库覆盖10种训练算法族，代理在4小时B300上重写训练算法，再从头重跑12小时并由隐藏固定评估器对统一0-1标尺计分，支持跨指标比较。
   - 结果：29种配置下平均分0.166，最强系统0.250；多数提交从不改变学习方式，而改变学习方式的提交平均分0.226 vs 0.126；增加推理努力将改变学习方式的提交比例从8%升至64%，平均分从0.094升至0.196，并开放全套评测代码与记录。

3. [Learning When to Think: Adaptive Reasoning for Test-Time Compute Allocation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20256v1)

**主要痛点**

**方法趋势**

1. [RoMAN-Flow: Taming Autoregressive Normalizing Flows for Offline Reinforcement Learning in Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20208v1)
   - 分类：Offline RL / Robotic Manipulation
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接对应离线强化学习在机器人操作中的应用，为解决流式策略的似然评估瓶颈提供可行方案
   - 痛点：离线强化学习利用历史数据训练机器人策略，但当前主流的扩散/流匹配策略不具备可处理的似然函数，无法直接用于基于似然的离线RL后训练。自回归归一化流(AR-NF)虽能同时提供表达性动作建模和精确似然评估，但其自回归顺序采样在策略优化和部署阶段引入巨大计算开销，导致此类策略在机器人操作中难以实用。
   - 方法：RoMAN-Flow提出双重瓶颈解决方案：策略优化阶段采用免采样的优势加权似然目标，直接从离线数据集中为高优势动作分配更高似然，避免从自回归策略采样带来的开销；部署阶段将优化后的自回归策略蒸馏为单步动作生成器，实现低延迟动作预测；两者结合使AR-NF策略在训练与推理中都保持高效。
   - 结果：在多个模拟操作基准和真实机器人平台上的实验表明，RoMAN-Flow能够以更低的推理延迟匹配或超越现有扩散与流匹配策略的性能，同时保留精确似然带来的可解释性，使AR-NF策略首次在机器人操作中达到实用水平，验证了免采样优势加权与一步蒸馏结合的有效性。

2. [Video2DoorTraversal: Push Door Traversal via Simulated Door Twins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20251v1)
   - 分类：Sim-to-Real / Mobile Manipulation
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接面向具身智能中的门操作任务，提出单视频到仿真的高效方案
   - 痛点：门开启与穿越是移动操作机器人面临的长时程定位-操作任务，需要精确的把手交互和底盘-手臂协调控制。现有方法通常依赖大量真实示范或手工三维建模，难以从单个视频快速泛化到新门型；同时铰链结构多样、任务阶段长，直接学习策略容易出现失败累积，制约了真实部署。
   - 方法：提出单视频“真实-仿真-真实”训练框架。首先DoorTwin通过一段RGB视频重建与实例对齐的可动仿真门双胞胎，保留真实几何和外观；其次仿真内循环代理将门铰链参数化为技能程序，并通过迭代修复失败轨迹生成物理可执行的演示；然后用这些演示训练ArticuACT双深度策略，采用机器人中心相机条件和交互感知监督，输出底盘、手臂、夹爪的协调动作。
   - 结果：在5扇真实门测试中平均成功率96.57%，对结构相似的未见过门零样本成功率80.95%，全程平均耗时约13秒，且全部感知与推理均在机载完成。结果表明，仅用单段视频即可快速生成可迁移的开门穿越技能，大幅降低部署成本。

3. [DreamHand: Repurposing Video Diffusion Models for Occlusion-Robust Egocentric 3D Hand Motion Recovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20308v1)
   - 分类：3D Hand Motion / Video Diffusion
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接为具身智能提供从第一视角视频恢复手部轨迹的高效方法
   - 痛点：第一视角视频蕴含丰富的操作数据，但严重物体遮挡和手部短暂离开画面导致度量级3D手部轨迹恢复困难。现有单帧或滑动窗口回归器在手部出画时失效，而视频扩散模型作为像素渲染器需要大量随机多步采样且不稳定，难以高效获得鲁棒的几何信息。
   - 方法：将视频扩散模型重新用作确定性几何编码器，在干净潜在空间上单次前向传播即可看到当前观测之外的场景内容（包括遮挡与出画手部）。DreamHand采用离线片段级框架，包含确定性干净潜在编码器和双向时空解码器，恢复带有度量位置的连续双手轨迹，无需外部检测器；另有基于射线的相机求解器支持无需测试时相机内参的配置。
   - 结果：在5个第一视角基准上刷新SOTA，在遮挡严重的ARCTIC上MPJPE-p降低30%，在HOT3D上降低40%；若将出画手部纳入评估，性能提升达46%-61%。这为从日常人视频到机器人操作数据提供了可扩展的路径，显著优于现有回归器和像素渲染式视频扩散模型。

**主要痛点**
- 机器人操作中扩散/流策略缺乏可处理的似然函数，难以进行似然型离线RL后训练
- 长时程定位-操作任务（如开门）从单视频到仿真迁移困难，真实部署成本高
- 第一视角手部轨迹恢复受遮挡和出画影响大，现有方法鲁棒性不足
- 智能体技能迁移可靠性低，跨任务技能归纳方式缺乏系统研究

**方法趋势**
- 免采样优势加权似然目标与一步蒸馏结合，解决自回归流采样瓶颈
- 单视频重建仿真门孪生，通过仿真内闭环生成演示并训练机器人策略
- 将视频扩散模型改造为确定性几何编码器，无需多步采样即可感知遮挡信息
- 提出解耦的规则感知规划框架，分离感知、执行与验证环节

**结果信号**
- RoMAN-Flow在多个模拟与真实基准上保持性能并显著降低推理延迟
- Video2DoorTraversal在5扇真实门平均成功率96.57%，零样本80.95%
- DreamHand在五个基准上降低MPJPE-p 30%-61%，刷新SOTA
- Inter-X++提供统一交互基准，OpenHHI在生成与感知任务上双SOTA

   - 分类：Recursive Self-Improvement / LLM Agents Benchmarking

2. [MidTool: Mid-training Data Synthesis for Agentic Tool Use](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20314v1)

### 1. [4DAnyone: Create Anyone in 4D from a Casual Monocular Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20335v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：attention, diffusion, BOTH novel-view video quality, light-stage and in-the-wild video, DGS existing camera-controlled video, in-the-wild video datasets
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20335v1>

**原始摘要**

We present 4DAnyone, a framework for reconstructing 4D humans from an uncalibrated monocular video by generating reconstruction-grade multiview-consistent videos and lifting them into 4D Gaussian Splatting (4DGS). Existing camera-controlled video diffusion models synthesize plausible novel-view videos but fail to maintain consistency when scaled to the tens of target views required for 4DGS reconstruction. We identify this failure as a bounded-attention-context problem: when target views exceed the capacity of a single DiT forward pass, they must be split into groups, exposing two coupled bottlenecks. On the reference-context side, conditioning on all previously generated views grows as $O(N)$, weakening cross-view appearance guidance. On the target-context side, disjoint groups cannot directly exchange information, causing global structural drift. 4DAnyone addresses both bottlenecks with two complementary designs: Reference Context Packing (RCP) compresses growing reference views into a fixed-length mixed-resolution context with $O(1)$ reference-context complexity, while Target Context Routing (TCR) rotates target-view groupings during denoising to share context across groups at high-noise steps and stabilize details at low-noise steps. We further build the MVGameHuman dataset using our in-house game engine and combine it with light-stage and in-the-wild video datasets for training. Experiments on DNA-Rendering and DyMVHumans show that 4DAnyone outperforms prior methods in both novel-view video quality and downstream 4DGS reconstruction, with robust in-the-wild generalization. See our project page for video results and source code: https://4danyone.github.io.

### 2. [WithEveryone: Unified Planning and Identity Grounding for Group Image Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20336v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：benchmark, similarity from for gpt-image-2, identity--layout PLAN and renders, training-time identity losses MUST, location while training-time identity, highest target-context identity similarity
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20336v1>

**原始摘要**

Identity-preserving image generation becomes increasingly unreliable when a scene must contain many specified people. Beyond retaining each identity, the model must bind every reference to a distinct person and location, while training-time identity losses must establish correspondence among several noisy predicted faces. We introduce WithEveryone, a unified framework for generating group images up to ten reference identities. WithEveryone injects each selected identity as an addressed token, predicts a structured identity--layout plan, and renders the plan as a visual condition. Its key objective, Layout-Grounded ID Loss, uses annotated face regions to supervise the intended identities directly, avoiding unstable embedding-based face matching; ID Representation Forcing additionally trains a prediction for each identity before image synthesis. On an identity-disjoint benchmark, WithEveryone achieves the highest target-context identity similarity, improving face similarity from 0.462 for GPT-Image-2 to 0.499, while reducing copy-paste artifacts from 0.169 to 0.055. It further covers 97.3\% of the requested identities with a duplicate rate of only 2.8\%. These results show that explicit identity--layout grounding enables identity-preserving generation to scale to larger groups without relying on direct reference-face copying.

### 7. [RoMAN-Flow: Taming Autoregressive Normalizing Flows for Offline Reinforcement Learning in Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20208v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：reinforcement learning, offline reinforcement, autoregressive normalizing flows, robotic manipulation, diffusion, benchmarks and real-world robotic
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20208v1>

**原始摘要**

Offline reinforcement learning improves robotic policies using previously collected data without further environment interaction. Yet prevalent diffusion- and flow-matching robot policies lack tractable likelihoods, limiting their use in likelihood-based offline RL post-training. AR-NFs offer both expressive action modeling and exact likelihood evaluation, but their sequential sampling incurs substantial sampling overhead during policy optimization and deployment. We present RoMAN-Flow (Robotic Manipulation with Autoregressive Normalizing Flows), an offline reinforcement learning framework that makes AR-NF policies practical for robotic manipulation by addressing this sampling bottleneck in both stages. Experiments across multiple simulated manipulation benchmarks and real-world robotic platforms demonstrate that RoMAN-Flow achieves competitive policy performance while substantially reducing inference latency. ### 1. [ConceptGuard: Benchmarking Context-Sensitive Unlearning in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20338v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：benchmark, unlearning techniques, large language models, forget and retain SETS, provide ideas for unlearning, unlearning namely the ability
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20338v1>

**原始摘要**

Large Language Models (LLMs) increasingly require selective removal of harmful or sensitive knowledge, called unlearning, yet existing methods and benchmarks fail to evaluate this capability completely. This framing fails to capture a key requirement of unlearning, namely the ability to eliminate harmful behaviors while preserving benign and beneficial knowledge. We argue that effective unlearning must operate at the level of concepts, ensuring complete removal of unsafe applications while maintaining their correct and useful usage, thereby achieving conceptually meaningful and complete unlearning. To better evaluate unlearning techniques from such a practical viewpoint, we introduce the notion of dual-use concepts: concepts that can be used in both harmful and benign contexts. Building on these concepts, we construct a benchmark called ConceptGuard where forget and retain sets are explicitly complementary in concept usage. Our benchmark uniquely enables unlearning to be explored and gauged at the level of concepts, instead of sparse facts, and evaluation is intent-sensitive with the goal of maximizing contextual separation to promote safer behavior. We demonstrate that current unlearning techniques perform poorly under this setting, showing weak contextual separation alongside poor performance in ROUGE and concept-level metrics. Our results reveal strong forgetting-utility trade-offs, limited gains in contextual sensitivity, and poor consistency in concept-level control across methods, and provide ideas for unlearning approaches that better align with real-world safety requirements. Our dataset is publicly available.
