1. [DMAD: Distribution Matching as Adversarial Distillation for Fast Visual Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02188v1)
   - 分类：Diffusion Distillation / Few-Step Generation
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接面向扩散模型少步蒸馏与快速视觉生成，与本领域核心问题高度相关。
   - 痛点：现有 DMD 类方法必须维护一个与学生同步演化的辅助扩散模型来估计目标与学生分数差，这带来额外显存和计算；而 GAN 式对抗蒸馏虽避免辅助分数拟合，却依赖判别器训练且与分布匹配梯度的理论联系不明确。如何在无需辅助分数模型、无需启发式曲率修正的情况下稳定获得分布匹配梯度，并统一适配图像、视频与音视频快速生成，是该方向的核心痛点。
   - 方法：DMAD 将分布匹配重写为分类问题，在共享骨干上设置两个判别器头，分别区分真实/教师样本与学生样本，并直接学习对数密度比；利用判别器 logits 与 log-density ratio 的经典恒等式，在判别器最优处证明线性损失能恢复 DMD 的分布匹配梯度。还提出基于 gap 的重加权，利用真实数据头在真实与教师样本间的经验 logit gap，自适应调节不同噪声级别的教师监督。
   - 结果：在 ImageNet-64x64 上一步生成达到 FID 1.04，在 COCO-10K 上用四步 SDXL 达到 FID 14.47，在 Wan2.1-T2V-14B 四步生成上取得 VBench 总分 85.15，均为对比方法中最佳。在 MiniMax-H3-33B 音视频联合生成上，四步学生相对 DMD2 和 rCM 的人类偏好率分别达 79.1% 和 84.6%（排除平局），表明无辅助分数拟合的对抗蒸馏可高效实现高质量少步生成。

2. [HiPhy: Hierarchical Alignment for Physically-Plausible Multi-Principle Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02197v1)
   - 分类：Video Generation / Physics Alignment
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：聚焦视频生成中的物理规律对齐与多原则交互，属于生成式世界模型的重要方向。
   - 痛点：视频生成模型虽具备高视觉保真度，但难以遵守物理规律，多物理原则并发场景（如浮力与流体动力学同时发生）更易失败；现有方法通常每个视频只关注单一物理原则，忽略原则间交互，且缺少多原则物理评测基准。如何让生成视频在局部时间动态和全局场景语义上同时物理一致，是迈向通用世界模拟器的关键痛点，也是多原则交互场景下竞争方法退化最严重之处。
   - 方法：HiPhy 提出分层物理对齐的强化学习框架，采用双层目标：局部约束单个物理原则的时间动态，全局保证整个场景的物理与语义连贯。为支持多原则生成，构建 5 万条提示数据集和 MultiPhyBench 基准，覆盖多种并发物理事件，并在 RL 中联合优化物理常识与语义对齐；其奖励设计同时考虑局部事件时序与全局场景一致性，避免单一原则监督带来的偏置。
   - 结果：实验表明 HiPhy 显著优于先前方法和基线，在多个基准上提升物理常识和语义对齐，尤其在多个物理原则并发的场景中增益最大，而这些场景恰是竞争方法退化最严重的地方。该工作同时提供了多原则物理视频生成的训练数据与评测协议，推动视频世界模型的物理可信度，并为后续多原则物理对齐研究建立可复用基准。

3. [Embedding Prediction Helps Image Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02203v1)
   - 分类：Diffusion Transformer / Conditional Generation
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：改进 DiT 的条件机制，与扩散模型架构和高效生成直接相关。
   - 痛点：扩散 Transformer 通常只在去噪开始时嵌入一次类别或文本条件，并在每一步重复使用同一条件，条件信号无法随当前噪声状态自适应调整；如何让生成器在去噪过程中获得动态、与当前噪声图像相关的条件，同时不显著增加采样开销，是提升条件生成质量与效率的痛点。现有 REPA 等方法通过外部对齐改善表示，但仍未直接让条件随去噪步演化。
   - 方法：提出 NEPA（Next-Embedding Predictive Autoregression），训练 Transformer 预测序列中的下一个连续嵌入；在生成中干净图像嵌入位于条件与噪声图像之后，因此用 Multi-Embedding Prediction 一次性预测这些嵌入，并以 Embedding Conditioned Generation 让 DiT 在每个去噪步都以重新计算的预测嵌入为条件。NEPA 作为第二网络参与采样，可与 REPA 结合，并研究多嵌入预测设计与两模型扩展。
   - 结果：在类别条件 ImageNet 256×256 上研究生成器条件设计、多嵌入预测设计与模型扩展；最终模型 NEPA-DiT-XL 结合 REPA 达到 FID 1.32，训练计算量约为 REPA 的三分之一。结果表明预测嵌入作为动态条件能有效提升图像生成质量并改善计算效率，为 DiT 条件机制提供新方向。

**主要痛点**
- 扩散与视频生成中的物理规律、多原则一致性和长时程记忆仍是瓶颈
- 少步蒸馏与推理时缩放面临辅助模型开销和预训练模型不完美问题
- 3D 生成中的拓扑保持、token 效率和可控编辑仍需改进
- 概念擦除、水印安全与评测协议缺乏鲁棒性和可复现性

**方法趋势**
- 对抗蒸馏、RL 对齐、预测嵌入条件、Feynman-Kac 校正等生成优化方法
- 3D/4D 几何 scaffold、切片潜变量、时空 KV 记忆等表示与记忆机制
- 程序化世界模型基准、评估管道和物理评测协议建设
- 离散扩散样本复杂度与多模态采样理论分析

**结果信号**
- 少步生成在 FID、VBench 和人类偏好上刷新指标
- 物理与可控视频生成在并发原则、视角变化和重访一致性上提升
- 3D 生成在拓扑误差和 token 效率上明显改善
- 理论工作揭示多模态采样和离散扩散样本复杂度的结构性限制

1. [TACO: Ternary Absolute-max Column-wise One-sparse Optimizer for LLM Fine-Tuning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02199v1)
   - 分类：Efficient Fine-Tuning / Optimizer
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接针对大模型全参数微调的优化器状态显存瓶颈，与高效训练方向高度契合。
   - 痛点：大语言模型全参数微调时，AdamW等优化器需要为每个参数保存一阶矩和二阶矩，优化器状态显存开销巨大，严重限制单卡可微调的模型规模。现有方法要么压缩优化器状态但损失精度，要么放弃一阶梯度信息，要么改变更新几何导致在AdamW预训练模型上性能下降，因此如何在几乎不损失精度和计算效率的前提下大幅降低优化器显存，是高效训练的关键痛点。
   - 方法：TACO沿用Muon的算子范数最速下降视角，在维度归一化的1→1算子范数下，对二维权重矩阵每列选取绝对值最大元素的符号，构造精确最速下降方向。实际实现仅保留每列少量低精度梯度分量，避免额外非线性乘性深度，保持一阶梯度信息，同时使持久优化器状态几乎可忽略；无需线搜索或启发式曲率修正，可直接替换AdamW类优化器。
   - 结果：在OPT-13B上，TACO将持久优化器状态相对AdamW8bit减少174倍（27.7GB降至0.16GB），峰值训练显存降低2.9倍（80.6GB降至27.5GB），精度与运行时和AdamW相当。进一步在单张80GB H100上实现30-32B参数模型的全参数微调，跨多个模型家族和任务验证有效，为显存受限的高效微调提供了新路径。

2. [DMAD: Distribution Matching as Adversarial Distillation for Fast Visual Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02188v1)
   - 分类：Fast Generation / Distillation
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：面向少步生成的高效推理，将分布匹配重写为对抗蒸馏，与高效推理方向直接相关。
   - 痛点：扩散模型推理需要多步采样，计算成本高；分布匹配蒸馏（DMD）虽能训练少步学生，但必须维护一个与不断变化的学生分布拟合的辅助扩散模型，带来额外显存和计算开销，且训练不稳定、需要辅助分数拟合。如何在不拟合辅助分数的情况下实现高效少步生成，是视觉生成推理加速的核心痛点。
   - 方法：DMAD将分布匹配重铸为分类问题，在共享骨干上设置两个判别器头，分别区分真实数据、教师样本与学生样本，并直接学习所需的对数密度比；利用判别器logits与对数密度比的经典恒等式，在线性损失下训练学生，无需辅助分数拟合。还引入基于间隙的重加权，根据真实数据头在真实与教师样本间的经验logit间隙，自适应调整不同噪声水平下的教师监督。
   - 结果：DMAD在ImageNet-64×64上单步生成达到FID 1.04，COCO-10K上四步SDXL达到FID 14.47，四步Wan2.1-T2V-14B达到VBench总分85.15，均为对比少步方法及多步教师中的最优值；在MiniMax-H3-33B上四步学生相对DMD2和rCM的人类偏好率分别为79.1%和84.6%，显著优于基于辅助分数拟合的DMD2等方案。

3. [Decoding Looped Transformers Better for (Almost) Free](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02185v1)
   - 分类：Efficient Inference / Decoding
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：训练免费的对比解码方法，同时提升效果并减少循环深度和前向FLOPs，直接服务高效推理。
   - 痛点：循环Transformer通过重复执行共享块实现参数高效，但标准解码只使用最终循环的输出，丢弃了早期循环的中间表示；早期循环计算量较少，天然构成对齐的弱-强预测对，却未被利用。如何在无需额外模型或训练的情况下，利用这些中间状态提升解码质量并降低推理计算，是循环架构推理效率的痛点。
   - 方法：LoopCD是一种训练免费的对比解码框架，用最终预测与较早循环的预测进行对比来指导token选择。它有两种变体：LoopCD-Logits在logit空间操作，需要一次额外输出传递；LoopCD-Hidden在隐状态空间操作，零输出开销。该方法将中间循环状态转化为弱模型指导信号，无需辅助模型或外部训练，可直接应用于现有循环Transformer。
   - 结果：在四个循环Transformer家族上，LoopCD在全循环深度下取得一致且显著的提升：LoopCD-Logits将Ouro-2.6B-Thinking的AIME 2024 pass@1从61.88%提升到73.33%，LoopCD-Hidden将Huginn的HumanEval pass@1从22.56%提升到31.71%；性能提升还允许将循环次数减半，同时匹配或超过全深度无指导基线，减少前向FLOPs 22.5%至48.2%。

**主要痛点**
- 大模型微调优化器状态显存瓶颈严重
- 少步生成蒸馏需辅助模型开销大
- 循环Transformer推理未利用中间状态
- MoE领域适应成本高
- 长程智能体上下文管理困难

**方法趋势**
- 一阶/零阶解耦优化与显存压缩
- 对抗蒸馏重铸分布匹配
- 训练免费对比解码
- 局部适配与专家选择
- 上下文压缩与KV记忆管理

**结果信号**
- TACO减少174倍优化器状态并支持30-32B单卡微调
- DMAD单步FID 1.04、四步VBench 85.15
- LoopCD提升AIME/HumanEval并减少FLOPs 22.5-48.2%
- ExpertLens实现4倍训练加速
- AutoCompact在SWE-bench提升9.2%

1. [Reconstruct, Practice, Go Real: Guided Self-Improvement for Embodied Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02204v1)
   - 分类：Embodied Agents / Self-Improvement
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接对应具身智能体自主技能改进与测试时自我提升，是机器人执行系统免权重更新学习的代表工作。
   - 痛点：机器人要在多样任务上可靠执行，通常依赖大量人工开发技能、设计奖励并整合感知与控制；当面对新任务或新初始化时，人工维护的技能库和提示难以覆盖长尾情况，且每次更新模型权重成本高、风险大；现有方法往往无法同时兼顾长期复用与在线适应，因此如何让具身智能体在不更新模型参数的前提下，利用离线数据、仿真实践和执行反馈自主诊断失败、扩展可复用技能并持续提升真实任务成功率，是亟待解决的痛点。
   - 方法：RPG框架先从离线数据集识别可操作能力，在仿真中构造相关练习任务；练习时利用执行反馈、特权仿真状态和数据集视频诊断失败，发展新符号技能、精炼已有技能并修订系统提示；再通过跨任务评估测试候选修改与合并修订，保留有效修改；测试时多模态LLM使用最终系统提示和技能库协调感知与机器人控制，从而在不更新权重的情况下持续改进执行系统。
   - 结果：在22个操作任务的留出初始化上，RPG从首轮练习28.6%成功率提升到15轮后95.0%，优于ASPIRE的75.5%和由GPT-6 Astra Pro驱动的CaP-Agent0的60.0%；经统一校准和硬件适配后，冻结系统在30次物理试验中全部成功，三个任务各10次，证明无需更新模型权重即可实现从仿真练习到真实机器人的自主能力提升。

2. [InterEvolve: Test-Time Evolution of Reward Programs for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02196v1)
   - 分类：Humanoid Loco-Manipulation / Reinforcement Learning
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接面向人形机器人loco-manipulation的测试时策略复用与奖励程序自演化，属于具身强化学习前沿。
   - 痛点：人形机器人loco-manipulation任务通常需要为每个新任务重新训练控制器或手工设计奖励，测试时面对未训练任务时难以复用已有技能；已有控制器虽具备广泛运动能力，但如何通过规划与控制接口把接触丰富、多阶段交互表达出来，并利用执行反馈在测试时自主演化奖励程序、释放已有运动能力，仍缺乏有效方法；同时真实硬件部署还要求学习过程不破坏安全执行与已有能力。
   - 方法：InterEvolve用object-aware forward-backward行为基础模型，在冻结身体先验上通过物体残差把新奖励转化为loco-manipulation行为；任务被写成带完成条件和可调常数的分阶段奖励程序，LLM代理根据执行反馈和已验证程序技能库在上下文中修改结构，数值优化器调节常数；每个候选在并行仿真场景中验证，从而探索诱导、复用和组合已有运动能力的新方式。
   - 结果：实验显示人工设计奖励只释放FB模型部分loco-manipulation能力，而InterEvolve演化程序能释放更多能力，有时产生全新策略；方法在多样任务、复杂场景和长时程组合中生成行为，演化技能可在Unitree G1上基于自我中心机载感知自主运行，证明测试时无需重训即可演化规划-控制接口。

3. [UniWAM: Unified World-Action Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02054v1)
   - 分类：World-Action Model / Vision-Language-Action

   - 痛点：VLA模型有语义理解但仅动作监督对世界动态接地不足，世界-动作模型继承视频生成时空先验但语义理解和分布偏移下推理有限；如何统一物理推理、视觉生成与动作预测，并利用人类自我中心数据与机器人数据互补监督，同时降低去噪步数并保持性能，是具身基础模型的关键痛点；此外，大规模人机混合预训练的缩放规律尚不清晰，限制了数据配方和模型规模的决策。

### 1. [KaliBench: A Fine-Grained Benchmark for Cybersecurity Tool Use on Kali Linux with Runtime-Free Verifiable Rewards](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02206v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：fine-grained benchmark, runtime-free verifiable, reinforcement learning, KALI linux, cybersecurity TOOL, verifiable rewards
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02206v1>

**原始摘要**

LLMs are increasingly applied to cybersecurity workflows, where they are expected to translate analysts' intent into tool invocations. However, existing evaluations focus on knowledge-based assessments or end-to-end agentic tasks, and do not directly measure LLMs' ability to generate executable commands for real-world cybersecurity tools. This gap is critical because cybersecurity operations rely on strict command-line interfaces (CLIs), where minor syntax errors, incorrect flag--value bindings, or argument misordering can invalidate execution. We introduce KaliBench, a fine-grained benchmark and dataset for natural-language--to--CLI translation on Kali Linux, comprising 8,504 query--command pairs spanning 1,642 tools across 23 capability dimensions and 5 security phases. KaliBench is constructed via a manuscript-grounded pipeline with deterministic canonicalization and alias-aware evaluation, enabling precise and reproducible assessment of tool selection and argument construction. To ensure both semantic correctness and practical executability, we develop a multi-stage verification pipeline that combines LLM-based validation, sandboxed terminal execution, and human-in-the-loop refinement. Building on these fine-grained, deterministic signals, KaliBench further enables runtime-free verifiable rewards for training. Across three evaluation modes and 24 configurations of general-purpose and security-focused open-weight models, no open-weight model exceeds 42% exact-command accuracy in the unrestricted setting, highlighting the difficulty of accurate CLI-based cybersecurity tool use without explicit tool hints. We further show that supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards derived from KaliBench significantly improve an 8B model and achieve performance comparable to a 685B MoE model.

### 2. [ROWBench: Do Video Models Render What the Program Specifies?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02205v1)

**原始摘要**

Programmable world models separate executable dynamics from visual generation, offering a promising foundation for next-generation game engines. However, their visual adherence to explicit rules and interactions remains insufficiently evaluated. Existing benchmarks assess visual quality, controllability, and instruction or physical adherence, but rarely test fidelity to fine-grained, program-specified world events. Grounded in these records, PROWBench evaluates entity control, long-horizon memory, and, with two VLM-based metrics, Logic-Render Alignment and Interaction Success Rate, adherence to the prescribed timeline and the visual realization of timestamped engine-recorded events.

### 5. [FERPO: Forward Entropy-Regularized Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02198v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：reinforcement learning, forward entropy-regularized policy, policy optimization, ferpo forward entropy-regularized, forward-kl objective, algorithm that performs policy
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02198v1>

**原始摘要**

Several state-of-the-art methods for online reinforcement learning in continuous control improve policies using action gradients of a learned critic. However, critics are typically trained to predict returns, and accurate value predictions do not necessarily yield accurate action derivatives, potentially leading to unreliable policy updates. We propose Forward Entropy-Regularized Policy Optimization (FERPO), an on-policy maximum entropy reinforcement learning algorithm that performs policy improvement using critic values without differentiating the critic with respect to actions. FERPO derives an optimal target action distribution from a policy-improvement objective regularized by entropy and Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence. We then fit the actor to this target by minimizing a forward-KL objective, estimated using self-normalized importance sampling (SNIS) with actions drawn from the rollout policy. By limiting the target distribution's deviation from the rollout policy, the KL regularization helps keep these importance weights well behaved. In contrast to reverse-KL objectives, which can favor a subset of the target distribution's modes, the forward-KL objective encourages coverage of multiple high-value modes and thereby promotes exploration. Experiments and ablations on MuJoCo Playground and ManiSkill show competitive performance and sample-efficiency gains. Computational benchmarks also demonstrate faster actor updates than Relative Entropy Pathwise Policy Optimization (REPPO).

### 6. [HiPhy: Hierarchical Alignment for Physically-Plausible Multi-Principle Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02197v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：video generation, reinforcement learning, hiphy hierarchical, alignment, benchmark, methods largely ignore multi-principle
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02197v1>

**原始摘要**

Video generation models have achieved remarkable visual fidelity and have strong potential to become general-purpose world simulators. Despite this progress, they still fail to generate videos which adhere to laws of physics. The problem becomes even more apparent in realistic settings where multiple physical principles must work together within the same video; for example, "a balloon floating upward while steam rises from a pot" requires buoyancy and fluid dynamics to unfold coherently and simultaneously. Yet existing methods largely ignore multi-principle interactions, focusing on a single principle per video. To support multi-principle generation, we construct a 50K-prompt dataset and introduce a prompt benchmark MultiPhyBench, spanning a diverse range of co-occurring physical events. Our experiments show that HiPhy significantly outperforms prior methods and baselines, improving physical commonsense and semantic alignment significantly across various benchmarks, with the largest gains on scenes involving multiple concurrent physical principles where competing methods degrade most sharply.

### 7. [InterEvolve: Test-Time Evolution of Reward Programs for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02196v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：test-time evolution, reward programs, agent, objects into loco-manipulation behavior, evolution for humanoid loco-manipulation, programs for humanoid loco-manipulation.
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02196v1>

**原始摘要**

We study test-time evolution for humanoid loco-manipulation: solving tasks that a controller was never trained for by repurposing its existing skills, improving from its own attempts, and retaining what it learns, without retraining. Our key insight is that a broad controller already holds much of the competence a new task needs, and that this competence becomes accessible through an interface between planning and control that is expressive enough to specify contact-rich, multi-stage interactions, yet executable and measurable enough that execution feedback can guide planning from experience. InterEvolve realizes this interface with two components. First, we develop an object-aware forward-backward (FB) behavioral foundation model, whose object residuals on a frozen body prior turn a new reward about the body or objects into loco-manipulation behavior at test time. Second, we specify tasks as reward programs: staged rewards with completion conditions and tunable constants. A large language model (LLM) agent revises the program structure in context, drawing on execution feedback and a skill library of verified programs, while a numerical optimizer tunes its constants. With every candidate verified across parallel simulation scenarios, the program explores new ways to induce, repurpose, and compose the controller's existing motor competence for the task at hand, and thus improves over iterations. Experiments show that human-designed rewards leave much of the FB model's loco-manipulation competence untapped, whereas the programs InterEvolve evolves release it, sometimes through novel strategies. It further produces behaviors for diverse tasks, complex scenes, and long-horizon compositions in simulation, and evolved skills run autonomously on a physical Unitree G1 from egocentric onboard perception.

### 8. [Cost-augmented Schrödinger bridges on graphs are exactly solvable: a Feynman-Kac tilt replaces learned control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02195v1)

- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02195v1>

**原始摘要**

The generalized Schrödinger bridge on a graph moves mass between two distributions while charging a cost for the states visited. It has been approached by learning the rates of a controlled continuous-time Markov chain, with a temporal-difference penalty that restores the cost. A state cost folds into the reference process as a Feynman-Kac tilt. The cost-augmented bridge is then a plain bridge against the tilted reference, and the penalty is unnecessary. ### 9. [Hierarchical Continuous Diffusion Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02193v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：diffusion language, continuous diffusion, hierarchical continuous, reasoning, autoregressive, language modeling lm1b hc-dlm
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02193v1>

**原始摘要**

Discrete diffusion language models offer a compelling alternative to autoregressive generation for tasks demanding bidirectional reasoning and global constraint satisfaction. Yet they share a structural bottleneck: when decoding in parallel, each token is sampled independently from its marginal, severing the statistical dependencies among the tokens decoded together. Continuous diffusion language models avoid this by denoising a shared continuous state, but their denoiser sees only that state, so nothing ties it to a valid token configuration until it is finally decoded. To address this, we propose Hierarchical Continuous Diffusion Language Models (HC-DLM), which couple discrete token generation with a continuous latent trajectory in a single, principled denoising process, whose training objective is derived from a variational bound on the token likelihood. In contrast to recent methods that attach continuous context to a self-contained discrete chain, HC-DLM makes the latent the only persistent generative state: tokens are read out from it at every step and feed back as a scaffold for the next latent update. On structured reasoning (Sudoku), mathematical planning (Countdown) and language modeling (LM1B), HC-DLM improves over discrete and continuous diffusion baselines at matched model size, in puzzle accuracy on Sudoku and Countdown and in generative perplexity on LM1B. Project page: https://hc-dlm.github.io/.

### 10. [Generative modeling of intrinsically disordered protein regions by reinforcing sparse autoencoder features](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02189v1)

- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02189v1>

**原始摘要**

Intrinsically disordered protein regions (IDRs) play central roles in cellular processes such as transcriptional regulation, signal transduction, and subcellular localization, yet their functional design remains challenging. Structure-based design methods do not readily apply to IDRs, and existing protein language models are trained on full-length protein sequences, thus learning a prior that is biased towards folded domains. Here, we present IDiom, an autoregressive protein language model trained on IDiom-DB, a dataset of 54 million predicted IDRs curated from the AlphaFold Database. More broadly, RL-SAE could extend to other protein design settings where interpretable features provide useful design targets. Code is available at https://github.com/rotskoff-group/idiom.

**原始摘要**

Current controllable video generation systems often rely on 2D motion trajectories or sparse drag signals for object motion.
