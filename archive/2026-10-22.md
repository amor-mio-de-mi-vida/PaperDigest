1. [Attn-Forcing: Learning Full-Context Attention Proxies for Streaming Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30036v1)
   - 分类：Streaming Video Generation / Attention Compression
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接关联流式视频扩散与长上下文注意力效率，是视频生成基础设施的重要进展。
   - 痛点：流式视频生成中，扩大注意力上下文能显著提升质量，但历史帧增长会带来不可接受的内存与计算开销；现有注意力选择或压缩方法受限于有界表示，且缺少注意力级显式监督，难以保留历史上下文对当前查询的真实贡献，容易在长程生成中丢失一致性，因此需要在不无限扩展 KV 的情况下逼近全上下文注意力，并避免历史信息被有界缓存截断，这对长视频和持续交互场景尤为关键。
   - 方法：论文将历史上下文对当前查询的注意力响应压缩为可学习视觉 latent 代理，并设计 Proxy Update Module 持续吸收历史 latent 中的视觉上下文；同时提出 Attention Matching Loss，用全上下文注意力作为监督，显式约束代理的注意力行为，使其在固定内存与计算预算下模拟完整注意力。整个框架可接入流式视频扩散模型，训练时学习代理更新与匹配，推理时以代理替代历史 KV 的显式聚合，从而保持因果流式生成。
   - 结果：在多项定性与定量实验中，Attn-Forcing 在固定内存和计算下持续提升流式视频生成质量，并在与现有注意力选择、压缩及流式视频基线比较时达到 SOTA；结果表明显式全上下文注意力监督比仅靠有界表示更有效，尤其有利于长程一致性和历史信息保留。消融显示代理更新与匹配损失均为关键，说明该方法能在不随上下文长度线性增加成本的前提下逼近全上下文注意力。

2. [Rethinking Video Autoencoders for Interactive World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30129v1)
   - 分类：Video Autoencoders / Interactive World Models
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接关联交互式世界模型与视频 tokenizer 压缩，适合评估生成式模拟器的成本与延迟权衡。
   - 痛点：交互式世界模型常直接复用文本到视频生成器的视频自编码器，这些自编码器为离线生成设计，没有智能体等待延迟约束，也缺乏原始训练数据和代码，难以在其他压缩率下重训；因此世界模型应在何处、以何种程度压缩视频，以及压缩如何影响每帧成本、动作生效延迟和长程预测质量，仍缺少系统答案。尤其当相机运动、操作与导航场景并存时，统一沿用 T2V 压缩可能造成次优的延迟-质量权衡，论文因此把视频自编码器压缩作为交互式世界模型的一等设计变量。
   - 方法：论文构建 CADRE，一个开放的因果扩散表示自编码器族，覆盖多种时空压缩率；各成员只在冻结特征上增加小瓶颈，并共享一个自蒸馏到一步或少数步的扩散解码器。作者在操作与导航两个域、各两个环境中，用同一世界模型分别搭配不同 CADRE 自编码器训练，系统比较压缩位置、压缩维度与解码器类型对延迟和生成质量的影响，从而把自编码器设计与世界模型 rollout 性能解耦分析。
   - 结果：CADRE 构成延迟-质量前沿的大部分，尤其在移动相机下优势明显；研究发现强压缩时扩散解码器比感知回归更锐利，机器人操作中时间压缩放在自编码器比放在世界模型更有效，操作与导航需要相反的压缩偏好，且静止相机下更粗的空间 token 对运动伤害远大于对图像质量的影响。这些结论为交互式世界模型的视频 tokenizer 设计提供了可复现的经验准则，并开源代码、模型与演示。

3. [Loopholing All the Way: Language Modeling with pure Self-Conditioning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30068v1)
   - 分类：Diffusion Language Models / Self-Conditioning
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接关联扩散语言模型与自条件化生成机制，适合文本生成与推理方向跟踪。
   - 痛点：扩散语言模型近期依赖 Self-Conditioning（Loopholing）提升性能，但通常把它当作辅助启发式，尚不清楚连续生成模型能否仅靠自条件化完成建模；同时离散 token 在多步去噪中反复解码会引入不一致，如何传播连续期望嵌入并在最后统一解码，是提升扩散语言模型推理与生成质量的关键问题。论文要回答纯自条件化是否足以替代传统离散扩散路径，并在 Sudoku、Maze 和数学推理上验证。
   - 方法：论文提出 LAW，将去噪器预测下的 Expected Embedding 在每一步之间传播，而不是每步采样并解码离散 token，只在最终时刻一次性解码；整个生成过程完全由自条件化驱动，形成连续的 loopholing 路径。作者在 Sudoku、Maze、TinyGSM 和 OpenWebText 上与离散扩散、连续扩散及 Loopholing Discrete Diffusion 对比，并进一步用 LAW 微调预训练 Uniform Diffusion Model Duo。
   - 结果：LAW 在 Sudoku 上超过此前的离散与连续扩散模型，在 Maze 上于 2 到 64 NFE 范围内最准确；在 TinyGSM 训练、温度 1、1024 NFE 采样时，GSM8K 解决率为 42.2%，高于 uniform Loopholing Discrete Diffusion 的 34.5%，微调 Duo 更把准确率从 17.2% 提升到 48.1%。在 OpenWebText 上，LAW 以 64 NFE 推进 Pareto 前沿并接近自回归模型，说明纯自条件化可作为连续扩散语言模型的有效主机制。

**主要痛点**
- 流式与长视频生成受限于注意力上下文成本及长程一致性
- 交互式世界模型缺少可重训的视频压缩与实例物理校准
- 扩散语言模型的自条件化机制与训练效率仍待系统理解
- 生成模型在合成数据训练下面临崩溃与多样性退化

**方法趋势**
- 注意力代理与全上下文匹配监督
- 因果扩散表示自编码器族与多压缩率对照
- 纯自条件化连续嵌入传播、循环 Transformer、未来块预测
- 物理描述子上下文校准与谱分析正则化

**结果信号**
- Attn-Forcing 在固定预算下达到流式视频生成 SOTA
- CADRE 给出延迟-质量前沿与四项压缩设计发现
- LAW 在 GSM8K 达 42.2% 并可把 Duo 从 17.2% 提到 48.1%
- Looped UDLM 以最多 3.3 倍更少 FLOPs 提效，Dreaming Learning 缓解模型崩溃

1. [Looped Uniform Diffusion Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30066v1)
   - 分类：Diffusion LM / Efficient Training & Inference
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接相关：通过循环层降低扩散语言模型训练FLOPs，并在匹配NFE下提升生成质量。
   - 痛点：均匀扩散语言模型必须在可能含噪的上下文中反复去噪，训练目标比掩码扩散更困难，导致FLOP开销高；现有选择性循环主要在掩码扩散中验证，尚不清楚能否在不改变训练目标和采样器的前提下提升均匀扩散的训练效率，并在匹配NFE预算下改善生成困惑度与下游推理表现。这使训练成本与推理效率成为该路线的核心瓶颈。
   - 方法：作者在每个去噪步骤内递归复用少量早期Transformer层，保持均匀扩散的训练目标和采样器不变，并系统比较不同循环次数分布；训练时更频繁采样较小循环次数，以在保留循环表达能力的同时降低平均计算量。该方法不引入额外参数结构，只改变层间计算复用方式，并在OpenWebText与FineWeb-Edu上以等FLOP预算对照非循环模型，从而将循环深度从固定超参变成可调计算预算。
   - 结果：循环均匀扩散在OpenWebText上以最高2.8倍更少训练FLOPs、在FineWeb-Edu上以3.3倍更少FLOPs达到非循环模型约0.5万亿token训练的验证NLL；匹配NFE时生成困惑度最多降低9.8%，且更频繁采样小循环数可再减少12%循环数而不损下游准确率。结果表明循环主要在中低循环次数带来收益，为扩散语言模型提供了一条训练与推理双重提效路径，同时保持采样器不变。

2. [EPIC: Rethinking Trust Regions for Autoregressive LLM Policies via Exact-KL Prefix Importance-Ratio Clipping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29999v1)
   - 分类：LLM RL Training / Trust-Region Optimization
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接相关：改进PPO式序列裁剪的信任域信号，面向长响应与离策略LLM RL训练的稳定性与效率。
   - 痛点：PPO式token级裁剪只用当前token条件概率衡量策略变化，而前缀上下文本身由策略生成，导致长回答和离策略训练中信任域约束与真实序列分布漂移不匹配，容易出现策略更新过大、训练不稳定和推理能力提升受限。现有序列级裁剪或专家路由回放虽试图缓解，但仍依赖局部二次近似或额外回放机制，缺少对前缀级精确KL漂移的直接控制。
   - 方法：论文将自回归生成建模为有限时域MDP，在行为策略采样下推导性能差恒等式，识别出生成前缀的累积似然比正是每步策略改进项的精确重要性权重；随后用精确反向KL坐标r-1-log r构造前缀级信任域，形成EPIC序列裁剪方法，直接约束前缀分布漂移而不依赖局部二次近似或专家路由回放。实现上保持PPO式优化框架，仅替换裁剪信号，使长响应训练中的信任域与真实前缀分布对齐。
   - 结果：在稀疏MoE语言模型的离策略RL实验中，EPIC在多个数学推理基准上稳定优于标准token级裁剪和序列级裁剪基线，并与基于路由回放的替代方案保持竞争力；结果表明前缀级精确KL约束能更有效控制长回答训练漂移，提升RL后训练稳定性与推理性能。该方法为LLM RL提供更精确的信任域信号，且不引入额外专家路由回放开销，实验覆盖离策略设置，直接针对长响应训练中的失配问题。

3. [Attn-Forcing: Learning Full-Context Attention Proxies for Streaming Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30036v1)
   - 分类：Streaming Video Generation / Efficient Attention
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接相关：在有限内存与计算下近似全上下文注意力，降低流式视频生成的推理成本。
   - 痛点：流式视频生成依赖不断增长的注意力上下文来保持历史一致性，但上下文变长会带来不可接受的内存与计算开销；现有注意力选择或压缩方法受限于有界表示，且缺少注意力级显式监督，难以在有限预算下保留历史上下文对当前query的真实贡献。这导致长视频生成在质量、延迟和显存之间难以兼得，尤其限制实时流式部署，需要一种在固定预算下逼近全上下文注意力的机制。
   - 方法：作者提出Attn-Forcing，用可学习视觉latent作为注意力代理，持续近似历史上下文诱导的注意力响应；Proxy Update Module吸收历史latent中的视觉上下文，Attention Matching Loss则以全上下文注意力为监督显式约束代理行为，从而在有界内存和计算下保留全上下文贡献。框架不直接扩展注意力窗口，而是学习一个可更新的代理表示来匹配历史注意力，训练与推理均保持流式生成接口。
   - 结果：定性与定量实验表明，Attn-Forcing在多个流式视频生成基准上持续提升生成质量，并优于现有注意力选择或压缩基线达到SOTA；关键结论是全上下文注意力代理可在有限内存与计算下近似历史贡献，从而缓解长上下文带来的延迟和显存瓶颈。消融显示注意力匹配监督和代理更新模块对保持时序一致性均重要，为实时流式视频生成提供了可扩展的推理方案，实验覆盖不同上下文长度。

**主要痛点**
- 训练侧：长上下文RL、扩散语言模型和LLM RL仍受数据稀缺、FLOP成本、信任域失配与信用分配不稳定困扰。
- 推理侧：流式视频、世界模型、推荐系统和长上下文生成受显存、延迟与固定计算预算限制，需要自适应或代理机制。

**方法趋势**
- 循环复用Transformer层、前缀级精确KL信任域、注意力代理与全上下文匹配、逐物品自适应递归、自条件化生成和紧凑蒸馏。
- 通过零数据自写任务、压缩率可调自编码器、语义感知稀疏化与量化、条件轨迹经验蒸馏来降低训练或部署成本。

**结果信号**
- 循环均匀扩散在最高2.8-3.3倍更少训练FLOPs下达到非循环NLL，并在匹配NFE时降低困惑度最多9.8%。
- EPIC在离策略数学推理RL中优于标准/序列裁剪；Attn-Forcing在流式视频生成中以有界内存计算达到SOTA。

1. [Agentic World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30136v1)
   - 分类：World Action Models / Agentic Robotics
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接相关：把代码规划、视频预测与动作生成统一到具身世界动作模型，覆盖技能复用与长程操作。
   - 痛点：现有世界动作模型虽能提供视觉前瞻和未来预测，但缺少可组合技能复用的显式机制；智能体机器人虽能用代码规划与推理复用技能，却往往缺少对物理动力学的预测落地，导致长程操作中规划、预测与执行彼此割裂。与此同时，代码、视频和动作三类模态的联合建模不足，使代码难以同时承担多步预测结构与可执行技能程序两种角色。
   - 方法：AW-0 在统一的 mixture-of-transformers 架构中，将代码、未来视频和机器人动作作为三条耦合生成流联合建模：代码专家生成参数化技能例程与控制逻辑，视频与动作专家分别生成未来视觉状态和底层电机指令。作者还设计自动标注流水线，把机器人/人类视频轨迹与对齐程序配对，并把重复例程整合为可复用技能库；代码既作为世界模型内部的多步预测结构，又作为后续智能体执行的技能程序。
   - 结果：在 RoboTwin 2.0 和真实双臂操作任务上，AW-0 相比 VLA 与 WAM 基线取得更高任务成功率，并在语言遵循、语义推理和鲁棒性上均有提升。评估表明，代码专家带来的技能库与联合预测有助于提升长程任务表现，而非只依赖视觉前瞻。该结果支持把代码流作为世界模型与智能体执行之间的桥梁，使规划、预测和执行在统一框架内连接，并展示可复用技能程序在物理任务中的价值。

2. [AtomicRL: Atomic Reinforcement Learning for Long-Horizon Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30097v1)
   - 分类：VLA Reinforcement Learning / Long-Horizon Manipulation
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接相关：面向 VLA 长程操作的 RL 后训练，提出原子任务信用分配、瓶颈探索和 Real2Sim2Real。
   - 痛点：现有 VLA 模型主要依赖模仿学习，难以掌握复杂长程操作；引入强化学习后又常受轨迹级稀疏奖励造成的延迟和有偏信用分配困扰。不同原子子任务的掌握程度不均衡会形成瓶颈，失败子任务的 TD 残差还会跨任务边界污染信用。真实机器人上的在线 RL 训练成本高、仿真到现实迁移困难，使 VLA 的 RL 后训练难以稳定落地。
   - 方法：AtomicRL 将长程任务分解为原子任务结构，提出 Semantic-Aware Policy Optimization（SAPO），用语义感知门控阻止失败原子子任务的 TD 残差跨任务边界传播，从而减轻信用污染，同时保留子任务内信用分配与价值自举。它还引入 Bottleneck-Adaptive Target Exploration（BATE），依据各原子子任务掌握程度动态协调任务采样与局部探索激励，把学习引向剩余瓶颈；并构建 Real2Sim2Real 流水线衔接仿真在线 RL 与真实部署。
   - 结果：在 LIBERO-LONG 上，AtomicRL 相比 PPO 在两种 VLA 架构上分别取得 3.6% 和 4.0% 的绝对性能提升；在 CALVIN ABC-D 上，全任务性能最高提升 9.5%。结合 Real2Sim2Real 流水线后，真实世界任务性能平均提升 21.3%，说明原子任务结构能改善 VLA 策略在长程操作中的学习效率。结果也表明，选择性阻断跨任务信用污染与瓶颈自适应探索是提升长程操作表现的关键。

3. [Asymmetric On-Policy Distillation Learns In-Context Exploration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30105v1)
   - 分类：Imitation Learning / Exploration
   - 研究价值：值得精读

   - 痛点：部分可观测下的序贯决策要求智能体先探索以推断隐藏任务信息再行动，但在线强化学习从头学习信息收集行为样本效率低；离线模仿又会在不同隐藏状态上平均专家动作，无法学到测试时探索。因此，如何在不付出昂贵 RL 交互成本的情况下，让历史条件学生策略学会“不确定时搜索、隐藏状态揭示后利用”的测试时探索，是具身智能体的关键痛点。视觉受限的真实机器人场景尤其需要这种高效探索能力。

### 1. [Agentic World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30136v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：world action, video and action, agentic world, reasoning, agentic robotics combines code-based, code-based planning
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30136v1>

**原始摘要**

Recent world action models provide visual foresight and future prediction for robot manipulation, but lack explicit mechanisms for compositional skill reuse. Agentic robotics combines code-based planning and reasoning with reusable skills, but often lacks predictive grounding in physical dynamics. We introduce AW-0, an agentic world action model framework that integrates code-based planning with joint video and action prediction. We jointly model code, future video, and robot actions as three coupled generative streams within a unified mixture-of-transformers architecture. Within this architecture a code expert generates parameterized skill routines and control logic while video and action experts generate future visual states and low-level motor commands. Code serves two complementary roles: it structures multi-step prediction and control within the world model and represents skills as reusable programs for subsequent agentic execution. We further introduce an automated curation pipeline that labels robot and human video trajectories with aligned programs and consolidates recurring routines into reusable skill libraries. Evaluations on RoboTwin 2.0 and physical bimanual manipulation tasks show improved task performance over VLA and WAM baselines, alongside gains in language following, semantic reasoning and robustness, demonstrating that agentic code enables connection between planning, prediction and execution within WAMs.

### 2. [Rethinking Video Autoencoders for Interactive World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30129v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：diffusion, world models, agent, text-to-video, autoencoders spanning multiple spatio-temporal, train the SAME world
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30129v1>

**原始摘要**

Video world models predict future frames from past frames and actions. They serve as simulators in which agents train, plan or are evaluated, and that humans can control interactively. They generate in the latent space of a video autoencoder, whose compression sets their cost per frame and how long each action waits to take effect. Yet world models often reuse the autoencoders of text-to-video generators, built for offline generation with no agent waiting and, without their training data and code, not retrainable at another compression. We therefore ask how, where and how much a world model should compress. We build CADRE, an open family of causal diffusion representation autoencoders spanning multiple spatio-temporal compressions. Its members differ only in a small bottleneck on frozen features and share a diffusion decoder self-distilled to one or a few steps. We train the same world model on each autoencoder in two domains, manipulation and navigation, with two environments each. CADRE forms most of the latency-quality frontier, especially with a moving camera. Our study also leads to four findings. At strong compression, a diffusion decoder is sharper than perceptual regression. In robot manipulation, temporal compression is more effective in the autoencoder than in the world model. Manipulation and navigation call for opposite compressions: temporal compression costs little when the camera is still, and spatial compression when it moves. Surprisingly, with a still camera, coarser spatial tokens hurt motion far more than image quality. Code, models and a live demo are at https://kyutai.org/blog/2026-10-21-cadre

### 3. [PhysiCalWorld: In-Context Physics Calibration for World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30125v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：world models, fine-tuning, dynamics via in-context prompting, instance-specific dynamics via in-context, physicalworld in-context physics calibration, physicalworld which calibrates action-conditioned
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30125v1>

**原始摘要**

Existing world models typically perceive a scene through current visual frame or a narrow history window. However, these observations may not reveal the physics properties that determine transition dynamics, such as an object's mass distribution, joint damping, or surface friction. Without calibrating these physics factors for world models, predictions tend to regress toward an empirical prior and induce severe drift in novel test scenes. To this end, we propose PhysiCalWorld, which calibrates action-conditioned world models to the instance-specific dynamics via in-context prompting, without fine-tuning or analytical physics modeling. Our method encodes an exploratory interaction clip collected within the test scene into a compact physics descriptor token, which grounds subsequent predictions in the scene's actual physical behavior. PhysiCalWorld is trained end-to-end with the latent prediction loss alone, without physics labels. At inference time, the descriptor token is extracted once and can be reused across rollouts with negligible compute overhead. We evaluate PhysiCalWorld across four environments covering diverse physics variations and show that it substantially improves rollout prediction accuracy and visual planning success rate. The learned descriptors also form a physically structured latent space and generalize beyond the physics ranges seen during training.

### 5. [Velocity Ambiguity Profiles: Time-Resolved Bayes-Risk Diagnostics for Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30107v1)

- 来源：arXiv

- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30107v1>

**原始摘要**

VAP turns an averaged flow-matching objective into a time-resolved diagnostic for locating ambiguity-limited and data-limited regions.

### 9. [Looped Uniform Diffusion Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30066v1)

Across several environments covering collaborative, competitive, and mixed-motive settings (Overcooked-AI, multiparty negotiation, Decrypto, Hanabi, and Collab Escape/Capture), we evaluate ToM2Vec's ability to support multi-agent interactions and collaboration, finding consistent improvements over the base model and a variety of competitive trained and prompted baselines. We show that latent reasoning is critical, with performance dropping when reasoning is expressed in text. Moreover, we show that ToM2Vec training generalizes to partner model families not seen at train-time, including a proprietary model (GPT-5.4). In our analysis, we provide evidence that the latent tokens encode a ToM, rather than task-specific information: ToM2Vec's tokens improve the agent's ability to directly answer questions about partner beliefs, desires, and next actions.

### 15. [FrontierCS 2: A Benchmark for Computer Science R&D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29932v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：agent, computer science, benchmark, point. for each patch, after the run. frontiercs, beyond these tasks frontiercs
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29932v1>

**原始摘要**

Can LLM agents make contributions to frontier computer science R&D? We introduce FrontierCS 2, a benchmark of 48 open-ended tasks. We take a paper and the code its authors released and delete the paper's contribution. The deleted code becomes the human reference that sets the bar. Earlier open-ended benchmarks give each agent a problem statement and grade one program after the run. FrontierCS 2 places the agent inside a research environment. The agent inherits a working repository and may submit a patch at any point. For each patch, a judge returns a development-suite score but keeps the evaluator, the held-out final suite and the reference private, and grades the final submission on that suite against the reference. The tasks span 13 domains, from machine-learning systems and high-performance computing to databases, security and robotics. Beyond these 48 tasks, FrontierCS 2 keeps a private set of more than 100 tasks, to be announced in the future. We evaluate 12 agents. The best one is Claude Opus 5.5, passing 58% of its runs. No run passes on 16 tasks. A higher score is not the same as a pass: most runs that beat the reference on the combined final score still fall short on some metric or workload. On a shared capability scale for agents and tasks, the median task is roughly as difficult as what the strongest agent can handle.

### 16. [Privacy Preference Controls for Agentic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29929v1)

**原始摘要**

In response to vast and widespread online tracking, regulators and technologists alike have worked to develop laws and tools that allow people to express their privacy preferences. These measures seek to offer users greater control over the sale and sharing of personal information, putting agency in their hands to protect their privacy. However, the web is undergoing a seismic shift, away from strictly \textit{user-driven} behavior towards \textit{agentic behavior}, where AI agents take autonomous actions on behalf of users. In this study, we ask: What should privacy preference controls look like in the agentic web and agentic systems more generally? To answer this question, we develop a taxonomy of enforceable privacy preferences for agentic systems. Our taxonomy is informed both by laws and empirical studies of existing privacy tools as well as an audit of 30 leading AI assistants and agent platforms. To explore how our taxonomy can be implemented, we design and develop eight open-source prototypes that mirror real-world agentic applications and demonstrate how major aspects of our taxonomy can be structurally integrated in agentic protocols, such as the Model Control Protocol and Agent2Agent protocol. Ultimately, our study seeks to provide the groundwork for ensuring end-user privacy and autonomy is not lost in the newly arriving agentic world.
