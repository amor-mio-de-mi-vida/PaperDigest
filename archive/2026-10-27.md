1. [Learning When to Commit: Schedule Distillation for Fast Diffusion Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35804v1)
   - 分类：Diffusion Language Models / Acceleration
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接对应扩散语言模型推理加速与并行解码调度，是生成式 AI 效率方向的高相关研究。
   - 痛点：扩散大语言模型虽能并行生成多个 token，但实际解码往往需要很多轮才能完成响应；现有加速方法主要按 token 可预测性决定提交顺序，却忽略了“揭示某个 token 是否能帮助其他位置更早可预测”这一全局收益。高可预测 token 未必提供有效上下文，而较早揭示低可预测 token 可能显著降低整体不确定性；因此缺少对提交调度本身的学习，会使并行采样在效率与质量之间难以兼得，也限制了 dLLM 在低延迟场景的部署。
   - 方法：dSpark 先构造能优先揭示“可降低其他位置不确定性”的 token 的 unmasking schedule，把提交顺序从局部置信度转为全局信息增益；随后将这些 schedule 蒸馏到扩散语言模型中，使模型在少量并行解码轮次内模仿高质量调度，并加入 KL 散度正则约束蒸馏前后分布，以在加速同时保留生成质量；该框架不改变基础并行采样接口，主要作为训练期调度蒸馏模块提升解码效率，并可推广到不同 dLLM 解码器。
   - 结果：在数学推理与代码生成任务上，dSpark 在 GSM8K 上相对 LLaDA-8B-Instruct 取得 10.12 倍加速，相对 Dream-7B-Instruct 取得 5.12 倍加速，同时任务性能优于多种基线；在代码生成等任务上也保持更高质量与更快并行采样，说明加速并非以明显牺牲正确率为代价。结果进一步表明，学习“何时提交 token”的调度策略比单纯减少去噪或并行步数更能兼顾扩散语言模型的效率与生成质量，并为不同 dLLM 解码器提供了可复用的训练期优化方案。

2. [OSWAM: An Effective World Action Model with One-Step Action Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35850v1)
   - 分类：Diffusion World Models / Embodied AI
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：面向扩散式世界动作模型的一步生成与实时部署，关联具身智能和自动驾驶中的生成式决策。
   - 痛点：直接策略型世界动作模型虽能避免显式未来视觉预测，但仍依赖数十步去噪才能获得有竞争力的动作质量，带来显著推理延迟，难以满足自动驾驶和具身控制的实时部署需求。简单减少去噪步数会把多步非线性去噪轨迹压缩成粗更新，导致动作质量明显下降；其核心矛盾在于随机噪声初始化缺少结构化动作先验，模型只能靠迭代去噪恢复连贯动作序列。
   - 方法：OSWAM 用 imagination tokens 初始化扩散过程，将物理先验和动作条件表征注入噪声起点，使单步去噪即可生成连贯动作；同时引入带未来条件信息的特权信息蒸馏，把未来感知知识迁移到动作生成中，增强一步生成的前瞻性。该设计把传统依赖多步迭代的直接策略 WAM 改造成一步动作生成模型，同时保留世界动作模型对动作条件的建模能力，并避免简单减步导致的粗更新问题。
   - 结果：实验表明 OSWAM 在自动驾驶和具身控制中取得与需要数十步的直接策略 WAM 及 Joint-WAM 相当甚至更好的结果，相对直接策略 WAM 基线约 3 倍加速，并在 NVIDIA RTX 5090 上支持编译后低至 40ms 的实时推理延迟；在多个任务设置中质量与速度同时改善。该结果验证了结构化动作先验初始化与未来条件蒸馏能在一步去噪下维持高质量动作生成，为实时世界动作模型部署提供了可行路径。

3. [Triage: Skip, Skim, or Refine Attention Tiles in Video Diffusion Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35706v1)
   - 分类：Video Diffusion Transformers / Efficient Attention
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：针对视频扩散 Transformer 注意力瓶颈，属于生成式视频模型的高效推理与系统优化。
   - 痛点：视频扩散 Transformer 的注意力需处理数万视觉 token，是推理的主要瓶颈；现有稀疏化、低精度或二者结合的方法对所有保留 tile 使用同一精度，忽略了不同 tile 的近似误差差异巨大。许多 tile 可以低误差跳过，一些用低精度已足够，少数即便低精度仍残留显著误差；因此缺少一种按 tile 预测误差并动态分配“跳过、低精度、精修”预算的机制。
   - 方法：Triage 是免训练注意力方法，为每个 tile 分配跳过、NVFP4 低精度略读、NVFP4 残差修正精修三个近似层级；轻量路由器根据块统计预测各层级输出误差，仅当误差下降足以抵偿额外计算时才把 tile 提升到更精确层级，并由单一价格参数控制精度与算力权衡。三个层级由融合 kernel 在单次前向中于 RTX Blackwell 的 FP4 张量核上执行，避免额外调度开销。
   - 结果：在 Wan2.2、HunyuanVideo 和 LTX-2.5 上，Triage 相比稠密 FlashAttention 最高加速 2.32 倍，并在五种设置中均快于所有基线，同时 PSNR 比最高 PSNR 基线高 0.3 到 3.0 dB；端到端生成质量和速度均获改善。结果说明按 tile 误差分层近似能在视频扩散 DiT 上同时提升推理速度与生成保真度，且作为免训练方法可直接部署到现有模型。

**主要痛点**
- 扩散/生成模型在视频、语言和具身控制中的推理延迟、注意力开销与多步去噪成本仍是主要瓶颈。
- 可控 3D/4D 生成、多视角一致性、评测和理论理解仍不充分。

**方法趋势**
- 调度蒸馏、一步扩散、按 tile 分层近似等效率优化。
- 视频扩散先验、潜在 4D 表示、几何条件与流匹配等生成建模。

**结果信号**
- 多项工作取得 2 到 10 倍级加速并保持或提升质量。
- 视频/3D 生成一致性和基准评测得到推进，但复杂任务仍未解决。

1. [Triage: Skip, Skim, or Refine Attention Tiles in Video Diffusion Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35706v1)
   - 分类：Efficient Inference / Video Diffusion Transformer
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接面向视频扩散 Transformer 的注意力推理加速，属于高效推理核心方向。
   - 痛点：视频扩散 Transformer 的注意力需要处理数万视觉 token，成为端到端推理的主要开销；现有稀疏化、低精度或两者结合的方法对所有保留 tile 采用统一精度，忽略了不同 tile 的近似误差差异，导致要么误跳过关键 tile、要么在低误差 tile 上浪费算力。
   - 方法：Triage 是一种免训练的三级 tile 注意力：用块统计量训练轻量路由器预测每个 tile 在 skip、NVFP4 skim、NVFP4 残差 refine 三档下的误差，仅当误差下降足以抵消额外计算时升级档位；融合 kernel 在 RTX Blackwell FP4 tensor core 上单遍执行三档。

2. [Learning When to Commit: Schedule Distillation for Fast Diffusion Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35804v1)
   - 分类：Efficient Inference / Diffusion Language Models
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接研究扩散语言模型的解码轮次压缩，属于生成式模型高效推理。
   - 痛点：扩散大语言模型可并行生成多个 token，但通常需要很多解码轮次；现有加速方法只关注 token 是否容易预测，忽略了“提交某个 token 会如何帮助其他位置变得更可预测”，因此高可预测 token 可能信息量低，而揭示较难 token 反而能更早降低整体不确定性。
   - 方法：dSpark 提出 schedule distillation 框架：先构造优先揭示“能让他处更易预测”的 token 的 unmasking schedule，再把这些 schedule 蒸馏进模型以加速并行采样，并用 KL 正则约束生成质量；模型学习的是何时提交 token 的策略。
   - 结果：在 GSM8K 上，dSpark 对 LLaDA-8B-Instruct 和 Dream-7B-Instruct 分别实现 10.12 倍和 5.12 倍加速，同时保持更优任务性能；在数学推理与代码生成任务上验证了调度蒸馏能减少解码轮次且不牺牲质量。

1. [Learning Force-Reactive Humanoid Loco-Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35862v1)
   - 分类：Humanoid Loco-Manipulation / Force-Reactive Diffusion Policy
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接对应具身智能中的力反应式人形 loco-manipulation，提供从无机器人演示到真实接触任务的完整学习与执行闭环。
   - 痛点：许多日常操作需要同时控制运动与接触力，而人形机器人必须在双手和全身之间协调力，单靠运动学轨迹无法指定力；现有仅位置策略在接触丰富任务中容易失败，且力跟踪与平衡控制强耦合，使真实机器人上的力反应式 loco-manipulation 仍缺乏可扩展、可复现的学习框架，演示采集、力传感接口与全身执行器协调也难以统一。
   - 方法：FLORE 用两个带力传感的 UMI 风格手持夹爪和可穿戴追踪器采集无机器人演示，获得手部运动、接触力旋量与身体运动；训练力反应式扩散策略，以视觉、本体感知和测量力旋量为输入，同时预测动作与每只手力命令；执行端采用带力跟踪的混合柔顺全身控制器，利用真实 F/T 传感器力误差积分在指令方向精确跟踪力、其余方向保持柔顺，并维持全身平衡。
   - 结果：仿真中控制器能在不同幅值和方向的手部力命令下保持全身运动精度；真实机器人三项接触丰富任务中，力反应式策略滚动成功率达 80%，而用同一批演示训练、同一控制器执行的位置策略仅 26.7%；这表明显式力命令与力跟踪能显著提升接触任务鲁棒性，也验证了从无机器人演示到真实人形力反应式 loco-manipulation 的可行路径。

2. [Differentiable Contact Dynamics for Legged Locomotion: Policy Learning and Contact-Implicit Trajectory Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35852v1)
   - 分类：Differentiable Contact Dynamics / Legged Locomotion
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：面向腿足机器人接触丰富运动，连接可微分物理、策略学习与接触隐式轨迹优化，是 embodied RL 与机器人控制的重要交叉。
   - 痛点：腿足运动中的摩擦接触是分段光滑且接触模式会切换，精确接触灵敏度只在当前模式内有效，无法为相邻接触模式提供一阶信息；这使基于梯度的策略学习与接触隐式轨迹优化容易卡在局部解，硬接触前向求解虽能保持 rollout 可行，但反向梯度缺失，而 Signorini 松弛又会改变接触物理，限制接触丰富动作生成与 sim-to-real 迁移。
   - 方法：论文提出一种只替换反向传播的平滑解析梯度：保持硬接触前向求解与实际 rollout 可行，对 de Saxcé 修正的 Coulomb 摩擦二阶锥互补问题做中心路径松弛，平滑分离、夹紧和滑动模式；用从硬接触解初始化的 Newton 迭代在中心路径点评估松弛灵敏度，使灵敏度跨接触模式连续，并在松弛消失时收敛到精确灵敏度。
   - 结果：用 10 万样本、桌面 PC 不到 2 分钟训练，所提梯度学到的策略无需微调即可迁移到硬件；机器人在 trot 策略下能承受推挤、穿越碎石和湿滑地面；在轨迹优化中，该梯度能找到精确梯度和 Signorini 松弛梯度遗漏的接触丰富解，说明平滑接触梯度同时改善策略学习与接触隐式规划。

3. [Pretraining Knows What, Simulation Teaches How: Compositional Generalization in a Sim-Trained Robot Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35817v1)
   - 分类：Robot Foundation Agents / Sim-to-Real Compositional Generalization
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：面向无真实机器人数据的机器人基础智能体，验证仿真训练与工具调用在组合泛化和跨本体迁移上的潜力。
   - 痛点：机器人策略既要理解场景中任务要求什么，又要知道如何移动机器人完成任务；现有 VLA 常依赖大规模真实机器人数据，跨新物体、新目标、新策略和新语言的组合泛化不足，而前沿多模态大模型虽推理强却成本高、速度慢，难以在真实机器人上频繁闭环调用，仿真训练到真实部署之间也缺乏可复用的工具接口。
   - 方法：论文提出 Zero-real Agent Policy（ZAP）：用现成 27B 视觉语言模型作为推理核心，通过简单工具 harness 控制机器人；先用预训练模型提供世界理解，再在简单仿真任务上做监督微调和 GRPO，教会模型如何移动机器人；训练不使用任何真实机器人数据，并以可替换工具接口连接感知、规划与低层动作。
   - 结果：ZAP 在真实任务上达到 96.7% 成功率，匹配 GPT-6 Astra 的 harness 表现，显著优于 MolmoAct2 VLA 的 33.3%，同时比 GPT-6 Astra 快 3 倍、便宜 17 倍；还能泛化到训练中未出现的新物体、目标、策略和语言，如试管架插管、空中换手、井字棋画 X 和国际象棋；在未见单臂 Franka 上 RoboLab-120 达 55.9%，领先最强 VLA/WAM 基线 4.9 个百分点。

**主要痛点**
- 具身智能中接触丰富操作仍缺乏可扩展的力/接触建模与真实部署闭环
- 长程机器人/智能体任务信用分配与奖励作弊仍是强化学习关键瓶颈
- 仿真到真实的接触、泛化与评测仍缺少统一框架

**方法趋势**
- 力反应式扩散策略与柔顺全身控制
- 可微分接触/中心路径平滑梯度
- 仿真训练+GRPO 的机器人基础智能体
- 主动 critic 学习与错误靶向 RL
- 多目标 RL 的路由集成与反事实信用分配

**结果信号**
- 真实人形接触任务成功率从 26.7% 提升到 80%
- 平滑接触梯度实现零微调 sim-to-real
- ZAP 真实任务 96.7% 并泛化到未见平台
- GRPO 审计 LB 将作弊压到近零
- 多目标 RL 路由集成平均提升 4.3%

1. [FOCUS: Active Critic Learning for Long-Horizon Language Model Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35801v1)
   - 分类：Long-Horizon Agent RL / Credit Assignment
   - 研究价值：值得精读

### 1. [VibeVideoBench:Evaluating Multimodal Agents on Code-Driven Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35864v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：code-driven video generation., agent, benchmark, multimodal, diffusion, video generation. its self-contained
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35864v1>

**原始摘要**

Diffusion video models produce striking clips, but they cannot reliably render an exact formula, a correct chart, a ruleconsistent game or narration synchronized to on-screen events. A different route is emerging: multimodal agents that write code to render every frame and synthesize every sound. We introduce VibeVideoBench, a benchmark for evaluating agents on code-driven video generation. Its 239 self-contained tasks in English and Chinese span 12 categories, from data stories and music-driven pieces to classroom lessons and paper explainers; each asks an agent, running in its native harness inside a sandbox, to deliver a finished MP4 with sound within one hour. Evaluation is hierarchical and evidence-based: program checks of the delivery spec, about 13k task-specific rubric checks traced to the requirements they enforce, and five quality dimensions anchored per task, judged by three VLM judge panels and validated with blind human preferences. Across nine frontier agent systems and 2,151 scored rollouts, the benchmark is far from solved: GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 lead clearly, yet even the best system fails a core requirement on about a quarter of tasks. Whether a video arrives at all matters as much as its quality, and three quarters of the lost videos are final renders started too late, run too slowly or never started. The judges agree with each other and with human preferences on system rankings, but only moderately on individual videos.

### 2. [Patient-specific rubric-based evaluation of large language models across longitudinal cancer care](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35847v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：benchmark, general-purpose and medical LLMS, large language models, reasoning, patient-phase units across SIX, CARE phases and patient-specific
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35847v1>

**原始摘要**

Large language models (LLMs) show promise in specialty medicine, yet existing evaluations largely focus on isolated clinical encounters rather than tracking how clinical responsibilities evolve across a longitudinal care pathway. Here we introduce NPC-RubricBench, a phase-aware benchmark for evaluating multistage clinical reasoning across the cancer care continuum, using nasopharyngeal carcinoma as a defined disease context. Curated from longitudinal records of 405 patients across two cancer centers, the benchmark comprises 941 patient-phase units across six sequential care phases and 17,688 patient-specific atomic criteria spanning five evaluation dimensions, with each assessment strictly restricted to decision-time clinical evidence. Evaluating 30 general-purpose and medical LLMs revealed that strong diagnostic performance does not carry over to downstream care. We evaluated 30 general-purpose and medical LLMs, showing that high diagnostic performance did not translate into improved downstream care. Instead, the dominant failure modes reversed across phases. Explicit decision errors dominated anatomical staging at 48.2%, whereas decision omissions dominated initial work-up and post-treatment follow-up at 33.4% and 30.4%, respectively. In the evaluated model set, medically adapted models showed higher omission rates than general-purpose models. Moreover, varying query granularity exposes a critical specification trade-off: broad open-ended requests degrade Decision Quality across all 30 models, while more detailed, checklist-like prompts raise Decision Quality but reduce Evidence Faithfulness and Safety Adherence. Furthermore, NPC-RubricBench demonstrates that static diagnostic benchmarks are insufficient to ensure consistent clinical reliability over time, highlighting the importance of phase-adaptive safeguards in multistage medical AI.

### 3. [ACARE: Agent-Context-Aware Reranker for Iterative Search](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35840v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：agent, BASE reranker, acare improves, reasoning, search effort. these benefits, reranker for iterative search.
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35840v1>

**原始摘要**

In agentic search, a document can match an agent-generated sub-query without helping answer the user's original question. Conventional rerankers score documents against the sub-query alone, overlooking the broader task and the agent's reasoning. We introduce the Agent-Context-Aware Reranker (ACARe), a compact 4B LLM trained to rerank documents using this context when available. Across multiple benchmarks and agent families, ACARe improves retrieval quality over its base reranker while reducing search effort. These benefits extend to answer generation: ACARe improves accuracy and reduces searches over no reranking, while matching or exceeding the base reranker's mean accuracy with more precise evidence. It also helps smaller agents narrow the retrieval-quality gap to larger agents, showing how better retrieval tools can make smaller agents more effective. The model, training data, and code will be released soon.

### 4. [I-HITL: Inalienable Human-in-the-Loop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35835v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：i-hitl protocol, agent, i-hitl using the concept, prototype i-hitl system called, i-hitl system called countersign, i-hitl inalienable human-in-the-loop. human
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35835v1>

**原始摘要**

Human oversight is a pillar of AI safety. It is often exercised by a human in the loop (HITL) who approves critical actions taken by an AI agent. AI agents' increasing ability to bypass guardrails, though, raises the question of how to enforce HITL robustly. We introduce the concept of inalienable human-in-the-loop (I-HITL). An I-HITL protocol ensures that an agent can initiate an action only with explicit human approval, even when the human interacts with a system (e.g., a local agentic harness) compromised by the agent. A strong I-HITL protocol additionally ensures such approval even when an adversarial human attempts to delegate approvals to an agent. To motivate I-HITL, we report on experiments with a popular agentic wallet platform in which raising an agent's spending cap requires HITL approval via emailed one-time passcodes (OTPs). Given access to the user's email and a task blocked by the cap, we find that ten different frontier models will often autonomously raise the cap, harvesting OTPs to bypass a HITL guardrail and surfacing other vulnerabilities. We formally define I-HITL using the concept of communication topologies and prove necessary and sufficient conditions for its realization. We show that an I-HITL protocol requires a trusted entity - a trusted computing device (or equivalent) - and characterize which such devices can and cannot realize an I-HITL protocol. We present a prototype I-HITL system called Countersign that can plug into and strengthen existing HITL systems using off-the-shelf consumer mobile devices.

### 5. [Profiling a Frontier Agent with EBench: From Direct Control to Harness with Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35832v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：agent, direct control, harness with policy, multimodal, vision-language, large language models
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35832v1>

**原始摘要**

Frontier multimodal large language models (MLLMs) can control robots without robot-specific training, but how their capabilities compare and interact with post-trained vision-language-action (VLA) and world action model (WAM) policies across manipulation demands remains to be thoroughly evaluated. In this report, we evaluate GPT-6-Astra on EBench as the core of an agent that controls a robot through an agent harness under the same visual and proprioceptive observation protocol as post-trained policies. We study two agent configurations: direct control, in which the agent generates waypoint commands directly, and harness with policy, in which it can also invoke a post-trained VLA or WAM policy for action execution. Under direct control, the agent achieves competitive overall performance relative to post-trained policies. It leads on short-horizon tasks and exhibits the least performance variation across generalization dimensions, whereas it underperforms post-trained policies on long-horizon tasks, tabletop manipulation, and high-precision contact-rich tasks. In the harness with policy, invoking a post-trained VLA or WAM combines the complementary strengths of both components, raising overall task success to the level of the strongest compared post-trained policies while substantially reducing token cost. However, performance remains below the per-task upper bound of the standalone components due to suboptimal policy invocation, fixed-instruction execution without subgoals, and grasp disruption when the agent resumes control. Overall, our study characterizes the capability boundary of a frontier MLLM agent and suggests directions for improving future agent harness design. ### 6. [Reva: Real-Time Vulnerability Detection through Contextual Probing of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35829v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：REVA real-time vulnerability, agent, reasoning, large language models, held-out TEST SET REVA, TOO LONG for real-time
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35829v1>

**原始摘要**

A vulnerability costs more to fix the later it is discovered. As coding agents generate code faster than developers can review it, software needs a first line of defense screening each new function in real time. LLM agents have recently become capable of finding real-world vulnerabilities by working as a security expert would: they run analysis tools, explore the surrounding code, test hypotheses and weigh the evidence before forming their own judgement. This reasoning, however, spans many model calls, so that each verdict takes minutes of compute, far too long for real-time screening. Faster detectors, which fine-tune deep learning models or prompt an LLM for a direct verdict, are less accurate: they judge a function without the context an agent gathers, and an LLM's generated verdict is unreliable even when given some of it. We present REVA (REal-time Vulnerability Analysis), an AI vulnerability detector that brings the security context and code understanding components behind agentic detectors into a single forward pass of an LLM. For each new function, REVA gives the LLM a pre-computed threat model of its module, lightweight static analysis findings and call-graph information. REVA leverages a trained probe to read the LLM's internal representations of the new function in this context. On PrimeVul's held-out test set, REVA on GLM-5.3-Flash reaches 60.0% accuracy in about one second per function. This matches VulTriage (59.2%), the state-of-the-art agentic detector, run on the same LLM, while being over 250 times faster and less than a tenth as costly. On the same prompt, the probe is 14% to 17% more accurate than the LLM's own direct verdict. Across three open LLMs, REVA is at least as accurate as VulTriage, and about 10% more accurate on two of them. An LLM's internal representations thus encode much of what agentic detectors obtain through reasoning.

### 7. [Back to Basics: A Skill-Centric Approach to Generalist Web Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35823v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：agent, fine-tuning, autonomously generates verified skill-centric, approach improves task success, perform. this approach improves, WEB comprising interconnected websites
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35823v1>

**原始摘要**

Browser agents do not just need more practice. They need to practice the right things. We introduce MiniWeb, a realistic miniature of the web comprising 65 interconnected websites across 15 domains, where agents navigate, search, and transact as people do. Its 369 human-annotated tasks cover everyday workflows and label 37 reusable interaction primitives grouped into 7 skill families, allowing us to diagnose which skills fail rather than only whether a task succeeds. Benchmarking ten open-weight agents, we find that drag & gesture is consistently the weakest family, with no agent solving more than 24% of its instances. To address this gap, we introduce MiniWebGen, a framework that autonomously generates verified, skill-centric training data from MiniWeb. Fine-tuning Qwen3.5-4B on just 3.1K verified trajectories improves single-primitive success by 24.9pp across 13 unseen websites, with gains across every skill family. However, these trajectories teach short-horizon interactions, which do not directly translate to full-task success. We build on this result with MiniWebAgent, which pairs the trained executor with a planner that decomposes full tasks into the skill-level steps it learned to perform. This approach improves task success by 19.4pp points on unseen MiniWeb sites and transfers to external benchmarks, with gains of 9.3pp on WebArena-Lite, 16.1pp on Online-Mind2Web, and 20.0pp on WebVoyager, without training on their trajectories. We provide a simple recipe for compact browser agents: practice reusable skills directly, then compose them to solve unfamiliar tasks.

### 8. [FOCUS: Active Critic Learning for Long-Horizon Language Model Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35801v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：reinforcement learning, reasoning, critic learning for long-horizon, learning for long-horizon language, critic. focus supports long-horizon, focus supports long-horizon agentic
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35801v1>

**原始摘要**

Sparse outcome rewards make it difficult for long-horizon language-model agents to assign credit to earlier actions. Learned critics provide value estimates for intermediate states, but these estimates can be inaccurate, while estimating intermediate values with repetitive continuation samples from points within a trajectory incurs substantial costs. To mitigate these issues, we introduce FOCUS, an RL method that uses selective branching to help the critic network learn its value estimator. The rollout controller facilitates active learning for the critic by sampling continuations from states that are expected to most reduce the critic's estimation error. These refined value estimates then score the segment between consecutive states with a TD($λ$) advantage, while the sampled evidence supplies training targets for the critic. FOCUS supports long-horizon agentic reinforcement learning through a modular context-management interface that decouples context management from rollout allocation. Our experiments show that using compaction-induced segment boundaries for value estimation and credit assignment, FOCUS outperforms GRPO and VinePPO with matched per-step generation budgets on legal reasoning, deep search, and code localization benchmarks, improving held-out accuracy at Qwen3.5-9B scale by 7.2 points over the strongest baseline on BrowseComp-Plus (55.4 vs. 48.2) and by 4.1 points on DeonticBench (56.5 vs. 52.4).

### 9. [A Unified Arena for LLM Agents across Diverse Eurogame](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35773v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：LLM agents, unified arena, three-player matches across TEN, goals and other agents, matches across TEN games., other agents choices. YET
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35773v1>

**原始摘要**

Interactive environments offer a practical way to evaluate large language model (LLM) agents, and competitive games extend this evaluation to decisions shaped by competing goals and other agents' choices. Yet existing evaluations confined to individual games or execution frameworks leave unclear which competitive advantages persist across settings. We introduce DECE (Diverse Eurogame Competitive Environments), a unified arena spanning ten Eurogames with shared interfaces and evaluation protocols. These games combine lasting strategic commitments, competition over shared resources, and multiple routes to victory. Our primary study evaluates 12 model-harness configurations, spanning nine models and four execution harnesses, in 480 three-player matches across ten games. The main and supplementary studies comprise 1,032 completed matches in total. These comparisons reveal broad cross-game ranking agreement alongside game-specific strengths. Matched studies show that the same model can perform differently across execution harnesses, adding another source of variation to these profiles. These performance advantages do not consistently translate into better token or cost efficiency, making resource use an additional consideration when comparing configurations. Together, the findings support evaluating complete agent configurations by both their competitive performance across games and the resources needed to achieve it.

### 10. [Free to Look, Free to Reason: Simple LLM Harnesses Outdiagnose Hand-Crafted Root Cause Analysis Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35767v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：agent, ROOT cause analysis, simple harnesses, reasoning, outdiagnose hand-crafted ROOT cause, LLM harnesses outdiagnose hand-crafted
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35767v1>

**原始摘要**

Root cause analysis (RCA) of microservice failures has attracted many LLM agents whose investigation is hand-crafted, through standard operating procedures, fixed stages and role agents. Each is evaluated on its own data against few baselines, so it is unknown whether this design beats letting a capable model investigate on its own. We compare three simple harnesses that prescribe none of the model's calls (one call over an evidence package, a SQL agent and the Codex coding agent) with five published LLM RCA agents and with statistical and causal methods on RCAtlas, 90 validated cases from eight public datasets, across six models and three reasoning efforts. The simple harnesses are the most accurate (PR@1 65-77% on three mid-tier models), ahead of the published agents (16-53%) and the non-LLM methods (6-39%), on every model on which both families were run. The published agents fix from a tenth to all of their model calls in the program, and a case study shows how these prescriptions skip the decisive evidence. A stronger model mainly improves reliability, a multi-turn harness mainly improves aptitude, and more reasoning effort changes accuracy by a few points at most while multiplying the time by up to seven. Among runs more accurate than the best non-LLM method, every Pareto-optimal run in cost or time uses a simple harness.

### 11. [Preference-Guided Cooperation in Multi-Objective Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35736v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：reinforcement learning, multi-objective multi-agent reinforcement, allowing the multi-objective structure, objective allowing the multi-objective, multi-agent cooperation and multi-objective, cooperation and multi-objective optimisation
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35736v1>

**原始摘要**

Multi-objective multi-agent reinforcement learning (MOMARL) addresses agent coordination and objective trade-offs simultaneously while optimising overall system performance. Existing work tends to treat multi-agent cooperation and multi-objective optimisation as largely independent components of a learning problem, which amounts to an exorbitant complexity except in trivial cases. We study here whether the structure provided by multiple objectives can itself be exploited to facilitate cooperation, rather than being treated as an additional optimisation challenge. We formulate MOMARL at both the team and individual levels and introduce objective-wise counterfactual credit assignment to evaluate each agent's contribution across the team objectives. We propose Meta-Gradient Informed Multi-Objective Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MIRL), with static (MIRL-S) and dynamic (MIRL-D) variants. By incorporating meta-gradient information, MIRL enables agents to learn how to prioritise objectives individually while collectively optimising the system-level objective, allowing the multi-objective structure to directly guide cooperation. Experiments on a multi-objective multiple particle environment show that our methods improve Pareto-front quality over baselines, with the dynamic variant achieving the strongest performance.

### 12. [From Self-Confidence to Statistical Accuracy: Scaling Human-in-the-Loop Evaluation with Direct and LLM-Assisted Auditing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35698v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：allocation. for judge-assisted evaluation, large language models, multimodal, direct and llm-assisted auditing., self-confidence can still support, through human-label and total-cost
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35698v1>

**原始摘要**

For large language models (LLMs) and multimodal large language models (MLLMs), model-reported self-confidence is often miscalibrated, yet it may still be useful for efficient evaluation. At the same time, LLM-as-a-judge offers a scalable alternative to direct human auditing, but its practical benefit depends on calibration quality and inference cost. This paper develops a unified statistical framework for both evaluation routes under alternative sampling strategies. For direct auditing, confidence-stratified sampling is used to recover population accuracy from partially verified outputs without requiring calibrated confidence. Finite-sample uncertainty is characterized, and the analysis shows how highly uneven confidence strata affect audit efficiency and label allocation. For judge-assisted evaluation, the judge is modeled through sensitivity and false-positive rate, yielding an error-corrected estimator with calibration uncertainty. The two routes are then compared through human-label and total-cost efficiency boundaries. Experiments on MMLU-Pro and MMMU-Pro show that miscalibrated self-confidence can still support statistically valid stratified auditing. Under highly imbalanced confidence distributions, proportional allocation substantially reduces estimation error relative to equal-per-stratum allocation. For judge-assisted evaluation, only a small subset of target--verifier pairs satisfies the idealized human-label efficiency condition, and automated inference costs can further limit potential savings. These findings clarify the statistical and economic conditions under which confidence-guided auditing and automated verification are useful.

### 13. [EvoRouter: Cost-Efficient Multi-Agent Routing via Insight-Guided On-Policy Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35690v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：on-policy distillation., multi-agent routing, insight-guided on-policy, reinforcement learning, large language models, scale with multi-agent systems
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35690v1>

**原始摘要**

Serving web queries at scale with multi-agent systems requires jointly configuring large language models, collaboration modes, agent counts, interaction rounds, and skills. However, the resulting configuration space grows combinatorially, while sparse execution rewards provide limited fine-grained supervision for interdependent planning decisions. We propose EvoRouter, a self-evolving multi-agent routing framework driven by insight-guided on-policy distillation. A dedicated expert periodically synthesizes performance-cost feedback from configuration trajectories across tasks into reusable conditional routing insights about capability requirements, returns from resource scaling, and interactions among configuration choices. A privileged teacher combines these evolving insights with current execution feedback to provide dense token-level supervision for the router's own planning trajectories through on-policy distillation. Cost-aware reinforcement learning jointly optimizes task performance and computational cost. Together, these training signals internalize execution insights into the routing policy. The updated policy generates new trajectories that support subsequent insight extraction, forming an execution-driven self-improvement loop. We evaluate EvoRouter on 19 in-domain datasets spanning five domains and two out-of-domain benchmarks. EvoRouter achieves an average in-domain accuracy of 68.69%, outperforming the strongest routing baseline by 7.23 percentage points while reducing inference compute cost by 50.95%. Our code is open-sourced at https://github.com/CenYangtze/EvoRouter.

### 14. [Evaluating DeepSeek V4.1 Flash-1017 for loss of control and harmful manipulation risks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35671v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：v4.1, flash-1017, control and harmful, attention, reasoning, gpt-5.6 SOL and gemini
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35671v1>

**原始摘要**

As frontier language models grow increasingly capable and autonomous, independent third-party evaluations are critical for characterizing their systemic safety risks. While closed-source releases are routinely accompanied by comprehensive system cards, open-weight models receive less scrutiny regarding frontier risks. To address this gap, we present an independent safety evaluation of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash-1017, the latest version of V4.1 Flash that departs from V4 with a new architecture, scaled-up training, and strong agentic capabilities. Drawing on the EU General-Purpose AI Code of Practice, we evaluate the model across 34 benchmarks, including 5 in-house suites, spanning evaluation integrity, loss of control, and harmful manipulation. Conforming to the AEF-1 standard, we compare V4.1 Flash-1017 against V4.1 Flash, earlier DeepSeek V4 models, and leading closed-source systems (GPT-6 Astra, GPT-5.6 Sol, and Gemini 3.8 Flash). Overall, V4.1 Flash-1017 shows substantial safety improvements over the V4 generation and approaches or matches leading closed-source baselines on several dimensions, while still exhibiting notable behaviors that warrant continued attention. Building on our findings, we highlight three key observations and recommendations. First, because safety can shift and even regress across minor updates, developers should integrate safety evaluation into incremental releases. Second, as final-turn outputs and end-state checks alone cannot reliably detect loss-of-control behaviors, deployments require process-level observability across both reasoning traces and action logs. Third, because public benchmarks elicit strong evaluation awareness and face data contamination risks, developing private benchmarks is essential for trustworthy frontier evaluation.
