1. [Know, then Draw: A New Learning Paradigm for Visual Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37626v1)
   - 分类：Visual Generation / AR-Diffusion Hybrid
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接对应生成式视觉模型中语义预训练与扩散渲染的结合，是团队关注的高效高保真图像生成与scaling规律方向。
   - 痛点：视觉生成同时要求高层语义理解与细粒度像素保真，但现有方法常把语义学习与渲染混在一起：从零训练像素扩散成本高，纯自回归生成虽语义强却在最终画质上不一定占优，且缺少关于预训练目标、tokenizer表征与计算最优scaling的系统结论，导致有限算力下如何构建强文本到图像模型仍不清晰；现有范式也很难在训练效率、语义正确性和细节保真之间给出可扩展折中，评测常只报单一分数而难以判断语义与渲染各自贡献。
   - 方法：提出“Know, then Draw”两阶段框架：先用自回归在纯语义token上预训练以获取高层知识，再用扩散后训练负责像素级高保真渲染；通过受控实验比较目标函数与表征，选择AR作为更强预训练目标，并设计仅受语言监督、不需要图像解码器和重建损失的语义tokenizer；进一步拟合知识获取与渲染的计算最优scaling law，用其指导3B模型的数据与算力分配，并分析AR-only质量与最终渲染性能的非相关性。
   - 结果：在仅6600 H100 GPU-hours训练下，3B文本到图像模型取得GenEval 86和DPG-Bench 87；分析发现AR-only生成质量与最终渲染性能不必相关，语义质量比像素先验更关键；与从零训练的像素扩散相比，AR预训练降低拟合的渐近渲染损失，并建立可外推的scaling规律，验证语义预训练加扩散后训练在效率与质量上的双重优势。

2. [ReferStream: Interactive Subject Injection for Streaming Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37566v1)
   - 分类：Video Generation / Training-Free Subject Injection
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：面向流式视频扩散的可控主体注入与训练-free编辑，关联视频生成、交互式创作和一致性评测。
   - 痛点：流式视频生成中按事件动态注入新主体是交互式创作的关键，但现有方法要么将参考独立编码、缺少与当前场景的上下文关联，导致新主体融合生硬；要么把参考特征长期写入全局缓存，干扰已建立主体与场景的一致性；同时缺少带定时参考到达、演化指令和主体存在标注的评测基准，难以同时衡量插入保真与历史保持，训练-free场景下尤其困难。
   - 方法：提出无需额外训练的ReferStream框架：用Joint Latent把参考latent放入最新场景latent的目标区域，使参考特征在上下文中提取；再用Cache Restoration以这些特征条件化注入chunk，并在生成后恢复被覆盖的全局缓存条目、保留新生成历史，从而不重训生成器也不修改已输出帧；同时构建ReferStream Bench，包含定时参考到达、演化指令以及InShot/MultiShot的主体存在标注。
   - 结果：在ReferStream Bench上按事件日程评估，ReferStream在文本遵循、主体相似度和数量准确率上超过基线，包括提前给出全部参考的离线方法；它能在注入新主体的同时更好保持既有主体与场景，并维持视频质量与指令遵循；基准覆盖InShot与MultiShot生成，为流式交互式视频生成提供了可复现的评测协议，且方法无需训练即可部署。

3. [GroundControl: Satellite-Conditioned Multi-View Diffusion for Reconstruction-Free Novel View Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37545v1)
   - 分类：Multi-View Diffusion / Novel View Synthesis
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：把卫星全局条件注入多视图扩散，关联可控新视角合成、户外场景生成与外推一致性。
   - 痛点：稀疏街景新视角合成在输入视角之外外推时，多视图或视频扩散模型容易凭先验幻觉出不真实的空间布局，尤其当目标视角与源视角重叠很小时；现有方法缺少外部全局布局约束，卫星图像虽提供俯视结构却未被有效用于条件生成；依赖3D重建的流程还增加成本并对位姿与覆盖敏感，因此需要一种reconstruction-free、能保持预训练扩散先验并遵循场景布局的条件生成方案。
   - 方法：提出卫星条件多视图扩散模型GroundControl，将卫星图像与带位姿街景输入通过cross-attention注入预训练多视图扩散先验，在生成新视角时同时遵循街景外观与卫星场景布局；模型不做3D场景重建，仅依赖带位姿街景和卫星视图，保留预训练扩散先验以生成无缝、真实的新视角；该条件机制可直接复用现有扩散骨干，避免从头学习户外场景几何，并支持少量源视图输入。
   - 结果：在户外数据集上，GroundControl优于仅卫星条件或仅街景条件的基线，特别在目标视角与源视角重叠很少的困难外推场景中提升明显；生成结果更符合卫星可见的场景布局并减少不合理空间幻觉，同时保持街景外观真实感；方法证明无需3D重建、仅用卫星全局条件即可增强多视图扩散的外推鲁棒性，项目页已公开，具备重建-free户外生成潜力。

**主要痛点**

1. [MuonGS: Pretraining LLMs with Relaxed Weight-Norm Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37647v1)
   - 分类：LLM Pretraining / Optimizer
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接面向大模型预训练优化器与权重范数控制，是高效训练的核心问题。
   - 痛点：现有权重衰减与显式权重范数约束虽能提升预训练稳定性，但缺乏统一解释，Muon 的原生更新在有效学习率累积上仅达对数级，可能限制探索并拖慢长程训练；MuonW/MuonH 又依赖固定范数或较强约束。如何在不放任范数漂移的前提下获得更快、更持久的有效学习率累积，并给出可证明的累积速率与收敛保证，是预训练优化器的关键痛点。
   - 方法：论文以累计有效学习率 C_T=Ση_t/||W_t||_F 统一分析 Muon 训练轨迹，指出有界更新方向下范数位移与 C_T 的关系，并提出 MuonGS：用 Gram-Schmidt 投影移除每次 Muon 更新的径向分量，但不固定权重范数，从而允许范数缓慢增长并持续累积有效学习率；该方法不额外引入显式约束，保留了 Muon 的几何更新特性，同时给出 O(T^{-1/2}) 的平稳性保证。
   - 结果：在 160M、400M 稠密模型和 135M 激活/1B 总参数 MoE 上，MuonGS 在长训练周期取得比 MuonH 更低的验证困惑度，稠密模型在匹配验证损失下估计约 15% 的 token 等效加速；理论还证明其 C_T=Ω(√T)，说明后期训练仍能维持有效学习率累积。这为放宽范数控制、提升预训练效率提供了可证明路径，也表明范数不必被硬性固定。

2. [Multi-State Linear Attention with Generalized Second-Order Routing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37645v1)
   - 分类：Long-Context Linear Attention / Efficient Inference
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接改进多状态线性注意力的长上下文路由，属于高效推理与长上下文建模交叉点。
   - 痛点：多状态线性注意力把长上下文切段压缩到独立记忆状态，但聚合时只用查询与各段键的均值对齐，忽略段内对齐分布与方差，导致段级相关性建模粗糙，长上下文检索和问答中容易漏掉关键证据；现有方法在内存受限时尤其难以兼顾精度与效率。如何在几乎不增加状态开销的前提下做内容感知路由，是 MSLA 的核心痛点，也是线性注意力走向长上下文部署的关键障碍。
   - 方法：MERLIN 为每个记忆段跟踪键的一阶与二阶矩，使查询能够恢复其与各段对齐的均值和方差，并据此对每个状态的读出做更内容感知的加权聚合；该方法作为通用路由机制嵌入 Gated DeltaNet-1.3B 与 Mamba-2-780M 的多状态变体，不改变主干结构，仅增加轻量矩统计与路由计算，因此适合现有 MSLA 模型的热插拔改造。
   - 结果：在 RULER needle-in-a-haystack 与长上下文 QA 上，MERLIN 相对基线分别提升 8.3%（Gated DeltaNet-1.3B）和 7.0%（Mamba-2-780M）；在 LongBench 多个子任务上提升 13.5% 和 12.0%，甚至优于消耗 4 倍内存的基线，表明二阶路由能在低内存开销下显著增强长上下文能力。在相同内存预算下，MERLIN 的收益说明二阶统计是比单纯扩大状态更有效的路由信号。

3. [GTFormer: A Syntax-Free Graph Network Alternative to Transformers for 8192$\times$ Length Extrapolation in Linear Time](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37524v1)
   - 分类：Long-Context Architecture / Linear-Time Attention Alternative
   - 研究价值：值得精读
   - 关联：直接挑战 Transformer 二次复杂度与长度外推瓶颈，是高效长上下文建模的高风险高回报方向。
   - 痛点：Transformer 的自注意力需要全对全 token 比较，带来 O(N^2) 计算与内存瓶颈，且序列长度超过训练窗口后 softmax 注意力被稀释并出现分布外坍塌；现有长上下文扩展方法常依赖位置编码外推、稀疏化或近似注意力，仍难在单卡上以线性成本稳定外推到极长序列。如何用非注意力结构同时解决二次复杂度和长度外推，是长上下文建模的关键痛点。
   - 方法：GTFormer 提出无句法的层级图网络替代扁平自注意力，将原始 token 流组织为有界度层次：平衡二叉归约树、水平边界桥接位移边、随机扩展捷径、多通道凸高速门控以及自顶向下广播；该结构在 O(N) 内存下传播信息，训练时仅见 N≤512 的序列，却通过图结构实现零样本长度外推。它把长程依赖转化为层级图上的多跳消息传递，并用多通道门控缓解深层信息衰减。
   - 结果：在 8 个长上下文代码、日志和多跳检索基准上，GTFormer 从不超过 512 的训练长度零样本外推到 N=4,194,304（8192×），在单张商用 GPU 上保持近完美准确率，而标准 Transformer 与 RoPE Transformer 出现注意力坍塌或显存耗尽；这说明线性时间图结构可作为超长上下文建模的可行替代。其线性内存与强外推是主要卖点，但仍需观察复杂语言任务上的泛化。

**主要痛点**
- 大模型预训练的优化器与权重范数控制仍缺可解释、可证明的高效累积机制。
- 长上下文线性注意力与 Transformer 替代结构需在低内存下提升检索精度与长度外推。
- 智能体 RL 的 rollout、扩散语言模型解码与端侧推理都存在明显计算浪费。

**方法趋势**
- 用累计有效学习率、二阶矩路由、层级图消息传递和可微分投影改进训练/推理核心算子。
- 通过环境级调度、元认知控制器、稀疏记忆传播和对抗蒸馏减少无效 rollout 与网络评估。
- 以可控数据合成、scaling law 和零样本先验注入提升数据与部署效率。

**结果信号**
- MuonGS 在匹配损失下取得约 15% token 等效加速，并证明 C_T=Ω(√T)。
- MERLIN 在长上下文 QA/LongBench 上提升 7-13.5%，GTFormer 实现 8192× 零样本长度外推。
- 多种推理加速方法报告 20%-400× 的成本下降，同时保持有竞争力的精度。

AI agents have made substantial progress in domains where they have a non-trivial amount of time to deliberate between actions, such as mathematics, software engineering, and performing tasks on desktop applications. It is less clear how such models fare in settings that demand real-time interaction, such as playing a video game or operating a physical device. In these settings, the environment state may evolve as the agent is reasoning, or even as the underlying model is performing a forward pass. To gain insight into the real-time interaction capabilities of vision-language agents, we introduce Gym-Anything-RT. Our key idea is to construct visual minigames that demand a portfolio of capabilities required for interactive real-time settings. Gym-Anything-RT uses an agentic pipeline to automatically generate such environments based on a taxonomy of six capabilities required for real-time interaction, including the ability to perceive the environment and act under an evolving environment state. A key feature of Gym-Anything-RT is that it allows for controlled comparison of real-time interaction capabilities: we can run the same minigame in real time, or pause or slow down the environment to relax the requirement for real-time actions. We also identify intriguing behaviors by the most powerful frontier agents: for instance, when given the ability to write code, these agents write code that bypasses the need to perceive and act in real time. We publicly release Gym-Anything-RT and the generated minigames to spur further research on AI agents in real-time interactive settings.

### 9. [Follow the Gradient: Tracing Safety Degradation Back to Training Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37481v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：safety degradation, fine-tuning, transformer, large language models, safety-degrading data looks benign, erode their safety EVEN
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37481v1>

**原始摘要**

Fine-tuning Large Language Models (LLMs) on instruction data can quietly erode their safety, even when no training example looks harmful. Because this safety-degrading data looks benign, standard content filters struggle to catch it. We tackle this problem by developing SimpleLoRIF, a scalable gradient embedding method that identifies fine-tuning examples whose gradients align with unsafe behavior. By simplifying a low-rank attribution method and restricting backpropagation to the final four transformer blocks, SimpleLoRIF can embed datasets on the order of a million examples, boosting gradient attribution from a small-scale analysis tool into a practical filter for full post-training datasets. We first validate it on emergent misalignment, where we find that gradients can connect insecure-code training data to the broadly harmful behaviors that appear after fine-tuning, with AUC as high as 0.865 in the gradient space of misaligned models. Finally, we analyzed the removed data and identify possibly under-studied sources of safety degradation, such as tool-execution failures and narrative arithmetic. We release SafeTulu along with a Hugging Face Space that lets researchers build their own customized versions of SafeTulu to fine-tune on, such as by changing the removal fraction or filtering only certain subsets.

### 10. [PerceptRL: Making Visual Perception Rewardable in MLLMs from Answers to Interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37471v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：single-turn perception, visual perception, perception agentic, reasoning, multimodal, multi-agent
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37471v1>

**原始摘要**

Fine-grained visual perception remains a bottleneck during post-training of multimodal large language models (MLLMs), as perceptual errors can constrain gains from reasoning-focused optimization. Although image-caption pre-training improves perception, its influence can weaken as subsequent stages change data mixtures and objectives. Continuing caption supervision during post-training is challenging because subjective, information-dense descriptions complicate verifiable reward design. We present PerceptRL, which strengthens perception at two complementary levels. Perception RL uses a multi-agent pipeline to extract verified errors from dense image captions and convert the corresponding visual facts into QA pairs with fixed answer labels and rule-based rewards. Chain QA links correlated errors, while multiple-choice QA combines unrelated errors. A specialized route targets high-error domains with tailored verification, and a domain-agnostic general route covers errors beyond these domains. Perception Agentic RL addresses fine-grained and dense cases unresolved by single-turn Perception RL through multi-turn inspection with visual tools. We observe coordinate drift even in models trained to use tools, where incorrect crops or annotations distort evidence and propagate errors. Coordinate Injection corrects spatial operations in cold-start trajectories, while a grounding-aware process reward supervises intermediate spatial fidelity alongside final-answer correctness. Training proceeds through shared cold-start SFT, single-turn Perception RL, and multi-turn Perception Agentic RL. ### 11. [Concordat: Typed Sessions for LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37461v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：agent, large language models, typed sessions for LLM, claude haiku the concordat, decisions reserved for humans, concordat typed sessions
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37461v1>

**原始摘要**

Agents built on large language models are increasingly allowed to take consequential actions: cancelling bookings, issuing refunds, changing accounts. The rules that govern those actions, such as "obtain the customer's explicit confirmation before changing anything", are usually stated in the prompt and followed only as reliably as the model chooses to follow them. We present Concordat, a Python library that treats an agent as an object whose interface is a finite-state protocol, in the spirit of typestate and session types, and enforces that protocol on the model at run time. On every turn the model is offered only the tools that are legal in the current protocol state, calls outside that set are rejected before they have side effects, decisions reserved for humans can only be requested, and token and tool-call budgets are charged and split linearly across handoffs. Protocols are checked when they are defined, and a subclass of an agent is rejected if it could bypass its parent's protocol, a behavioural form of Liskov substitution. We evaluate a Concordat agent against the standard agent of tau-bench on its 50 airline tasks, adding simulated customers who pressure the agent to skip confirmation. The standard agent made changes without the required "yes" in 13 of 50 tasks with gpt-4.1 and in 29 of 128 task-runs with Claude Haiku 5.5; the Concordat agent made none, with no significant difference in task success. tau-bench's own reward did not detect most of these violations. Concordat is open source.
