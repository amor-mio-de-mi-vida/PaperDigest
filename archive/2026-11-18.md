### 1. [FastFrames: Video Generation at Interactive Latency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23226v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：video generation, autoregressive video diffusion, fastframes enables real-time interaction, camera-conditioned and physics-conditioned video, b200 GPU, medium for interactive visual
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23226v1>

**原始摘要**

Autoregressive video diffusion models generate frames based on new observations and inputs, making them a promising medium for interactive visual experiences. However, existing models are orders of magnitude too slow for fluid interaction. This paper introduces FastFrames, a family of fast autoregressive video diffusion models that achieves interactive latency without compromising quality. We profile the inference pipeline of state-of-the-art models and find that operations often treated as fixed implementation details, such as VAE decoding and KV cache updates, in fact account for over half of end-to-end inference latency. We challenge the assumption that these components are indispensable and replace them with lightweight alternatives. Our 1.3B variant runs at 36 ms per frame, or 28 responses per second (RPS), on a single B200 GPU while matching the generation quality of a state-of-the-art model that is nearly 8x slower. Our 14B variant runs at 45 ms per frame, or 22 RPS, on an 8x B200 GPU node and yields significantly better visual quality and realism. We show that FastFrames enables real-time interaction in applications such as camera-conditioned and physics-conditioned video generation.

Human cognitive state (e.g., distraction, stress), gaze, and behavior interact with the driving environment in subtle ways that AI could use to assist drivers, but ground-truth labels for human cognitive state levels are costly to obtain at scale. ### 3. [Distortion-Free Semantic Watermarks for Black-Box Generative Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23208v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：distortion-free semantic, semantic embedding, semantic watermarks, diffusion, autoregressive, generative model and black-box
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23208v1>

**原始摘要**

We present a provably distortion-free semantic watermarking algorithm that requires only black-box sampling access to a generative model and black-box access to a semantic embedding. A watermark is called distortion-free if, averaged over its secret detection key, it leaves the generator's output distribution unchanged; this provides a formal guarantee that watermarking does not reduce output quality. Conventional such watermarks are often brittle to transformations. This is especially problematic for images, where benign operations such as compression, cropping, or resizing change the entire pixel representation. Semantic watermarks therefore require detection to be robust to content-preserving transformations. Previous methods combining these properties rely heavily on specific white-box properties of the underlying model: either to autoregressive text generation or to diffusion-specific access, such as control of the initial noise or diffusion inversion. In contrast, our construction is fully generic: It only interacts with the generative model and the semantic embedding, a function that determines the notion of "preserving content" under which detection should be robust, via sample and query access. We then instantiate this framework for image generation. We characterize the two competing properties required of the semantic embedding: it should remain stable under transformations that preserve an image's content, but should distinguish independent generations from the same prompt. We train an image encoder directly for this tradeoff. The resulting encoder improves separation while maintaining stability over off-the-shelf alternatives and enables strong aggregate detection while remaining robust to various transformations. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our watermark scheme on both open-source and closed models, which is possible only due to restriction to black-box access.

### 4. [WoRD: Diffusion-Based Posterior Inference for Online Goal Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23192v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：agent, candidate GOAL, diffusion, multimodal, WORD RUNS ONE warm-started, high-level symbolic recognition abstracting
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23192v1>

**原始摘要**

Goal recognition is the task of inferring an agent's intended goal from its observed behavior. Most existing work focuses on high-level symbolic recognition, abstracting away the continuous motion through which agents reach their goal. Methods that do reason over continuous trajectories rely on an explicit dynamics model that is hard to acquire and rarely transfers across settings. Neither approach explicitly learns how agents move toward each candidate goal, limiting accuracy on ambiguous trajectory prefixes where such knowledge is most needed. We present WoRD (Waypoint Recognition with Diffusion), which uses a learned trajectory prior to infer the agent's goal from partial observations. At each step, WoRD runs one warm-started denoising chain per candidate goal, steers each chain toward the observed trajectory through a closed-form log-likelihood gradient, and reweights the resulting samples by Bayes' rule into a calibrated posterior over goals. We evaluate WoRD on simulated robot settings, pedestrian trajectories, and vehicle trajectories using four metrics targeting distinct failure modes. Across all three settings, the same inference procedure preserves calibrated multimodal beliefs through ambiguous prefixes where filter- and classifier-based observers collapse to a single mode or grow overconfident.

### 5. [TextSphere: Efficient Text-to-Image Generation via Shared Hyperspherical Alignment and Uniformity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23155v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：alignment, efficient text-to-image, text-to-image generation, diffusion, flickr30k demonstrate that textsphere, language conditioning and cross-modal

**原始摘要**

Experiments on CUB-200-2011, MS-COCO-2014, and Flickr30k demonstrate that TextSphere consistently reduces generation FID (gFID) over the text-conditioned baseline while achieving reconstruction FID (rFID) below 1.0. We further observe that improved reconstruction fidelity does not necessarily yield better generation quality. In addition, we use Partial Least Squares Singular Value Decomposition (PLS-SVD) to analyze cross-modal alignment and modality gap, offering insight into the effectiveness of our approach.

### 6. [Capability Composition in Heterogeneous Teams via Barrier-Guided Diffusion Sampling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23149v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：agent, TEAM trajectories, diffusion, heterogeneous teams via barrier-guided, heterogeneous TEAM can cooperate, sampling from generative
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23149v1>

**原始摘要**

The agents in a heterogeneous team can cooperate to accomplish tasks that exceed their individual capabilities. The team trajectories for a cooperative task can be generated by a generative model, provided that the model has been trained on demonstrations of the agents cooperating. Yet, demonstrations of cooperation are costly and may not exist for tasks that the team has never performed. The conditions under which the agents can combine their capabilities, however, are physical properties of the team and are known before any demonstration is collected. We encode the conditions as constraints on the team trajectories, and we enforce the constraints while sampling from generative models trained on demonstrations of each agent alone. We prove that the proposed method can generate every team trajectory that satisfies the constraints, although the demonstrations contain no cooperation. Hence the constraints determine which team trajectories can be generated, and the demonstrations determine how likely each team trajectory is. Numerical results on two cooperative tasks show the effectiveness of the method, which generates cooperative team trajectories that satisfy the constraints and requires no retraining when agents join or leave the team. Finally, we implement the method on a multi-robot testbed.

- 关键词：LLM agents, still NEED, HOW THEY should self-improve, GOAL for self-improvement agents, self-improve under these conditions, self-improvement agents construct
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23219v1>

**原始摘要**

LLM agents increasingly drive their own learning. Self-improvement methods delegate various components of model optimization to the models themselves. As models become more capable, this motivates a basic question: do these increasingly capable models still need us to specify how they should self-improve? In this paper we study an autonomous paradigm in which, given basic primitives, resources, and a general goal for self-improvement, agents construct their own self-learning algorithm for context-based optimization. Using the setting of gameplay as a testbed, a frozen LLM agent is given a compute budget and tools for collecting experience and updating text documents, and allowed to freely experiment without an externally imposed loop or access to real-world data. We find that sufficiently capable models do self-improve under these conditions, demonstrating strategies that align with classic cognitive frameworks for self-regulated learning and constructs from machine learning literature on self-generated curricula. Furthermore, we study the extent to which the learned text artifacts transfer to external benchmarks, observing improvement across different game horizons, mechanics, and 3D navigation.

### 3. [Mind the Gap: A Deployed Multi-Agent System for Documentation Maintenance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23142v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：agent, documentation GAP, alignment, re-answered the SAME ticket, judges preferred the post-repair, preferred the post-repair answer
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23142v1>

**原始摘要**

The "AI revolution" has enabled teams across the technology industry to deliver software features at unprecedented velocity. While code evolves faster than ever, customer-facing documentation often struggles to stay up to speed. As users increasingly rely on LLM-based agents that consume documentation as their primary interface, the impact of this documentation gap worsens: stale information propagates and is treated as authoritative. Signals for identifying where documentation has fallen behind already exist in abundance, both explicit (e.g., resolved support tickets revealing what documentation lacks) and implicit (e.g., code-documentation discrepancies detectable from automated loops). We deploy a multi-agent system, built around a triage agent we call "TriAgent," that continuously extracts these signals and repairs documentation drift. Over 43 days of shadow-mode operation, our system triaged 499 tickets and surfaced 92 gaps in the documentation. Using a panel of LLM judges from independent model families, 81.1% of TriAgent's verifiable assertions were correct (245 of the 302 assertions the human resolution addresses; majority of three judges); on length-comparable pairs the panel preferred TriAgent's response in 70.5% of pairs and the human engineer's in 23.2% (the rest had no judge majority). Importantly, agent- and human-sourced replies tended to be complementary, with the human often surfacing insights that were either buried within the existing documentation or missing from it entirely; our system turns each such insight into a documentation gap and, after human review, a fix. Repairing a gap measurably improved the agent's later answer: when the agent re-answered the same ticket after the repair, blinded judges preferred the post-repair answer over its own pre-repair answer 71.4% of the time (n=35). A blinded human audit confirms strong alignment on self-improvement.

### 4. [GameRealm: Grounding Visual Learning in Interactive Games](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23140v1)

**原始摘要**

Visual foundation models are central to physical intelligence, yet their development remains constrained by fragmented training datasets, inconsistent annotation quality, and limited multimodal supervision. To address these challenges, we introduce GameRealm, a large-scale, richly annotated dataset that grounds visual observations in synchronized, game-native supervision. GameRealm encompasses over 120 million RGB frames and 1,000 hours of gameplay footage across all views. Beyond its unprecedented scale, GameRealm provides rich multimodal supervision, including metric depth, camera parameters, synchronized audio, control inputs, and 3D point trajectories. Extensive experiments across diverse visual learning tasks demonstrate that GameRealm consistently strengthens visual foundation models in both visual generation and geometric learning. These results establish GameRealm as a versatile and valuable data resource, unlocking new opportunities for advancing visual intelligence.

### 5. [Dynamic Task Allocation with Dialogue](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23119v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：dynamic task allocation, agent, large language models, beliefs about task allocation, domain extended with dynamically, extended with dynamically arriving
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23119v1>

**原始摘要**

Effective multi-agent collaboration requires agents to adapt task allocation as goals and responsibilities evolve. Prior task-allocation frameworks often assume static goals, full observability, or rely on large language models (LLMs) without explicit, verifiable beliefs about task allocation or intent. These assumptions limit their applicability to real-world collaboration, where agents often lack full information about each other's actions, progress, or local environment. To support dynamic task allocation under partial observability, we introduce a communication-augmented Bayesian task-allocation framework. Our agent uses dialogue to reduce uncertainty about the environment and collaborators, while keeping decisions grounded in explicit probabilistic beliefs. We evaluate our framework in an Overcooked domain extended with dynamically arriving tasks under both full and partial object-space observability. Our communication agent consistently outperforms a vanilla LLM agent in both settings. Additionally, our framework performs comparably to a non-communicating task-allocation baseline under full observability, but substantially outperforms it under partial observability as task complexity increases by an average speed up of 0.585 and completes on average 18 timesteps faster. These results indicate that our agent is more efficient in dynamic task allocation than baselines in partially observable environments, and that communication is important as available information decreases.

### 7. [The Resolution Hypothesis: Agent Evolution's Key Is Identifying Improvements](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23068v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：agent evolution, resolution hypothesis agent, identifying improvements., evolution methods and SIX, papers and evolution often, support for the hypothesis
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23068v1>

**原始摘要**

Agent evolution follows a simple loop: a proposer writes candidate artifacts (prompts, skills, memories) and selection accepts those that score higher. This loop has shown promise, yet practitioners face a puzzle: methods trade wins across papers, and evolution often fails to bootstrap on certain tasks. We trace this puzzle to the Resolution Hypothesis: agent evolution stalls not because methods fail to propose better artifacts, but because noisy evaluations cannot reliably identify them. Across seven evolution methods and six models (over 10M LLM calls), we find support for the hypothesis at both levels where evaluation scores are used. At the candidate level (choosing artifacts), helpful candidate artifacts exist, but selection is no better than random at telling them from harmful ones, so errors accumulate across rounds. At the method level (ranking methods), typical margins of a few percentage points are smaller than the difference between two independent runs of the same method, so published rankings often rest on luck. Together, these findings indicate that agent evolution is often bottlenecked by reliably identifying improvements. This calls for a shift in how we develop and evaluate self-improving agents, from designing ever more candidate improvement systems to making true improvements detectable and accumulable.

### 8. [Values Shape What Self-Improving Coding Agents Build](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23067v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：agent, self-improving coding, values WERE, attention, values shape WHAT self-improving, self-transcendence. across diverse coding
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23067v1>

**原始摘要**

Self-improving agents are typically optimized for the results they produce, with less attention to how they produce them. Their instructions give designers limited ways to shape how they work, especially when the task is open-ended. This paper studies whether a short value statement in an agent's persistent instruction file changes not only how well the agent performs but also what it builds. Four higher-order value types in Schwartz's theory of basic human values were evaluated: openness to change, conservation, self-enhancement, and self-transcendence. Across diverse coding tasks, the values were held fixed within each run over multiple iterations of self-improvement. Three findings emerged: (1) Values matter for performance across a diverse set of tasks. The openness values increase performance on held-out data, whereas the opposite conservation values result in scores below those of an agent given no values. (2) Values change what agents build, producing solution algorithms that differ in size, complexity and approach. (3) Human oversight is still needed for designing values. Allowing the agent to revise its own values narrows the differences between sets of values, and values distilled from the AI literature provide no detectable gain over having no values. Properly designed values can thus influence what a self-improving coding agent builds and, where the task permits, how well it performs.

### 9. [PhysScope: Agentic Multimodal Physics Reasoning with Grounded Visual Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23010v1)

- 关键词：multimodal physics reasoning, agent, benchmark, vision-language, evaluated vlms. with gemini-3.8-flash, inference-time visual harness
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23010v1>

**原始摘要**

Vision-language models (VLMs) can correctly interpret physics diagrams yet apply constraints outside the states or conditions in which they hold, causing premise errors to propagate through subsequent calculations. We introduce PhysScope, an inference-time visual harness that supports self-correction of these scope mismatches without updating model weights. PhysScope enables the model to link physical scopes directly to its visual observations and explicitly constructs derived views to support reasoning. The harness imposes explicit constraints on state distinctions, conditions, and physical scopes during model reasoning, and checks agent-proposed revisions against sources and scopes. Accepted revisions invalidate registered dependents, thereby maintaining scope-aware working memory. On PhysAlign, PhysScope achieves higher solving scores than direct solving across all six evaluated VLMs. With Gemini-3.8-flash, it outperforms Canvas-CoT by 9.34 percentage points and substantially improves solving accuracy on the out-of-distribution (OOD) PhysUniBench benchmark. Ablation studies support explicit scope management and dependency maintenance as design principles for multimodal physics reasoning agents.
