### 1. [UltraTex: Aligning Physical Knowledge for High-Fidelity PBR Texture Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24941v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：PBR texture, physical knowledge, diffusion, alignment, vision-language, knowledge for high-fidelity PBR
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24941v1>

**原始摘要**

Generating physically based rendering (PBR) texture maps for a 3D mesh from a single reference image is highly ambiguous, as the observed appearance entangles material identity, surface reflectance, and illumination. While current generative methods produce realistic albedo maps, they still struggle to infer accurate metallic and roughness attributes for relighting. We present UltraTex, a cascaded multi-view framework that moves beyond appearance matching by integrating physical knowledge into multi-view diffusion-based PBR generation. UltraTex first jointly predicts albedo and world-space shading normals to generate more detailed albedo maps, and then predicts metallic-roughness (MR) maps conditioned on the generated albedo, reference image, and material semantic priors. To inject material semantics, a lightweight adapter transforms hidden-layer features of a vision-language model (VLM) into intrinsic material representation tokens, providing part-level knowledge anchors for MR estimation. To model material-light interaction, photometric representation alignment guides intermediate diffusion features toward ground-truth material renderings under controlled lighting. This makes metallic and roughness variations observable through highlight intensity, specular sharpness, and local reflectance contrast, providing stronger guidance for fine-grained material prediction without explicit lighting supervision. Extensive experiments show that UltraTex generates high-quality textures and outperforms existing open-source and commercial methods in visual fidelity, material accuracy, and physical plausibility.

### 2. [$Z$-sight: Unifying Vision-Language-Action and Latent World Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24940v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：latent world modeling., vision-language, world modeling. video-based world, modeling. video-based world action, human video and robot, baseline and across SIX
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24940v1>

**原始摘要**

We introduce $Z$-sight, a unified robot foundation model that couples vision-language-action (VLA) and generative latent world modeling. Video-based world action models (WAMs) show the value of joint future-and-action generation for control, yet inherit the heavy cost of video generators. Recent latent-space policies avoid pixel generation, yet typically align intermediate features or rely on frozen target encoders, decoupling future prediction from the VLM's native feature space. $Z$-sight instead predicts the future directly in the visual token space in which its VLM perceives the present. Coupling a foresight expert and a continuous action expert with the VLM in a mixture-of-transformers, $Z$-sight jointly denoises future spatial tokens and actions at inference without pixel reconstruction or external encoders. Trained on human video and robot trajectories, $Z$-sight achieves 81.9% success on SIMPLE (+9.6 points over the strongest baseline) and 73.3% across six Unitree G1 tasks (+20.0 points) at 110 ms per chunk, while remaining competitive on tabletop benchmarks.

### 3. [Phoenix: Learning Scene Structure Representations for Generalizable World-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24938v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：phoenix encoder, scene structure, benchmark, distillation, models. world-action models jointly, world-action models jointly predict
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24938v1>

**原始摘要**

World-action models jointly predict future observations and actions, yet reconstruction-oriented visual representations do not explicitly emphasize the spatial relationships and object-state changes needed for manipulation. We introduce Phoenix, which learns representations of scene structure for world-action modeling. Specifically, Phoenix Encoder enriches DINOv3's general visual semantics through dual-teacher distillation of geometric and spatiotemporal semantic knowledge. Next, CS-VAE causally aggregates and compresses Phoenix Encoder features into generative latents while preserving scene information and spatial layout. Finally, Phoenix jointly predicts these latents, complementary appearance latents, and robot actions, linking scene evolution with action generation. To study pretraining data efficiency, we curate approximately 39,000 hours of egocentric and robot manipulation data for representation learning and use the robot demonstration subset for WAM pretraining. Following benchmark-specific post-training, Phoenix achieves state-of-the-art performance across simulation and real-robot benchmarks spanning single-arm, bimanual, and dexterous manipulation. It demonstrates strong robustness to perturbations, achieving success rates of 94.8% on LIBERO-Plus and 72.4% on RoboTwin 2.0 C2R, alongside strong cross-embodiment transfer and real-world manipulation performance on Bench2Dex and RoboDojo-RealWorld, respectively. Pretraining-scale analysis shows larger performance gains from data scaling than a video-VAE-only baseline, indicating stronger scaling benefits and more effective data utilization. Moreover, Phoenix maintains high success rates when adapted with limited downstream demonstrations, demonstrating strong few-shot adaptation. Our code, pretraining data, and models are available on the project page to help the community reproduce our results.

### 4. [Render the Robot, Generate the World: One Video World Model Across Embodiments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24925v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：video world, agent, world models, single- and bimanual robot, ALL eight embodiments doppel-8, human embodiments from single-
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24925v1>

**原始摘要**

Predicting the consequences of an agent's actions requires modeling both the body's motion and the environment's response. Most video world models attempt to learn both from numerical actions, even though geometry and kinematics already specify the intended motion. We introduce Doppel, an embodiment-agnostic video world model conditioned on camera-aligned renderings of future body configurations and numerical action trajectories. By explicitly providing the intended motion, Doppel can focus on predicting object motion and interaction-driven changes in the environment. Rendered motion also provides a common visual interface across different embodiments and action spaces. To train on robot datasets without camera extrinsics, we recover camera poses and align rendered trajectories with recorded observations. We jointly train a single shared video backbone and conditioning adapter on eight datasets spanning eight robot and human embodiments, from single- and bimanual robot arms to a dexterous humanoid and human hands and bodies, using about 600 hours of video without embodiment labels. Across three robot-arm datasets, Doppel improves PSNR over action-only baselines by 1.00 dB on held-out trajectories and 1.20 dB under out-of-distribution evaluation, while predicting more consistent object motion. Training on all eight embodiments (Doppel-8) improves robot predictions over the three-robot model (Doppel-3) on four of six evaluation sets. Project page: https://doppelwm.github.io/.

### 5. [Unified Law for Visual Tasks (The Four Laws of Computer Vision)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24917v1)

- 关键词：unified LAW for visual, FOUR LAWS, ALL visual, computer vision, large language models, framework for future visual-task

**原始摘要**

In this paper, we propose a Unified Law for Visual Tasks, which could be further divided into Four Laws of Computer Vision, with the goal of enabling a single framework or method to uniformly train on and perform nearly all visual tasks. Our starting point is an inspiration and hypothesis: if the world information is encoded into image, which we call World Snapshots, then image processing is world processing, and image generation is World Snapshot generation. ### 6. [WorldRuler: Interactive World Modeling under Configurable Rules](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24869v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：user-defined rules, video generation, world states, world models, rules beyond real-world behaviors., cases where rules TAKE
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24869v1>

**原始摘要**

Rules govern how player actions change interactive worlds. Different rules create distinct gameplay experiences within similar scenes and action spaces. For customizable gameplay, generative world models must follow user-defined rules beyond real-world behaviors. However, realizing user-defined rules presents two challenges: Rule-State Persistence and Rule-to-Visual Realization. The former requires preserving rule definitions, dependencies, and world states across saving and loading, so that restored worlds follow the established rules. The latter requires accurately realizing rule-induced changes on designated objects and attributes, particularly when affected objects are unseen. In this paper, we propose WorldRuler, which organizes rule definitions, dependencies, and object states in an executable 3D rule graph and connects rule execution, video generation, and perceptual feedback in a closed loop. The graph supports joint saving and restoration of rules and world states, while dynamic object binding grounds rule-specified entities in visual instances and uses stored states to constrain generation from their first appearance. We further introduce object attribute state replacement and counterfactual reconstruction training, enabling accurate attribute control and compatible appearance and motion changes while limiting interference with unrelated content. We develop an automated data collection and annotation pipeline using GTA-V and Habitat to construct RuleVerse, a dataset of rule-driven interactions with detailed state and event annotations, including cases where rules take effect before affected objects become visible. Extensive experiments demonstrate the effectiveness of WorldRuler in preserving rule-state consistency across saving and loading and accurately realizing rule outcomes on designated objects and attributes.

### 7. [Does the Action Matter? Probing Action-Conditioned Semantic Forecasting as a World Model for Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24840v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：action-conditioned semantic forecasting, semantic segmentation, without action, action dependence, reasoning, world models
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24840v1>

**原始摘要**

World models are increasingly used in autonomous driving to predict how a scene evolves under the ego vehicle's actions, supporting counterfactual reasoning and model-based planning. However, existing evaluations focus on prediction fidelity and planning performance, and rarely verify that the predictions depend on the conditioning action. In this paper, we study action dependence in action-conditioned semantic forecasting, which predicts future semantic segmentation from past segmentations and the ego action.
