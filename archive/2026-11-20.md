### 1. [Posterior Matching Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26629v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：distillation, video generation, posterior matching, alignment, diffusion, posterior sampling with any-step
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26629v1>

**原始摘要**

Diffusion distillation accelerates image and video generation by compressing a pretrained teacher's iterative sampling process into a few network evaluations. Distribution-based methods achieve this by matching the student's output distribution to the teacher's. This matching is often driven by reverse-KL objectives, whose mode-seeking bias favors dominant teacher modes at the expense of diversity across samples. We introduce Posterior Matching Distillation (PMD), a new member of the distribution-based distillation family that combines mode-covering posterior sampling with any-step generation. At zero population loss, PMD recovers the teacher's full posterior of clean images at each trained noise level. Its stochastic student supports any-step sampling budgets within the trained timestep grid without further training. To learn these posteriors, PMD matches exponentially reweighted averages of student samples conditioned on a fixed noisy state to corresponding teacher posterior means. Under Gaussian corruption, exponentially tilted posterior mean is a single teacher evaluation at a shifted input. We prove that, matching tilted means over an open neighborhood uniquely identifies the full posterior. PMD thus supervises the entire conditional distribution through a family of mean constraints, without any auxiliary student-score network. Experiments on SDXL and FLUX.2 [klein] 4B demonstrate that PMD achieves the best FID among 4-step methods on both models, while maintaining competitive or leading performance across prompt-alignment and perceptual-quality metrics.

### 2. [RL Post-Training in Diffusion Transformers Is Activation Steering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26627v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：reinforcement learning, diffusion, post-training without new input-dependent, post-training substantially improves, steering vectors added, activation steering. reinforcement
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26627v1>

**原始摘要**

Reinforcement learning (RL) post-training substantially improves the visual quality, text rendering, and compositional accuracy of diffusion transformers. Because capabilities such as rendering requested text or composing multiple objects are inherently prompt-dependent, a natural hypothesis is that improving them requires learning complex, input-dependent transformations. To isolate how much of these gains an input-independent shift explains, we probe each RL-tuned model with a deliberately simple parameterization: a fixed set of steering vectors added to the block outputs of the frozen base model. On SD3.5-M, these vectors hold 65x fewer parameters than the RL-tuned LoRA (0.29M against 18.8M). We fit them to match the velocity predictions of public RL-tuned checkpoints, with no reward in the objective. Although the vectors are identical for every prompt, token, and sampling seed, they recover 90.0-96.3% of the reward gains of six RL-tuned image models, and 89.8% and 111.9% on two RL-tuned video models. Initialized at zero and trained with online RL, the vectors alone exceed the reward gains of weight-based RL on text rendering (OCR) and human preference (PickScore) on SD3.5-M. A constant shift thus suffices for most of the gain of policy-gradient RL post-training: without new input-dependent transformations, it steers the pretrained generator toward what it can already compute.

### 3. [One Prior, Two Dynamics: Repurposing Video Diffusion Transformers for Self-Supervised Latent Action Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26617v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：pretrained video, ONE prior TWO, latent action, video generator, diffusion, BOTH dynamics end-to-end through
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26617v1>

**原始摘要**

Videos reveal how the world changes, but rarely the actions that cause those changes. Latent action learning recovers such supervision from visual transitions, yet often learns the underlying dynamics largely from scratch. We ask whether a pretrained video generator can instead provide the prior for both inverse and forward dynamics. We introduce O$^{2}$LaF, built on a simple principle: one prior, two dynamics. We initialize both models from the same pretrained video DiT and adapt them to their distinct roles: a feed-forward transition encoder with learnable action queries for inverse dynamics, and a causal, action-conditioned video generator for forward dynamics. By controlling information access and optimizing both dynamics end-to-end through a compact bottleneck, O$^{2}$LaF learns transferable actions from unlabeled videos. Across navigation and robotic manipulation, O$^{2}$LaF sets a new state of the art in few-shot action probing and enables stronger zero-shot action transfer across visual contexts and embodiments. Beyond generative video priors, we further extend our framework to predictive representation priors such as V-JEPA2, achieving competitive performance. Our findings suggest a broader paradigm for latent-action learning: repurposing pretrained video models to discover transferable actions and predict their consequences.

### 5. [Bio-PM-Guided Synthetic IMU Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26607v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：synthetic IMU, diffusion, benchmark, transformer, autoregressive, demonstrate that high-fidelity generation
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26607v1>

**原始摘要**

Collecting and labeling inertial measurement unit (IMU) data for human activity recognition (HAR) is expensive, and models trained on one group of subjects often fail to generalize to unseen individuals. Even with balanced data, classifiers struggle to distinguish dynamically similar activity classes and benefit from targeted augmentation. We explore whether synthetic IMU data generated in the representation space of a pretrained biosignal encoder, Bio-PM \cite{b1}, can serve as a practical augmentation source. Rather than synthesizing raw signals, we leverage Bio-PM's frozen encoder to convert 10-second IMU windows into token embedding sequences and train class-conditional decoders in this space: (1) an autoregressive transformer decoder that predicts tokens sequentially, and (2) a diffusion decoder that learns the token distribution through a denoising objective. These results demonstrate that high-fidelity generation in a pretrained encoder's representation space is achievable and practically useful for cross-subject HAR augmentation.

### 7. [ProtoDiffusion: Token-per-Layer Generative Modeling of Multi-Layer Images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26586v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：diffusion, images text-to-design reaches FID, multi-layer images. graphic designs, merged images text-to-design reaches, multi-layer variational autoencoder, token-per-layer generative modeling
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26586v1>

**原始摘要**

Graphic designs are ordered compositions of RGBA layers whose separation enables selection, movement, and reuse. Existing layered models represent each layer with a full-canvas or region-sized spatial latent grid, so representation size grows with the cumulative spatial extent of the layers. We present ProtoDiffusion, a latent-diffusion framework built on ProtoVAE, a multi-layer variational autoencoder that encodes a design into one shared spatial prototype and one candidate vector per layer. Each candidate conditions a shared decoder to reconstruct its corresponding RGBA layer. For $N$ layers, the representation contains $HW+N$ tokens before backbone-specific packing, compared with $NHW$ for full-grid-per-layer representations. ProtoDiffusion jointly denoises the prototype and a fixed set of candidate slots, conditioned on an image or text, with bounding boxes as optional layout guidance. The model leaves unneeded slots empty and determines the number of layers and, in absence of boxes, their layout, requiring neither as input. The same architecture supports both image-to-design and text-to-design. ### 1. [SoK: Assessing the Evolution of LLM Capability in Software Vulnerability Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26601v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：LLM capability, vulnerability research., real-world software without human, stakeholders from the best-supported, before. YET the TIME, TIME between the stages
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26601v1>

**原始摘要**

LLM-based agents are becoming increasingly capable at discovering, validating and patching vulnerabilities in real software, and vendors and researchers report that this is already changing how vulnerabilities are found and fixed. These claims, however, are scattered across papers, vendor reports and disclosures and are rarely comparable. We introduce a framework that rates these capabilities on an autonomy scale modeled on the levels of driving automation, and qualifies each claim by its correctness, its availability and the strength of its evidence. We use this framework to systematize the published evidence on LLM capability in vulnerability research. We find that agents discovered, validated, and patched vulnerabilities in real-world software without human intervention before the restricted frontier models of 2026, and that open-weight models subsequently attained comparable autonomy for discovery and validation. We then measure the consequences of evolving LLM security capabilities in a decade of public vulnerability records, advisories and exploitation data. In 2026 the number of disclosed vulnerabilities more than doubled, with an increasing share rated critical or high, and severe bugs that had survived a decade or more in widely used code were reported several times more often than before. Yet the time between the stages of a vulnerability's life, from report to fix and from disclosure to attack, has not shifted noticeably so far. We grade the mitigations proposed in the literature with an explicit rubric and draw guidance for each group of stakeholders from the best-supported ones.

### 2. [Coda-DaVinci: Reimagining Video Generation with Code2Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26595v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：video generation, code2video, direct generation, agent, simulation with proxy-grounded prompting., coordinates simulation with proxy-grounded
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26595v1>

**原始摘要**

Code2Video uses agent-authored 3D proxies to guide video generation, enabling explicit control over structure and dynamics. However, how to construct proxies and prompts for optimal downstream quality remains underexplored, as do the capabilities and limitations of this paradigm. Through controlled experiments and blinded human evaluation, we study proxy granularity, physics simulation, and prompting. The results reveal task-dependent trade-offs: coarse proxies favor natural motion, while medium-grained proxies favor consistency. Physics simulation and proxy-aligned prompting further improve average quality. Experiments demonstrate leading instruction following on VTR and scene consistency on R2M, improved physical plausibility over direct generation, and higher visual realism than the compared Code2Video baselines. Long-video evaluations further show gains in event structure and consistency across successive interactions, while revealing limitations in transition stability and adherence to scene settings.

### 3. [Replication of Agentic Security Events: Audit and Investigation (ROGUE-AI)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26553v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：agent, events audit and investigation, security events audit, large language models, REAL incidents the rogue-ai, audit and investigation rogue-ai
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26553v1>

**原始摘要**

Agents based on large language models (LLMs) can now delete files and send payments on behalf of their users. News media reports routinely describe failures of these agents (e.g., Replit's coding agent deleting a production database), but a report often only says what went wrong and rarely (in)validates the incident or finds its root cause. Incident catalogs collect these reports, and benchmarks record whether agents fail, but neither replicates an incident nor tests the cause its report might identify. In this paper, we present ROGUE-AI (Replication of Agentic Security Events: Audit and Investigation), a multi-agent framework that closes the above gap and rebuilds each reported incident offline together with a matched control that lacks only the blamed cause, and runs agents on both. Our results suggest that ROGUE-AI is able to reproduce the reported harm in 89% of its 108 attempted replications. Agents built on five current GPT, Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek models made the reported mistake in 18% to 33% of runs, against at most 1% without the blamed cause. Of the 72 incidents with real-world harm, 62 had no underlying attacker, and in these, agents did what their task asked for but missed an ordinary hazard, such as a database shared with the live site. Replications without an attacker caused more harm than replications of prompt injection for all five models. Defenses against prompt injection look for injected instructions, but these replications contain none. Further, a warning after each tool output that names the hazard and its consequence lowered harm for all five models, whereas a note of the same length that does not mention the hazard left most of the harm in place. Built from real incidents, the ROGUE-AI replications form a reusable audit framework that vendors can rerun before each new release.

### 4. [Cost-Aware Agentic Feature Optimization System for Fleet-Scale Ranking Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26531v1)

- 关键词：agent, system for fleet-scale ranking, optimization system for fleet-scale, pipeline COST, fleet-scale ranking models., THEN tunes the feature
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26531v1>

**原始摘要**

Large Web platforms operate large fleets of ranking models, each selecting features from a shared pool of tens of thousands. We present an end-to-end agentic system that optimizes features across the fleet under resource constraints shared across models and those set per model, such as feature pipeline cost and serving throughput (QPS), respectively. In this system, LLM agents propose hypotheses, and deterministic tools execute experiments and verify them. For each model, an LLM generates and shortlists cross features from a combinatorial candidate space, and all features are then ranked by several importance methods. Feature blending mixes these rankings into a single selection, using Pareto fronts across tasks for multi-task models. An LLM Researcher agent then tunes the feature selection in 10--25 asynchronous trials under resource caps, and a trimming step keeps the pipeline cost within budget. Across production models, the system improves normalized entropy by 0.31% on average within each model's budget, and the trimming step cuts pipeline cost by 2.3% with no regression beyond measurement noise. The optimized models were launched, and in online A/B tests they improved a primary online metric by 0.20%.

### 5. [AutoMaster: Autonomous Post-Training by Making the Same Model a Better Teacher](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26510v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：autonomous post-training, teacher trajectories, agent, fine-tuning, teacher rollouts THEN fine-tunes, post-training should reinforce. EVEN
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26510v1>

**原始摘要**

Task success alone does not specify which behaviors post-training should reinforce. Even successful teacher trajectories can contain unsupported assumptions, unproductive repetition, or superficial verification. We propose Skill-FT (Skill-Guided Fine-Tuning), which uses natural-language skills to suppress undesirable habits and elicit underrepresented effective behaviors during teacher rollouts, then fine-tunes a target model on the resulting trajectories. We introduce AutoMaster, an autonomous post-training system in which an LLM agent develops these interventions through trajectory analysis, skill design, data selection, and feedback from rollouts and target-model evaluation. Across Terminal, Search, and software engineering tasks with 4B and 9B target models, AutoMaster improves mean performance over Vanilla SFT on successful unguided teacher trajectories by 1.42-8.99 percentage points, with the teacher, task pool, training set size, and SFT recipe held fixed. Together with the ablations and trajectory analyses, these results suggest that behavioral shaping can make a fixed model a more effective teacher. We further find that retaining skill instructions during SFT yields higher mean performance than removing them across all six settings, despite their absence at evaluation and identical supervised outputs. These findings suggest that preserving skill context as part of a training demonstration can support better behavioral internalization.

### 6. [MemFolder: Structure-Guided Memory Folding for Efficient Agent Memory Management](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26427v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：structure-guided memory folding, agent, attention, multimodal, memory groups using cross-record, three text-based memory benchmarks
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26427v1>

**原始摘要**

Long-term agents accumulate large external memory stores as interactions unfold, increasing the costs of storage, retrieval, and response generation. Efficiency is constrained by two coupled factors: a growing record count increases retrieval overhead, while longer retrieved contexts increase prefill computation and decoding-time attention costs. Existing methods prune or consolidate records, but pruning can discard useful evidence, whereas consolidation may produce fewer yet longer retrieval units. We introduce MemFolder, a structure-guided memory folding framework that sequentially reduces record count and context length. The framework comprises two core components that share memory groups, using cross-record structure to guide both evidence consolidation and compact representation. RecordFolder grounds entity mentions, progressively selects representative yet complementary anchors, and combines semantic and entity affinity with temporal chaining to consolidate compatible records into fewer retrieval units. ContextFolder encodes the original records within these groups into compact text and uses verification feedback to revise encodings when key facts cannot be recovered. Experiments on the three text-based memory benchmarks LoCoMo, PersonaMem, and LongMemEval and the two multimodal benchmarks M3-Bench-web and M3-Bench-robot demonstrate the strong performance of our methods. Notably, on Mem0, RecordFolder retains 99.0% of the original average performance across the three text-based benchmarks with 50% fewer records, exceeding the strongest baseline by 1.9 percentage points in performance retention. Combined with ContextFolder, existing record compression methods further shorten their output records while retaining most of their performance.

### 7. [ZeroVLN: Learning to Navigate without Supervised Fine-Tuning of the VLM Brain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26414v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：VLM brain, reinforcement learning, vision-language, fine-tuning, ZERO navigation-domain SFT, action expert
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26414v1>

**原始摘要**

Representative vision-language navigation (VLN) methods typically couple a vision-language model (VLM) brain with a learnable action head and fine-tune the VLM brain on navigation-domain data to predict geometric goals. However, such adaptation can substantially alter base VLM weights and degrade general visual capabilities. We introduce ZeroVLN, a slow-fast navigation framework that achieves effective navigation with zero navigation-domain SFT of the VLM brain. Rather than teaching the brain to predict geometric goals, we equip it with a semantic tool-calling interface for conveying movement decisions to an action expert in natural language. We first adapt the action expert to carry out these movement requests, then jointly improve the brain's movement decisions and the action expert's continuous execution through closed-loop reinforcement learning (RL). Trained on the same navigation episodes, ZeroVLN substantially outperforms representative SFT-based hierarchical VLN methods on standard VLN-CE (continuous environments) benchmarks, while inducing only minor changes to the brain's weights and largely preserving its general capabilities. To our knowledge, ZeroVLN is the first slow-fast VLN framework with zero navigation-domain SFT of the VLM brain, and the first to jointly optimize its slow and fast policies through closed-loop agentic RL in VLN-CE.

### 9. [SpecFlow: Flow Matching in the Acceleration Spectrum for Multi-Agent Trajectory Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26390v1)

- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26390v1>

**原始摘要**

Multimodal trajectory forecasting must cover diverse future behaviors without sacrificing dynamical coherence. Most generative forecasters evolve frame-wise positions directly, leaving the relationships among position, velocity, and acceleration implicit. We present SpecFlow, a conditional flow-matching framework that treats the Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) coefficients of future accelerations as its generative state. - 关键词：agent, benchmark, self-distilled memory for LLM, lessons from self-distilled memory, deployment lessons from self-distilled, learn from its OWN
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26361v1>

**原始摘要**

LLM agents on long-horizon tasks fail in recurring, procedural ways. They mis-paginate an API, resolve the wrong entity, or answer when no answer was asked. We built a pipeline that lets an agent learn from its own history. It first mines short \emph{lessons} from the agent's own trajectories, one to three per episode. We distill these into recommendations for teams deploying agent memory.

### 13. [Reasoning on Call: Efficient Agentic Robot Control for Long-Horizon Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26341v1)

**原始摘要**

Large multimodal models can support long-horizon robot manipulation, but frequent model calls incur substantial latency and token costs. The central challenge is determining when agent assistance or intervention is needed and whether errors require correcting robot execution, subtask planning, or both. To address this problem, we propose RoC (Reasoning on Call), an efficient framework that coordinates a low-level policy and a high-level reasoning agent through an online task-state monitor. The monitor combines temporal visual observations with ordered interaction memory to jointly estimate progress within the active subtask and predict its successor. Overall, RoC supports efficient agent intervention and coordinated recovery across execution and planning for long-horizon robot manipulation.

### 14. [ResVision: Spectrum-Matched Resizing and Context-Preserving Inspection for Visual Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26318v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：context-preserving inspection, spectrum-matched resizing, reasoning, multimodal, vision-language, visual sampling and context-preserving
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26318v1>

**原始摘要**

Agentic visual reasoning improves vision-language models by selectively enlarging evidence without adding new scene content, but the benefit depends on whether fine structure remains accessible and whether local observations stay grounded in the full scene. We identify three recurring bottlenecks: high-frequency detail can be attenuated during visual-token formation; spatially distributed evidence can remain encoded yet difficult for the language model to retrieve; and isolated crops can weaken whole-detail correspondence through scale and positional mismatch. ResVision addresses these limitations with two complementary components. Spectrum-Matched Resizing (SMR) estimates image complexity and selects an adaptive global sampling scale, avoiding unnecessary enlargement on simple inputs while allocating more visual tokens to detail-rich images. Context-Preserving Inspection (CPI) returns each inspected crop together with the complete working view, preserving local detail and global context across tool turns. We evaluate Direct and Agentic variants on four frontier VLMs and 29 benchmarks spanning six visual-reasoning families. Complete ResVision (Agentic Base plus SMR plus CPI) is best for every model, improving by 3.36 to 4.94 points over Direct Base and 1.87 to 2.94 points over Agentic Base. These results show that adaptive visual sampling and context-preserving inspection address distinct, complementary constraints in multimodal reasoning.
