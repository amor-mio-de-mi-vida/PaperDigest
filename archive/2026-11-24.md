### 1. [Pruning Autonomous Driving VLAs: What to Preserve for Reasoning and Action](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31240v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：vlm-only pruning, reasoning, autonomous driving, vision-language, fine-tuning, chain-of-thought
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31240v1>

**原始摘要**

Reasoning vision-language-action (VLA) models for autonomous driving use chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning to guide action generation, but incur substantial memory and computational costs. We study structured pruning for these models, focusing on preserving both scene understanding and trajectory prediction. Our analysis of Taylor importance in Alpamayo-1.5-10B shows that reasoning and trajectory objectives assign different importance to the same vision-language model (VLM) structures. In the action expert, MLP channels exhibit more stable importance rankings across denoising steps than query heads, and a small fraction of channels accounts for most of the measured importance. Based on these findings, we propose MoOP, which combines importance ranks from both objectives for VLM pruning and aggregates stepwise importance to select a fixed subset of expert MLP channels. At 24.0% total parameter reduction, VLM-only pruning with MoOP performs comparably to or slightly better than the strongest baselines in driving, while retaining 94.0% of the dense model's LingoQA score, far above all other methods. At a higher reduction of 30.0%, MoOP degrades much less than every other method, as it is the only one whose reasoning does not collapse. Removing 93.75% of expert MLP channels increases total parameter reduction to 39.4%, with nearly unchanged open-loop errors relative to VLM-only pruning and competitive closed-loop performance. The resulting model achieves a 1.37x end-to-end inference speedup and 36.6% lower peak memory than the dense model, without post-pruning fine-tuning.

### 2. [Lyris: Layout-Guided Long Video Generation with Persistent 3D Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31239v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：video generation, LONG video, vision-language, beyond WHAT WAS captured, UPON return. memory-based video, persistent point-cloud memory where
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31239v1>

**原始摘要**

Exploring a scene from as little as a single photo requires generating a long, camera-controlled walkthrough that extends far beyond what was captured, yet remains consistent upon return. Memory-based video generation methods preserve what they have already seen, but have no anchor beyond it, where the camera drifts from its path and a text prompt can name objects but not place them. Layout-conditioned methods provide such an anchor and precise control over what appears where, but rely on layouts that are typically authored by hand. Both limitations arise because, beyond what was captured, nothing anchors the video model unless a human authors one. To overcome both, we propose Lyris, a long video generation framework that never leaves the video model without an anchor: a persistent point-cloud memory where the scene has been observed or generated, and a 3D layout that a vision-language model authors where it has not. Our key insight is that the interface between the two models can be surprisingly coarse. A layout of one labeled 3D box per object is simple enough for a vision-language model to author (and for a user to edit), yet expressive enough for a video model to follow once rendered along the camera path. We demonstrate walkthroughs of over three minutes and 50 meters from a single photo, passing through doorways into rooms it never shows. When evaluated on minute-long round trips across four datasets, Lyris outperforms state-of-the-art methods by a wide margin, reducing camera error by 3.8x and improving consistency upon return by 22% on average.

### 3. [Let Vision-Language Models Generate Images through Their Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31237v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：pretrained vision-language, visual representations, multimodal, interacts with the vision-language, LET vision-language models generate, vision-language models already provide
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31237v1>

**原始摘要**

Recent unified multimodal models aim to combine visual understanding and generation within a single model, yet effectively integrating the two capabilities remains challenging. Leveraging pretrained vision-language models offers a way to build on already learned multimodal knowledge, but making this knowledge effectively accessible to visual generation remains nontrivial. We find that a central challenge lies in the heterogeneous visual representations used for understanding and generation. To address this challenge, we introduce Integral, which converts a pretrained vision-language model into a unified multimodal model by constructing a shared visual representation space across the two capabilities. Integral implements this design through two complementary components. Abstraction Fusion constructs shared visual tokens by aggregating hierarchical representations from the frozen vision encoder, while Block-wise MoT introduces a lightweight generative stream that interacts with the vision-language model only at block boundaries. Our approach effectively preserves the pretrained understanding capability while extending it to strong visual generation. Controlled comparisons further demonstrate that sharing visual representations between understanding and generation substantially improves generative learning compared with using a separate visual representation space, despite comparable reconstruction fidelity. These results suggest that representations learned by pretrained vision-language models already provide an effective foundation for visual generation.

### 4. [Flood forecasting using a physics-guided conditional denoising diffusion probabilistic model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31212v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：conditional denoising diffusion probabilistic, flood forecasting, surrogates for regional-scale flood, forecasting with FULL two-dimensional, probabilistic model. high-resolution flood, probabilistic surrogates for regional-scale
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31212v1>

**原始摘要**

High-resolution flood forecasting with full two-dimensional shallow water equation (SWE) solvers remains computationally expensive, even on GPUs. We develop a conditional denoising diffusion probabilistic model (DDPM) that forecasts the evolution of 30 m water-depth fields over a four-day horizon for the Des Plaines River watershed, Illinois. The model is trained on SynxFlow SWE simulations of 113 historical storms observed across twelve Midwestern states (2003-2024), each under three antecedent-wetness scenarios, and can optionally be conditioned on coarse 100 m or 150 m hydrodynamic simulations. Because reverse diffusion is stochastic, every forecast is a 20-member ensemble drawn from the same trained model. Coarse conditioning reduces the four-day mean water-depth RMSE of the ensemble mean from 0.170 m to 0.054 m (100 m) and 0.048 m (150 m). The coarser 150 m conditioning also gives the best critical success index (0.839), false alarm ratio, CRPS and Brier score at lower cost, so finer conditioning does not guarantee better skill. Averaging members reduces error by 5-11% relative to a single realization, and the ensemble provides spatially explicit exceedance probabilities, although the ensembles are under-dispersive (spread-to-error ratios 0.35-0.73). On a single GPU, a four-day forecast over 26 non-overlapping patches is 7.8x, 11.0x and 16.3x faster than the 30 m simulation for the 100 m, 150 m and unconditioned configurations, and projected speedups for a 14-day forecast reach 12-49x for one member and 48-58x for 20 members. Physics-guided diffusion models are thus promising probabilistic surrogates for regional-scale flood forecasting.

### 5. [WorldLatent: A Unified 4D Latent Space for World Perception and Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31209v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：latent space, world perception and generation., multimodal, foundation models, large language models, scalable foundation for general-purpose
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31209v1>

**原始摘要**

Generative Pre-Training (GPT) represents a profound milestone in natural language processing, serving as the foundation for most of today's state-of-the-art large language models. By casting diverse understanding and generation problems into a shared token representation, GPT enables a single architecture and unified optimization loop to master heterogeneous tasks across massive corpora, giving rise to powerful foundation models for natural language. In contrast, 4D spatiotemporal vision lacks a common representational protocol: perception (such as depth and camera estimation) and generation are either handled by separate one-way pipelines or bolted together from unaligned single-modality spaces. In this work, we introduce WorldLatent, a framework that formulates a unified 4D latent space as a common protocol for both world perception and generation. Rather than relying on fragmented, task-specific models or stacked single-modality representations, our framework proposes a multimodal VAE architecture that compresses and fuses complex 4D visual appearance, geometry, and dynamics into a single structured latent representation. Within this formulation, perception and generation operate as complementary denoising processes over the same latent space, enabling one generative model to learn both capabilities simultaneously within a single training loop. Experiments across representative 4D benchmarks verify that our fused latent protocol achieves specialist-level zero-shot 4D perception, coherent 4D generation, and controllable editing from a single model. By demonstrating that diverse 4D tasks can be seamlessly unified under a shared representational protocol, our work provides a scalable foundation for general-purpose 4D spatial intelligence.

### 6. [VITA-WAM: Manifold-Aligned World-Action Modeling for Real-Time Robot Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31195v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：alignment, attention, diffusion, distillation, single ODE STEP vita-wam, modeling for real-time robot
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31195v1>

**原始摘要**

World action model (WAM) is a promising paradigm for visuomotor control by jointly modeling world dynamics and actions. Notably, existing WAMs often rely on large generative models and iterative sampling which incur substantial latency for action generation, limiting real-time deployment. **原始摘要**

Building general-purpose robots is a central goal in robotics. Agentic robotics pursues this goal with planners that control robots through tool calls. However, existing methods either rely on proprietary models accessed through APIs, which incur high costs and latency, or post-train a planner with reinforcement learning (RL) in a single domain, requiring a separate specialist for each domain with incompatible tools. In large language model (LLM) post-training, multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD) has been used to consolidate domain specialists into a single generalist. Yet whether MOPD can build a generalist planner for agentic robotics, how well it performs, and how it compares with other consolidation methods remain unexplored. In this paper, we systematically study these questions: we introduce Robo-MOPD, which trains a single student on closed-loop rollouts in simulation under token-level supervision from matching frozen specialists, and compare it with weight merging, off-policy specialist supervised fine-tuning (SFT), and multi-domain online RL. Experiments on LIBERO-PRO and RoboCasa365 show that a single 9B Robo-MOPD planner reaches 72.9% and 44.8% success (58.9% average), surpassing all consolidated baselines in average success and both 9B specialists on their respective benchmarks. Robo-MOPD excels on long-horizon tasks, outperforming multi-domain online RL by 13.0 percentage points on LIBERO-10.

### 15. [WorldLeague: Benchmarking Physical Agency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30989v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：physical GOAL, benchmark, vision-language, embodied AI, interactive score, THEM physical success RATE
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30989v1>

**原始摘要**

AI systems can now operate Blender, follow instructions in complex video games, and manipulate objects with robot arms. What range of physical goals can today's AI systems achieve autonomously? Answering this question requires testing systems on tasks that demand different combinations of perception, memory, planning, and action. We introduce \textbf{WorldLeague}, a benchmark that compares AI systems on a common set of \textbf{60 simulated robot tasks} by measuring progress toward each task's physical goal from the robot's trajectory. Robots explore scenes to gather evidence in \textbf{WorldSeek}, rearrange objects in \textbf{WorldShape}, and adapt to changes in \textbf{WorldShift}. Tying these goals to a single control interface limits which AI systems can be compared. In WorldLeague, vision-language models (VLMs) choose targets and motions, while vision-language-action models (VLAs) and world-action models (WAMs) use their native controls. For each of six capability dimensions, we measure how much progress a system makes toward its goals (Interactive Score) and how often it achieves them (physical success rate). Together, these measures form the system's \emph{Physical Agency Profile}. Among 13 representative AI systems, Claude Fable 5.2 achieves 13 of the 60 goals and earns the highest mean Interactive Score (28.58/100). Fable leads in four dimensions, while GPT-6.1-Sol and GPT-6-Astra tie for first on rule-following tasks. Even when VLMs answer every final question correctly, they receive an Interactive Score of zero in 71.8\% of those episodes. WorldLeague gives Embodied AI, Physical AI, and Agentic AI a shared basis for comparing what AI systems achieve autonomously, from partial progress toward a physical goal to full goal completion. ### 16. [Self-Evolving Evaluation Environments for Computer-Use Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30962v1)

- 来源：arXiv
- 关键词：agent, benchmark, evaluation environment that co-evolves, evaluation environments for computer-use, evaluation scope through cohort-calibrated, closed-loop evaluation environment
- 链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30962v1>

**原始摘要**

Computer-use agents (CUAs) are rapidly improving, yet their evaluation remains largely static. Existing benchmarks provide a strong foundation, but fixed difficulty ceilings, limited feature coverage, and coarse aggregate scores progressively weaken their discriminative and diagnostic value. We introduce CUA-SEE, a closed-loop evaluation environment that co-evolves tasks and executable verifiers from rollout evidence. It disentangles three objectives: benchmark evolution expands evaluation scope through cohort-calibrated difficulty and coverage growth; diagnostic evolution increases resolution through agent-conditional capability-boundary probes; and verifier evolution improves precision by translating changing requirements into state-grounded checkpoints with adversarial validation. Across multiple agents, applications, and rounds, CUA-SEE generates challenging, measurable tasks while preserving process-level evidence. Our results position adaptive evaluation as a scalable counterpart to self-evolving agents, continuously tracking what agents can do, where their boundaries lie, and how reliably progress is measured.
